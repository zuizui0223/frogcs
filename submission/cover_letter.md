# Cover letter — Journal of Animal Ecology

Dear Editors,

Please consider our Research Article, **“Rainfall-associated frog chorus activation reveals recurrent within-taxon multi-site organization,”** for publication in *Journal of Animal Ecology*.

Rainfall-sensitive calling is familiar in anurans. We ask a different question: **when a short environmental pulse recruits taxa into an active chorus landscape, is the resulting multi-site pattern simply the sum of species-specific responses and historically suitable sites, or does activity retain additional dependence across sites within the same taxa?**

We analysed 4,236 matched wetter–drier comparisons from 15 years of standardized North American Amphibian Monitoring Program surveys across 585 routes and 21 U.S. states. Rain-associated change often involved direct transitions from acoustic silence into overlapping or full chorus. The spatial excess was concentrated in a deep multi-site tail: marginal second- and third-stop participation matched activation nulls, whereas the fourth through tenth occupied stops were overrepresented. NAAMP stops were spaced by about 0.5 miles (0.80 km) or more, so this represents distributed route-scale activation rather than repeated scoring within one local chorus.

Our principal test used 2,916 matched pairs with strictly prior physical-site history. A route-cross-fit comparator allowed taxa to differ in rainfall sensitivity, represented prior species × SiteID use, retained dry-state persistence and matched total wet incidence. It predicted a within-taxon concentration coefficient of **1.353 versus 1.650 observed**; the conditional residual was **0.297**, beyond the simulated 95% range of −0.132 to 0.119 (P = 0.000999). A stronger held-out rain × history gate still underpredicted the observed concentration. Strong wet-state chorusing preferentially reappeared at historically strong physical sites (β = 0.151). Post-hoc structural diagnostics showed that a uniform whole-route species-night switch was too coherent and that, after exact activation depth and current site propensity were fixed, unusually deep activation was preferentially expressed on strictly-prior strong-chorus sites. We present this mechanistic refinement explicitly as exploratory rather than as independent confirmation.

We believe this fits *Journal of Animal Ecology* because the result concerns not another mean rainfall response, but a **pulse-revealed dependence structure in joint species × place activity**. A separate Australian FrogID analysis of 40,754 recordings showed the same recent-rain direction for taxonomic deepening within already-active acoustic samples, while lacking the repeated fixed-site design needed to test the North American spatial mechanism. The wet-state chorus is more spatially concentrated within taxa than first-order species responses, static site propensities and total activation magnitude predict. The resulting structural picture is a fast species-night gate expressed through a distributed slow species × place template. This provides a testable broader hypothesis for behaviourally cryptic communities: short environmental pulses may expose recurrent spatial organization that is weakly expressed during inactive periods.

The integrated hypothesis was developed post-opening within NAAMP, and we state this explicitly. Cross-fitting prevents focal-route leakage in fitted comparator terms but is not independent confirmation. We therefore present the current paper as a large, geographically broad exploratory discovery with explicit internal falsification rather than as a prospectively confirmed result. An external Wisconsin test has been frozen before response access, including its endpoint, comparator and interpretation rule, but **that future confirmation is not part of the evidence claimed in the present manuscript and is not a condition for this submission**.

The study remains observational and does not infer literal synchrony among sequentially surveyed stops, individual movement, demographic occupancy change or a unique lower-level mechanism. The manuscript is prepared for double-anonymized review, is within the journal's word limits, and is accompanied by Supporting Information, reproducible figures and an auditable analysis history.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

The authors
