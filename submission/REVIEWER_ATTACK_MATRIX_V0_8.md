# Reviewer attack matrix v0.8 — RC8 amplification-versus-recruitment framing

| Likely attack | RC8 answer | Status |
|---|---|---|
| “Rain makes frogs call” is textbook natural history. | Agreed. Rainfall–calling is context, not novelty. RC8 asks whether the resulting community expansion is simple amplification of baseline participation or recruitment beyond that expectation. | Addressed |
| The paper still sounds like another rainfall–richness study. | Q1 establishes expansion, but Q2 is the central structural test. The four-component species × stop allocation is compared against a magnitude-matched uniform-activation null. | Addressed by explicit question spine |
| “Amplification” and “recruitment” are just rhetoric. | They correspond to distinct empirical objects. Uniform amplification is the frozen common-log-odds-shift null; recruitment is the excess entry of route-new species × site incidences beyond that null expectation. | Operationalized |
| A high 92% boundary-crossing share is exactly what uniform activation would produce. | Partly correct: the null predicts 80.9% on average. But observed 92.0% exceeds the 74.2–87.4% null interval, and the four-component vector rejects the null at P=.001 under κ=1,2,5. | Strongly addressed |
| The null rejection could be driven by one arbitrary smoothing choice. | No. κ=1,2,5 were frozen before readback and all reject at P=.001; observed boundary crossing lies above every upper 95% null bound. | Strong smoothing robustness |
| Which component actually drives the departure? | Corner expansion and taxonomic deepening are high; spatial spread and within-core rearrangement are low. The departure therefore indicates excess route-new taxonomic participation rather than generic excess of all boundary components. | Addressed |
| “Recruitment” sounds like colonization. | RC8 uses recruitment only for entry into the **behaviourally realized acoustic community**. It explicitly does not infer colonization, occupancy change or demographic recruitment. | Terminology bounded |
| The novelty depends on a null beta-diversity result. | No. Uniform activation is rejected by incidence allocation. Sørensen is a separate bounded result showing practical preservation of local differentiation. | Orthogonal tests |
| A non-significant beta coefficient does not prove no homogenization. | Correct. RC8 uses a fixed post hoc ±0.025 practical-equivalence margin. Primary and exact-year 90% CIs both lie inside it. | Stronger bounded result |
| Alpha and gamma increasing makes beta stability partly mathematical. | RC8 reports pairwise Sørensen, turnover, nestedness, normalized Whittaker beta and incidence allocation. The main structural departure comes from the four-component matrix test, not from diversity arithmetic. | Addressed |
| The matrix decomposition is tautological. | The four components sum algebraically, but their coefficient allocation is not fixed. The empirical result is the deviation of that allocation from an explicit null distribution. | Addressed |
| Wet surveys may simply be easier acoustically. | Hearing/noise/wind adjustment, traffic sensitivity and Massachusetts noise-index sensitivity retain the footprint/alpha/gamma signal. Unmeasured detectability remains acknowledged. | Addressed with limitation |
| Rain recency is protocol-conditioned. | Correct. RC8 treats rainfall timing as observational. A frozen sensitivity requiring the drier survey to be ≥4 days after rain retains CI-positive footprint/alpha/gamma effects. | Addressed |
| Geographic heterogeneity undermines the general claim. | The claim is not state-level uniformity. All 21 leave-one-state-out refits retain CI-positive footprint/alpha/gamma effects, while state-specific slopes remain heterogeneous. | Addressed |

| “Metacommunity” overstates connectivity. | The manuscript defines an operational active-community/metacommunity-scale sampling unit and explicitly disclaims demographic connectivity. | Addressed |

Excess route-new participation remains relative to that stochastic baseline. Species-specific detectability is still acknowledged as a limitation. | Addressed but not eliminated |

## Strongest current claim

> **Recent-rain conditions expand the behaviourally realized frog community through more new taxonomic participation and less core rearrangement than a magnitude-matched uniform activation process predicts, while pairwise Sørensen differentiation remains practically stable within a fixed ±0.025 slope margin.**

## General principle

> **Environmental pulses can recruit previously inactive spatial and taxonomic participation beyond uniform amplification without erasing local differentiation.**

## What would change the paper materially

Only three classes of evidence should reopen the frozen story:
1. an external/independent monitoring system estimating the same amplification-versus-recruitment contrast;
2. a genuinely proximal, independently sourced response trait with adequate coverage and a frozen prediction;
3. a documented defect in the current null/equivalence implementation.

Everything else belongs in Supporting Information or future work.
