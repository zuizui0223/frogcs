## Core ecological story

1. Within the same route and seasonal sampling window, wetter runs contain more acoustically active species.
2. Richness gain increases with rainfall contrast and persists in exact consecutive-year comparisons.
3. The wet assemblage is not a simple nested extension of the dry assemblage; the consecutive-year sensitivity shows increased turnover with rainfall contrast.
4. Species wet-versus-dry responses are strongly heterogeneous after pair-level environmental and temporal adjustment.
5. More than half of the family-matched heterogeneity remains within families; Hylidae and Ranidae are each internally heterogeneous.
6. Therefore rainfall-associated activity reflects **species-selective short-term reassembly of the active community**, not uniform activation of an unchanged assemblage.
