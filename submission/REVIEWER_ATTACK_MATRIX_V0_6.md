## Strongest current claim

> **Recent-rain surveys expand the behaviourally realized frog community along both spatial and taxonomic axes: more stops become active, local and route richness rise, about 92% of added incidence growth crosses a spatial and/or taxonomic boundary, and measured beta diversity does not detectably shift while matrix fill remains within a frozen practical-equivalence margin. The pooled footprint, alpha and gamma pattern survives every single-state omission and remains CI-positive when the drier survey lies beyond the programme's 0–3 day rain-target window.**

## What would change the paper materially

Only three classes of new evidence justify changing the main story:
1. a prespecified test showing material change/equivalence in an explicit matrix-structure quantity;
2. a genuinely proximal, independently sourced response trait with adequate coverage and frozen prediction;
3. an external/independent monitoring system estimating the same multiscale quantities.

Everything else belongs in Supporting Information or future work.
