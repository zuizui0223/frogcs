## Core ecological story

1. The response is not confined to the day of rain.
2. Within fixed route × seasonal-window strata, active-community richness is elevated relative to >=8-day runs at:
   - day 0: +0.654 species;
   - day 1: +0.544;
   - days 2–3: +0.411;
   - days 4–7: +0.381, 95% CI 0.202–0.561.
3. Matched wetter–drier comparisons independently show richness gain increasing with rainfall contrast.
4. The richer wet assemblage is not simply nested addition; exact-consecutive-year turnover increases with rainfall contrast.
5. Species responses are strongly heterogeneous after adjustment and remain heterogeneous within major families.
6. Therefore recent rainfall predicts a **multi-day expansion plus species-selective reassembly of the active community**, not only an immediate chorus response.
