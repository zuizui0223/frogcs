## New RC7 geographic result

The pre-readback leave-one-state-out gate achieved **strong PASS**.

Across all 21 single-state omissions:
- active-stop slope range: **0.346–0.433**;
- local-alpha slope range: **0.0496–0.0955**;
- route-gamma slope range: **0.218–0.329**;
- every 95% confidence interval remained above zero for all three responses.

Therefore **no single sampled state drives the headline multiscale expansion signal**.

This does **not** mean state responses are uniform:
- estimable state-specific slopes were positive in 16/19 states for active stops;
- 13/19 for local alpha;
- 13/19 for route gamma;
- Vermont had a wholly negative state-specific interval for active stops;
- Massachusetts had a wholly negative state-specific interval for local alpha;
- no state had a wholly negative interval for route gamma;
- Florida and Texas did not support the frozen route-clustered state-specific specification.

Authorized wording: **geographically robust to omission of any single sampled state, with local heterogeneity**.

Forbidden wording: **positive in every state**, **uniform across states**, **continental universality**.

## Mechanistic interpretation

RC7 adds no new tested species-level mechanism. Discussion now connects the observed pattern to independent anuran literature showing species-specific meteorological responses, rainfall-dependent opportunistic breeding, rain-triggered chorusing thresholds/lags and inundation-associated richness/chorusing responses.

Authorized interpretation: heterogeneous species/site activation thresholds are a **plausible hypothesis** that could generate spatial + taxonomic expansion without homogenization.

Not authorized: a causal pathway identified by these data.
