## Verified gaps, not guesses

1. **No repository root `LICENSE` or `CITATION.cff`**, and no root `requirements.txt` or `pyproject.toml`, at the inspected 2026-11-09 head. GitHub's public source does **not** automatically waive rights. Select project-owned code/data license and record a pinned execution environment, without granting third-party rights that the authors do not hold.
2. **No project archive DOI, reviewer access link, or complete permanent numerical artifact package.** DOI registration must not be represented as complete; CI passes validate claims and source presence, not an external data editor's acceptance.
3. **Original third-party materials require per-source rights decisions**, especially WorldClim and EarthEnv rasters, GBIF publisher records and sensitive sites. Copying all of `research/results/` blindly is not an acceptable deposit strategy.
4. **R5 Actions artifacts may expire.** Archive their exact complete original 4,800 outcomes, including the original incorrect arm-label subgroup aggregate and the *separate reporting-only repaired aggregate*, with documentation distinguishing labels from unchanged individual estimates.
5. **Coauthor approval, legitimate author-order/contribution/funding/COI data and journal figure proofs remain unknown.** Their absence is not repaired by source data or more simulations.
