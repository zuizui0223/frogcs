# Novelty audit v0.10 — RC10 allocation-centred story

## Central discovery

The paper no longer treats “without homogenization” as the headline. Pairwise Sørensen is secondary because:
- the ±0.025 equivalence margin was selected after the conventional coefficient was known;
- the observed Sørensen slope lies inside the uniform-activation null distribution.

The title-level discovery is instead:

> **Rainfall-associated expansion is more boundary-biased than uniform activation predicts.**

## Why this is not mechanical

Observed boundary crossing = **92.0%**.

Original magnitude-matched null:
- mean = **80.9%**;
- 95% interval = **74.2–87.4%**;
- omnibus P = **.000999** under κ = 1, 2 and 5.

Persistence-preserving stress test:
- removes hierarchical species-level shrinkage;
- anchors pair-specific dry cell state at a = 0.50, 0.75 and 0.90;
- still applies one common log-odds activation shift and matches wet incidence magnitude.

Primary a = 0.75:
- boundary null mean = **77.9%**;
- 95% interval = **73.4–82.8%**;
- omnibus P = **.000999**;
- within-core = **22.1% expected vs 8.0% observed**.

At a = 0.90:
- boundary null mean = **77.6%**;
- upper 95% bound = **82.3%**;
- omnibus P = **.000999**.

Thus the main pattern is not an artefact of hierarchical shrinkage weakening stable species × stop identity.

## Direction of the departure

Under the persistence-preserving primary null:
- corner expansion: **36.9% observed vs 28.6% expected**;
- spatial spread: **15.2% vs 19.3%**;
- taxonomic deepening: **39.9% vs 30.1%**;
- within-core rearrangement: **8.0% vs 22.1%**.

The pulse therefore disproportionately recruits route-new taxonomic participation and underproduces core-only rearrangement relative to uniform activation.

## FrogID position

FrogID is **not** described as independent validation.

An earlier FrogID analysis already showed a rain-associated ≥2-species signal. RC10 therefore uses the continuous-depth analysis as an **estimand-aligned cross-dataset consistency check**.

North America:
- active-stop richness β = +0.0794, 95% CI +0.0231 to +0.1357.

Australia:
- 40,754 FrogID recordings;
- excess-richness β per 1 SD dryness = -0.0818, 95% CI -0.0982 to -0.0654;
- recorder and within-cell sensitivities agree.

The claim is only that the **direction of active-unit taxonomic deepening is consistent across the two datasets**. No pooled effect size, independent replication or worldwide universality is claimed.

## Ecological interpretation

A common activity shift does not reproduce where new incidences enter, even when dry cell identity is strongly preserved. This supports a biological hypothesis that recent wet conditions preferentially activate species × site combinations that are rare under dry conditions.

Temporary or intermittently suitable breeding sites are one plausible source of this pattern. NAAMP lacks the hydrological measurements needed to identify that mechanism, so it remains a Discussion hypothesis.

## Preferred one-sentence novelty statement

> **Recent-rain frog-community expansion crosses spatial and taxonomic boundaries more strongly than magnitude-matched uniform activation predicts, and this departure survives a null that strongly preserves pair-specific dry species × site identity.**
