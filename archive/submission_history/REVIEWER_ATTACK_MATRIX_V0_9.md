## Strongest current claim

> **Rainfall-associated taxonomic deepening within already-active acoustic units is directionally consistent across independent North American and Australian monitoring systems; within fixed North American routes, additional participation is also more boundary-biased than uniform activation predicts, without practical Sørensen homogenization.**

## General principle

> **Environmental pulses can recruit additional taxonomic participation across independent systems, while the spatial geometry of that recruitment depends on what the monitoring design can resolve.**
