# Novelty audit v0.5 — RC6 metacommunity expansion

## Reviewer-safe central advance

Rainfall effects on anuran calling are established and are **not** the novelty claim.

The current manuscript asks a narrower community-ecological question than prior rainfall/calling, inundation/richness and short-term acoustic-composition studies:

> **When a short environmental pulse increases observed animal diversity, where does that increase enter the spatial community matrix?**

In 4,236 matched NAAMP survey pairs, recent-rain conditions are associated with:
- a larger active spatial footprint;
- higher local alpha richness among active stops;
- higher route gamma richness;
- no detectable shift in the measured among-active-stop beta-diversity metrics;
- species × stop incidence growth dominated by opening spatial and/or taxonomic matrix boundaries rather than rearranging an unchanged active core.

The manuscript therefore contributes a **multiscale localization of a behavioural community response**, not another demonstration that frogs call after rain.

## What is already established

1. Rainfall influences frog calling probability, chorus activity and observed richness.
2. Alpha, beta and gamma diversity are mathematically related and must be interpreted jointly.
3. Species × site incidence matrices provide a standard representation of spatial community structure.
4. Functional diversity and response diversity are conceptually distinct.
5. Response diversity does not necessarily stabilize communities under pulse disturbances.

These established ideas set the comparison class; none is claimed as newly invented here.

## Nearest published comparison class

The novelty claim should be evaluated against work that already studies short-term anuran community activity rather than against single-species rainfall studies.

Two especially close precedents are:

- **Sugai et al. (2021, Journal of Animal Ecology; DOI 10.1111/1365-2656.13399)** quantified short-term changes in calling-assemblage composition and temporal beta diversity across night periods in tropical anurans. This establishes that short-timescale acoustic community composition and beta diversity are not themselves novel objects.
- **Sarker et al. (2022, Ecological Indicators; DOI 10.1016/j.ecolind.2022.109640)** showed that wetland inundation can increase frog richness and abundance and alter community composition, and also compared chorusing before and after flow arrival. This establishes that wetting-driven richness and community-composition responses are not themselves novel.

RC6 therefore does **not** claim novelty for:
- rainfall/wetting effects on calling;
- increases in frog richness after wetting;
- short-term changes in calling-assemblage composition;
- use of beta diversity in anuran community ecology.

The narrower contribution is the **joint localization of one short-term rainfall-associated response within the same standardized spatial sampling unit**: active spatial footprint, conditional local alpha, route gamma, among-active-site beta, proportional species × site matrix fill, and the exact incidence pathways by which new detections cross spatial and/or taxonomic matrix boundaries.

The literature audit did not identify a directly comparable anuran study that combines those quantities into the same matched short-term matrix decomposition. This should be phrased as a scoped literature-positioning statement, not as a universal priority claim.

## What RC6 adds

### 1. A pulse is localized simultaneously in space and taxonomy

The active community expands along two axes:
- **spatial dilation:** more route stops contain callers;
- **taxonomic deepening:** more species participate within both newly active and already-active stops.

The exact four-way species × stop decomposition shows that only about 8% of the rainfall-associated incidence slope is pure rearrangement of existing route species among already-active stops.

### 2. Alpha and gamma increase without a detectable beta-diversity shift

The paper does not infer this from richness arithmetic alone. It measures:
- pairwise Sørensen dissimilarity;
- Simpson turnover;
- nestedness-resultant dissimilarity;
- normalized Whittaker beta diversity.

All are close to zero with respect to rainfall contrast while active footprint, alpha and gamma increase.

This pattern motivates the term **two-dimensional active-community expansion without detectable spatial homogenization**, rather than “reassembly”.

### 3. Local deepening is not just a single-to-multispecies threshold effect

The active-stop alpha increase is exactly decomposed:
- ~29% from crossing 1 species to >=2;
- ~71% from additional multiplicity beyond the second species.

Richness also rises within the exact same StopNumbers that are active in both paired surveys.

### 4. The main pattern survives recorded acoustic-quality adjustment

The footprint, alpha and gamma coefficients remain positive after adjustment for:
- recorded hearing impairment;
- major-noise timeouts;
- wind;
- traffic counts in a large subset;
- Massachusetts MassNoiseIndex in its available subset.

This constrains a simple “wet surveys were easier to hear” explanation while explicitly not eliminating all detectability or masking.

### 5. Taxonomic, functional and response dimensions do not collapse onto one axis

Within a frozen four-axis AmphiBIO life-history space:
- trait-covered richness increases;
- functional MPD shows no supported shift;
- four community-weighted means show no supported shift.

Among 24 species, functional distance does not predict rainfall-response dissimilarity under 100,000 response-label permutations, including a within-family sensitivity.

Thus conventional life-history functional similarity is not a proxy for rainfall-response similarity in this species set.

### 6. A promising species-level response trait was falsified rather than rescued

Activation geometry initially appeared unusually strong because species rankings repeated across non-overlapping years (rho = .774) and completely disjoint route sets (rho = .785).

A separately frozen placebo gate then tested whether that ranking was actually rainfall-specific. It was not:

- reverse-direction dry gains: rho = **.929** with wet geometry;
- low-rain-contrast gains: rho = **.953**;
- opportunity-corrected baseline solitude tendency: rho = **.782**;
- raw singleton-calling fraction: rho = **.876**.

The gate therefore required geometry to be removed from the title and main novelty claim.

This falsification is important to the evidence hierarchy: RC6 does not convert repeatability into mechanism when a stable rain-independent species property explains the same ranking.

### 7. A tempting response-diversity mechanism was tested and retained as a negative result

A non-overlapping temporal holdout asked whether early-period wet/dry response-sign diversity buffers later route-richness sensitivity. It did not.

This prevents the paper from turning species heterogeneity into an unsupported insurance-effect story.

## General ecological contribution

The broad contribution is:

> **Short environmental pulses can change the dimensions of a behaviourally realized community without requiring either demographic turnover or a detectable collapse of spatial compositional differentiation. Taxonomic expansion, functional structure and environmental response diversity are distinct properties and can respond differently on the same short timescale.**

This is a community-ecology result with frogs as the system, rather than a frog natural-history result with community terminology added afterward.

## Why the incidence-matrix framing matters

A richness-only analysis cannot distinguish:
- new active sites containing existing species;
- new species appearing in already-active sites;
- species and sites entering simultaneously;
- reshuffling within a fixed active core.

RC6 separates these processes exactly.

The matrix framing also prevents “route richness increased” from being overinterpreted as reassembly, colonization or occupancy change.

The exact summation itself is not the novelty: by construction, the four incidence components equal total species × stop incidence change. The empirical content is **where the rainfall-associated slope falls**. Nothing in the identity requires ~92% of that slope to cross at least one matrix boundary or only ~8% to remain within the pre-existing active core.

## Contemporary response-diversity relevance

Recent response-diversity work emphasizes that:
- response traits need not be represented by conventional effect/functional traits;
- species responses can be context dependent rather than fixed;
- response diversity is not universally stabilizing under pulse disturbances.

RC6 retains species-response heterogeneity and conventional-trait nulls as Supporting Information, but does not claim a validated response trait. The most promising candidate, activation geometry, was highly repeatable yet failed its frozen rain-specificity placebo gate.

## Remaining novelty risks

1. **The raw driver is familiar.** Editors must see the matrix-scale community question before seeing “rainfall makes frogs call”.
2. **“Metacommunity” can sound too demographic.** The manuscript must repeatedly qualify this as behaviourally realized / metacommunity-scale spatial organization.
3. **Most structural analyses are post-opening.** Transparency is essential; their value is convergence across frozen decompositions, not confirmatory status.
4. **Null beta results are not proof of exact invariance.** Use “no detectable shift” unless a separately frozen equivalence test supports a prespecified practical margin.
5. **The remaining main result is structural rather than a species-level mechanism.** Editors may ask whether matrix decomposition is descriptive; RC6 should answer with the matched multiscale pattern, practical-equivalence test, same-stop deepening and detection-condition robustness, not by reviving a failed trait story.

## Phrases to avoid

- rain makes frog metacommunities richer for the first time
- rainfall causes community expansion
- rainfall causes colonization of new sites
- beta diversity is unchanged / preserved exactly
- recent rain homogenizes or prevents homogenization of occupancy communities
- functional traits are irrelevant
- wet-only species are functionally redundant
- response diversity stabilizes the community
- activation geometry is a rainfall-specific response trait
- temporal or route repeatability rescues activation geometry after the placebo failure
- the active route is a proven dispersal-connected metacommunity
- week-long reassembly
- preregistered metacommunity hypotheses

## Preferred one-sentence novelty statement

> **We resolve a familiar rainfall–calling association into the geometry of a spatial community response: recent-rain surveys add both active sites and active species, most additional incidences cross spatial and/or taxonomic matrix boundaries, and measured beta diversity does not detectably collapse as the active community expands.**
