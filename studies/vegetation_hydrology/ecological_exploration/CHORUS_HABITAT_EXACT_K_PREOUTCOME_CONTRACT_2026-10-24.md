# NEW exploratory analysis contract: Is recurrent frog chorus placement environmentally selective?
**Frozen before any NEW frog-outcome readback for this study — 2026-10-24.**

**Authority / isolation.** This is a new ecological question on a new `research/chorus-habitat-conditional-exploration-2026-10` branch. It inherits environment-only pipelines from `research/vegetation-hydrology-feasibility-2026-10`. **Do not edit or reinterpret** `main`, JAE RC6, its frozen endpoints, or published conclusions. All analyses involving previously studied NAAMP frog records are necessarily **new, post-hoc exploratory secondary analyses**, *not* independent confirmation, regardless of route/year blocking.

## Biological question and possible outcomes
For a taxon that was not recorded calling at any of the ten drier-survey stops, does the spatial placement of its strong CI2/3 chorus at the wetter revisit disproportionately match:
(1) durable habitat mosaics (500m woody+emergent wetland vegetation and NLCD open-water fraction);
(2) temporally prior satellite-observed 500m water, **when visible**;
(3) neither, such that unexplained route-night dependence remains?

The evidence is acoustic, not demographic. Negative frog calls are nondetections, not local absence. Route stops were observed sequentially, not simultaneously; no movement or individual fidelity is inferable. Static NLCD values are annual retrospective habitat context, not true time-prior causal exposures. JRC class 0 is not observed dry land.

### Frogs, route surveys and matching
1. Reuse frozen parent NAAMP analysis eligibility: unified-protocol years 2001–2015; nonmissing survey date, State/RouteNumber/RunNumber/RunID; valid DaysSinceRain in [0,180]; 10 non-skipped physical stops; >=8 stop temperatures converted to Celsius; mean temperature −10 to 45°C; route RunNumber in 1–4; adjacent *observed* years within State × RouteNumber × RunNumber, with unequal DaysSinceRain. Only matched-pair physical SiteIDs that are stable/verified at all ten stop positions may enter primary placement endpoint; explicitly report retained route/pair counts and any SiteID turnover.
2. The wetter survey is the member with **fewer DaysSinceRain**, not necessarily larger recent rain amount. No rainfall causality is asserted. `Counts.csv` contains **positive CallingIndex 1–3 records**, so CI0 is an eligible but **not detected** taxon×stop, not an explicit original zero record.
3. The focal taxon is acoustically silent at **all ten eligible drier-run stops** and has **k=2..9** wetter-run stops at CI2/3, to define nontrivial strong-chorus placement. Count each taxon×matched-run-pair once. Complex operational taxon tokens require the original study's frozen identity filtering; report species/token scope and no arbitrary post-result taxon removal. A k=1 event carries no multi-site concentration; k=10 has no placement degrees of freedom. Other families (CI1 or mixed CI) are secondary descriptive only.
4. For each focal group, the binary outcome is whether each of its original ten physical sites has CI2/3 in the wetter survey. Condition the outcome **exactly on its realised k** (no marginal rainfall-effect or total activation inference). The per-group normalizer enumerates all `choose(10,k)` possible strong-site placements, preserving k and fixed 10-stop opportunities. The model must not treat the ten station rows as independent Bernoulli replicates.

### Frozen primary comparisons and validation
- **M0 historical-site baseline:** route-cross-validated taxon-specific previous strong-chorus site propensity, computed solely from earlier calendar survey years relative to the entire matched pair, with a frozen Jeffreys/Laplace-like smoothing formula fixed in code before readback; no current or future site calls permitted as predictor. No opportunistic parameter fitting on held-out routes.
- **M1 habitat candidate:** M0 plus *two* predeclared site covariates: 500m woody+emergent wetland and 500m NLCD open water. Before fitting, subtract the group-specific ten-site mean so slopes reflect **within-route site placement**, not cross-state forest composition. No new class/site radius/threshold/interaction selection based on test results.
- Model parameters estimated on route-disjoint training folds, with each physical route assigned by deterministic `int(SHA256(State + ':' + RouteNumber)[:8],16)%5` to one of five folds. Every repeated year/route and its physical SiteIDs remain in one fold. Freeze regularization strength and optimization checks in code before first outcome execution (no tuning to maximize P).
- **Primary answer:** does M1 improve held-out route log score of the *exact-k placement likelihood* relative to M0? Define improvement as `total held-out log-likelihood difference / number of eligible taxon×pair groups`; report its distribution with physical-route cluster bootstrap 95% interval. Report pooled habitat coefficients and uncertainty as exploratory, with a same-sample M0/M1 comparison; do not infer slopes from significance alone. A non-positive/outside-precision improvement is a valid outcome.
- **M2 water secondary:** only for groups with all ten JRC sites valid at the **>=50% observed-pixel** gate (and a separate >=80% gate). Compare M0, M1, and M2 (M1 + strictly preceding JRC water) on the **identical M2-complete data**. If geographic/seasonal representation or within-group water gradient is insufficient, report *non-estimable*; do not treat missing imagery as a negative water effect. Also report which state×season×habitat strata were lost.
- **M3 residual dependent configuration** is a separate future family; it is NOT licensed by an M1/M2 effect and must receive a new test contract if pursued.

### Uncertainty, negative interpretations and stopping
- Route cluster resampling (not stop-level IID), fixed random seed 2840223 for bootstrap/simulations; two-sided comparisons and full interval, no species or region hunting.
- Pre-declare logistic/exact-k likelihood numerical diagnostics, sample-size gates, source and coordinate attrition before opening `Counts.csv`: minimum 200 focal taxon×pair placement groups from >=50 physical routes for M1; M2 minimum 100 groups, >=30 routes and ≥10% route-run water contrast with >=5pp spread, otherwise non-estimable. The study is *exploratory* regardless of these gates because the frog study programme has already inspected NAAMP outcomes.
- Interpret categories as **mapped habitat context**, not substrate identity or annual site fidelity. Sound propagation, road proximity, observer effects, taxon availability, local demography and previous acoustic opportunity may confound association. Satellite water largely sees exposed water and cannot exclude transient shaded/vegetated wetland hydrology.
- Publish all null/positive results and source/parameter versions; do not revise choices after readback and call the same study confirmatory. If sources are inaccessible, stop as **data access blocked**, not estimated zero.

**Environment-only basis for this prereadback contract:** Exact 185,221 station-visit joins; 14,638 source calendar runs with ≥8 sites/≥6 usable JRC sites and 40.4% ≥5pp within-run JRC water gradient; route-balanced static wetland vegetation contrast in 73.7% of 1,140 routes; JRC same-site same-season interannual >5pp only 6.2% overall and 1.6% in vegetation-wetlands. These are feasibility checks, *not frog outcome fit results*.
