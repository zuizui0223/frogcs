# USGS ARMI 2024 v1.1 — original public frog survey opportunity and inter-observer ecological feasibility gate

**Source-first contract, 2026-10-14 JST (before extracting call outcomes from the 2024 CSV).** Independent draft PR #135 only; JAE RC6/main frozen. The goal is not another arbitrary subgroup search in Hoefer's detection-only data. Original USGS [2024 night call data DOI:10.5066/P19ZCGWM](https://doi.org/10.5066/P19ZCGWM) are CC0/public, revised April 2026, and independently collected from the older NAAMP/JAE and the separate Australian studies. This is not a CEWH wetland hydrology panel.

## What the ACTUAL original source preflight established, without species outcomes

The [official ScienceBase item](https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/68016413d4be0263cab109a8), item ID `68016413d4be0263cab109a8`, returned a source-listed exact **235,391-byte** CSV named `ARMI_2024_cricket_frog_data_release_2025_v1.1.csv` (source metadata and original FGDC XML also public). [Actual source-only CI 38421276910](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/38421276910) retrieved the original unmodified CSV, SHA256 `dafd388c7ccb79e08dbadd37a25c604473a8caf4ff5c8318532740acb56a04e4`, and found **1,934 rows**, **15 columns**, **343 distinct public source `Site` IDs**, **51 date labels in 2024**, **two observer IDs**, **two method categories**. Original columns: `Date`, `Arrival_time`, `Project`, `Site`, `Site_original`, `Latitude_WGS84`, `Longitude_WGS84`, `Observer`, `Method`, `Wind_Beaufort`, `Cloud_cover_percent`, `Species`, `Number_heard`, `Compass_direction`, `Comments`. No individual source site, coordinate, observer ID, event or taxon label was saved; the raw file is not in the repo.

**Critical source distinction:** 1,934 lines are **not** 1,934 independent listening visits. The `Species` field is nonblank in all 1,934 lines; `Number_heard` in 1,714. Neither fact establishes absent species or completed all-frog surveys. Official metadata/field definitions and repeated source keys must control zero interpretation, not assumed frog natural history.

## Frozen first ecological question and decisive source gate

**Biological comparison:** can observed frog-community turnover across genuinely repeated *same physical site* night visits be distinguished from variability between independent human observers during the *same site×date listening event*? If the original source contains full recorded independent zero-call visits, non-detection and observer/site/date opportunities might permit properly observation-aware analysis. If it exposes only positive species rows, descriptive *detected-species-list* similarity is the maximum claim.

Before reading taxon responses, independently assess ONLY the original survey-design dimensions:

1. Original FGDC XML definitions of `Site`, `Site_original`, `Date`, `Arrival_time`, `Observer`, `Method`, `Species` and `Number_heard`; any explicit record-of-no-frog or `0` code vs blank/missing. Do not reinterpret missing `Number_heard` as absent species or a completed visit without dictionary backing.
2. Non-sensitive counts of unique `Site×Date×Observer` keys and `Site×Date` keys, source dates per site, site-days with 2 independent observers, distinct `Arrival_time`/method values within site-date-observer, counts of rows per key. **No linked raw site/observer/coordinate values may be printed or committed.** Check if `Method` changes by observer and whether repeat visits differ in months/call time; source methods may not be directly comparable.
3. Only if enough **paired, same-site-date, comparable-time and same-method** independent observers AND enough within-site distinct-date repeated full survey opportunities exist can a within-event observer agreement vs between-date temporal-community difference be identified. Fix exact timing tolerances, pair rules, site unit, reference contrast and holdout plan using original field metadata BEFORE viewing any species response list.
4. If the required paired observer/time/method and complete source-negative opportunities do **not** exist, STOP the ecological comparison and preserve a useful falsification of source adequacy; do not set unrecorded cells to zero, invent observers' independence or compare unmatched nights for a positive result.
5. Exclude precise coordinates and full observer/site/species names from any public frogcs receipt. Author official USGS sites may be publicly listed under CC0, but this project is deliberately publishing only anonymized source grain/aggregate counts, not a new location-linked animal data export.
6. Other long-term multi-species modelling releases are not automatically independent validation: [USGS 2026 Blanchard cricket frog paper](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/blanchards-cricket-frogs-acris-blanchardi-have-re-expanded-northward-mid-2000s) already reports >300 historical 2003–08 sites revisited in 2024 and species occupancy results. **Do not claim their already-published northward recolonization as a new discovery or assume historical keys agree without original crosswalk.** Separately, [Doser et al. (2026), JAE public model repository](https://github.com/doserjef/Doser_et_al_2026_JAE/tree/v1.0.0) explicitly says original Minnesota frog survey observations are proprietary/unreleased and should **not** be mined or silently substituted by public model outputs.

The 2024 source has environmental observation columns (wind/sky) but not same-site measured inundation history. Thus hydrological rising vs receding/memory Q-A/Q-B are **not** identifiable here irrespective of survey quality.
