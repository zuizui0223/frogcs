**To:** cewomonitoring@dcceew.gov.au  
**Subject:** Flow-MER 2014–22: wetland–point and original acoustic/larval survey metadata (no fauna records requested)

Dear CEWH Monitoring Data Team,

I am assessing whether the public Flow-MER 2014–2022 frog data can support a subsequent independent analysis of frog calling, physical wetland conditions and later larval records. At this stage I request **data dictionaries and non-identifying metadata only**, not fauna observations, precise site coordinates, private water observations, or original sound recordings.

The 2019 Murrumbidgee Monitoring, Evaluation and Research Plan distinguishes repeated short call surveys from 2 × 20-minute adult visual transects and separately sampled tadpoles. It also lists **3 × 2-minute** audio bouts in its method tables but **6 × 2-minute** audio bouts in the Methods text; I am not assuming either was the realized protocol. The plan describes core and alternate wetlands, and the 2019–20 technical report includes tadpoles identifiable only to *Limnodynastes* genus. The public frog file presents aggregated species-labelled rows without original bout-level or net-effort keys.

Could you please clarify four source-provenance items, or refer me to the relevant field-data custodian?

1. **Physical wetland unit:** Is there a versioned mapping from the public frog `SamplePoint`/official MDMS `NAME` or `SAMO_ID` to a **stable physical wetland unit**, including replacement/alternate site years? An aggregate number of distinct wetlands represented by the 28 public Murrumbidgee point labels would already be helpful.
2. **Actual calling/visit method:** Which acoustic bout count and protocol version were **actually used by water year**, and is there an original completed visit/bout/effort manifest that distinguishes `not heard in a completed species opportunity` from `not surveyed` or `survey aborted`? The question is about documentation, not a request for actual species call outcomes.
3. **Larval field identification:** What field identification ranks and gear/effort variables underlie published `speciesCode × CPUETadpoles`? Specifically, how were *Limnodynastes* genus-identified tadpoles represented in species-labelled public records, and are original netting dates/effort available for legitimate alignment?
4. **Potential recorder metadata (optional):** The 2019 plan mentions acoustic recorders deployed at wetlands in 2016. Does any original recorder **inventory**, station–wetland crosswalk, deployment dates and detection/quality protocol survive (no recordings requested)?

If publicly accessible versioned dictionaries already answer these questions, a document link or yes/no answer would be sufficient. I understand that no protected data may be releasable.

Thank you for supporting long-term wetland monitoring and source-transparent ecological research.

Sincerely,  
[Author to fill name/affiliation upon deciding whether to send]
