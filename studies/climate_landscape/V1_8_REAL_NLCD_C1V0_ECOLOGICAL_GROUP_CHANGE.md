# v1.8 — Real paired-pixel Annual NLCD Collection 1.0 land-change screening

**2026-10-08. Independent frog climate–landscape project. No RC6 changes.**

## What was actually measured

The successful response-blind official ArcGIS ImageServer source run [37754405051](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/37754405051) returned original **Annual NLCD Collection 1.0 (C1V0)** land-cover-category TIFFs for 2004, 2009 and 2014. [Source artifact 11539925980](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/37754405051/artifacts/11539925980) contains three files and the original 20-buffer receipt. The three TIFF SHA256 values are frozen in `scripts/audit_c1v0_ecological_class_change_v18.py` and are checked before extraction.

- All years: one categorical band, EPSG:5070, aligned 30-m cells, exactly **409 × 170 pixels** covering the original nominal Iowa 360417 ten-stop AOI; the same class legend and collection version.
- Independently re-extracted 10 mapped stops × radii **250 m and 1 km**; 2004–2009, 2009–2014, and 2004–2014 changes. All 20 buffers had complete paired pixel coverage.
- All results describe **mapped land-cover classes around nominal stations**. The published 2021 DNR map agrees with archived USGS coordinates at 10/10 stops to within 6m, but historic **2001–2015 field-site continuity remains unverified**. No NAAMP Counts.csv or frog acoustic outcome was used in this v1.8 screening.

## Measured land-cover changes, 2004–2014

| Equal-weighted site mean (10 stops) | 250m | 1km |
|---|---:|---:|
| Change between any of the original **16 class codes** | 4.697% | 5.506% |
| Change between broader **functional land-cover groups** | **2.060%** | **1.730%** |
| Stops with ≥1 forest-loss mapped pixel | 3/10 | 9/10 |
| Stops with ≥1 forest→developed mapped pixel | 1/10 | 2/10 |
| Stops with ≥1 forest→agriculture mapped pixel | 3/10 | 9/10 |

The functional grouping uses open water, snow/ice, developed, barren, forest, shrub, grass, agriculture, and wetlands. Original class codes are retained in a second measure. This does **not** mean pasture/hay (81) and cultivated crop (82) are biologically equivalent; it means their mutual switch should not be interpreted as forest loss or newly impervious land. Likewise, forested wetland (90) and emergent wetland (95) are distinguished in a retained within-group metric because their ecological functions may differ for anurans.

A striking example: at **stop 2, 250m**, 38 of 221 cells (17.195%) changed 16-class code; **36** were 81↔82, **2** were within developed classes, and **0** crossed a functional group. This is a strong measurement-warning example, **not** a biological habitat-reallocation result. At **stop 10, 250m**, mapped forest fraction dropped **12.73% → 5.45%**, and 12 cells switched forest→agriculture; the area supports a source-only environmental case study, **not** a causal frog response claim.

### Coverage and inferential limits

1. Collection 1.0 is **not Collection 1.2**. A valid C1V2/1.2 class raster and Land Cover Confidence / Change products remain the next source-validation requirement. Differences between classification versions can overturn small pixel changes. Never merge different versions in one pixel transition series.
2. The ArcGIS service's exported 30m pixel grid is aligned across the three requested years. It is a service-rendered categorical crop; without independent original-tile metadata and confidence values, do **not** claim verified in-situ disturbance or long-term hydroperiod change.
3. Fixed 250m and 1km buffers were selected before frog outcome association. Neighboring 1km buffers overlap and cannot be treated as independent samples; year-to-year repeated pixels are also dependent.
4. **Only one 250m site** has a forest→developed transition; the single-route sample cannot identify whether terrestrial conversion drives acoustic allocation. The independent 9-route five-year climate prediction was negative and remains frozen. Do not optimize class definitions/thresholds, select favorable frog taxa, or read frog outcomes to rescue a result.
5. Inferences about ecological tracking vs historical-site acoustic legacy require independent historic station relocation evidence plus pre/post surveys at the same physical locations. The DNR map postdates the NAAMP survey years.

## Reproducibility and outputs

- `scripts/audit_c1v0_ecological_class_change_v18.py` validates source TIFF and reference stop hashes, computes exact same-cell group transitions, and tests class-count conservation.
- `tests/test_c1v0_ecological_class_change_v18.py` synthetic integrity tests.
- `receipts/IOWA_360417_NLCD_C1V0_ECOLOGICAL_GROUP_SUMMARY_V18.json` compact immutable summary; the full 20-row JSON is generated by the workflow and distributed in the downloadable audit archive.
- `receipts/iowa_360417_nlcd_c1v0_2004_2014_site_change_v18.csv` complete source-only 20-row site/buffer metrics for all three before/after windows.
- `.github/workflows/climate_nlcd_c1v0_ecological_group_audit_v18.yml` reacquires original immutable public CI source artifact and reproduces summaries without reading NAAMP frog counts.

**Scientific interpretation:** The source-backed terrestrial record contains some genuine *mapped class transitions*, but much of the apparent 16-class turnover is within agricultural/development subtypes. The novelty question—climate and landscape change reorganizing calling locations—remains open, not yet positively supported.
