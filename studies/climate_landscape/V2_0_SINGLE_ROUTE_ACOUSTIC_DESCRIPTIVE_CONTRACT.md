# V2.0: actual NAAMP acoustic-site response readout after nominal Annual NLCD change

Date: 2026-10-08. Exploratory, post-NLCD-readback, independent study. This is **not** a new preregistered or confirmatory effect test and does not reopen the RC6 publication or the locked negative v0.9 climate prediction.

## Source-only gate completed before focal Counts.csv read

Original SHA256-pinned NAAMP 360417 runs, six survey years 2010–2015, 27 full ten-stop runs. A retrospective mapped C1V0 forest-label change appeared at stop 10 in 2006 (no pre-change NAAMP observation) and 2012 (8 pre 2010–2011 runs, 6 2012 ambiguous-date runs excluded, 13 later 2013–2015 runs). The 2012 breakpoint **was chosen because the land-cover series was viewed**, so no statistical significance, causal effect, confirmation, or independent prediction may be claimed.

## Restricted descriptive acoustic endpoint

Read original pinned USGS Counts.csv only after freezing this endpoint. Use **every species, all ten fixed recorded stops, all 27 runs**. For each genuinely sampled stop, reconstruct the number of unique species with CI>=1 and CI>=2; do not equate zero to true absence. Group by pre 2010–2011, uncertain change-year 2012, and post 2013–2015. Report run-weighted counts and relative within-route shares of strong species×stop cells as descriptions, never as independent frog population replicates. Report repeated survey dates, observer and survey-round imbalance. If duplicates exist for a single RunID/StopNumber/Species, use maximum observed positive CallingIndex once and report the number collapsed.

Lag every annual land-cover fraction strictly to calendar year preceding the frog survey (2010 survey uses 2009 image etc.). Source is **older Annual NLCD Collection 1.0** not version 1.2 and does not have category confidence. For descriptive 2012 interval only, show full ten-stop 2011→2012 class shifts without using the same-year 2012 image to explain earlier 2012 frog calls. Report 250m and 1km buffers, both with spatial overlap. Do not focus on stop 10 or stop 3 as if they were selected independently of mapped class change.

## Hard interpretation limits

A single route with 27 runs and 6 years cannot estimate a route-general habitat effect, let alone climate-change causality. The 2021 state route PDF agrees numerically with archived SiteID coordinates but does not independently certify historic 2001–2015 physical station continuity. Seasonality, weather, observer, site-specific taxon prevalence, source category uncertainty and repeated observations can all confound the comparison. All effects are unadjusted descriptive time series; no P-values, policy recommendations, occupancy/extinction, abundance or successful breeding claims are permitted.

Only if these descriptive results reveal an informative sample without a new measurement contradiction should a **separately sourced, geographically independent multiple-route study** be specified before analysis (not achieved here). Negative/descriptively flat patterns must be preserved as such.
