# v5.8 — CEWH minimal ecological-unit and larval-stage provenance enquiry (UNSENT)

**Prepared 2026-10-10 JST. Not sent, not authorized for sending by this repository.** Human author review is required; do not insert unconfirmed author metadata into public version-control history. This is separate from the closed JAE RC6 manuscript.

## Source basis and why this is shorter

The actual government MDMS metadata PDF field table has now been read: `NAME` is the *sample-point* name unique within programme; `DESCRIPTIO` is an *optional description*; `DATATYPENA` describes data types; `SAMPLECOUN` counts MDMS records. Their semantics are no longer the principal unknown. The outstanding question is whether an **independent wetland unit** is documented elsewhere. Source: [v5.8 field-definition receipt](V5_8_REAL_OFFICIAL_MDMS_FIELD_DEFINITIONS_AND_WETLAND_CROSSWALK_STOP.md), [Australian Government dataset](https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/mdms-monitoring-locations).

Official dataset public contact: `cewomonitoring@dcceew.gov.au`.

## Email text for author to review (NOT SENT)

**To:** cewomonitoring@dcceew.gov.au  
**Subject:** Flow-MER frog records: physical wetland unit and original survey-stage dictionary (metadata only)

Dear CEWH Monitoring Data Team,

I am assessing the feasibility of an independent secondary ecological study using the public Flow-MER Frog Abundance 2014–2022 data, and would appreciate clarification of a few original data definitions. This first enquiry requests **metadata or aggregated, non-identifying counts only**—not species observations, unpublished hydrology values, or sensitive site coordinates.

I verified that 48 public frog `SamplePoint` names correspond uniquely to the official MDMS monitoring-point `NAME` features (including 28 Murrumbidgee points). The published MDMS metadata PDF defines `NAME` as a sample-point label and `DESCRIPTIO` as an optional description, so neither by itself authenticates independent ecological wetlands.

Could you please advise:

1. Is there an original, versioned **MDMS `NAME/SAMO_ID` → independent physical wetland/ecological site ID** dictionary for frog monitoring during 2014–2022? Are stable site identities preserved through changes in monitoring design? If possible, can you report **only the number** of distinct wetlands represented by the 28 Murrumbidgee sample-point labels, and an aggregate count of wetland units represented by the 24-point strictly eligible subset if the programme can perform an authorized internal subset join?
2. Can the original adult-calling records distinguish **completed negative surveys** from unvisited/aborted attempts, with actual field visit dates and transect effort? Are larval-net sampling dates and effort available to support stage-level alignment?
3. How were tadpoles identified only to genus in original field surveys (especially `Limnodynastes` spp.) mapped, if at all, into the public `speciesCode` × `CPUETadpoles` records?

A source dictionary, a short yes/no answer, or a referral to the relevant data manager would be sufficient. Thank you for making the monitoring data available.

Sincerely,  
[Author to provide name and affiliation when they choose to send]

## Explicit stop

The public PDF does **not** provide the physical wetland ID crosswalk. Do not estimate independent wetland counts by sharing of `DESCRIPTIO`, `ANAE_TYPE` or `SystemType`; do not treat point uniqueness as replicated independent wetlands. Do not analyze new frog outcomes or use the existing v5.2 sign reversal as species-specific recruitment/payoff without source-authenticated stage mapping. This enquiry has **not** been sent.
