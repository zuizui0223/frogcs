# v3.5 — Rain as water, weather cue and sound: a prospective causal-discrimination design

**Date: 2026-10-08. New theoretical/design hypothesis only — NOT a result from frogcs/NAAMP.**
No manipulation has been run, no field permission sought, no Iowa-native 2010–2015 stop-event W/D acquired, no new frog outcomes read, and the locked JAE RC6 manuscript and results are unchanged.

## 1. Why three generators were insufficient

The existing JAE RC6 results support *rain-associated* recurrent species × place chorus expression, with directional rain-selective CI3 site targeting (β=0.02449; 95% CI 0.00700–0.04198) and a **post-hoc** deep (k≥4) versus shallow rain-alignment contrast (Δβ=0.02970, P=0.02098). The rain × history first-order comparator predicts 1.3323 concentration against observed 1.6503 on the 2,916-pair matched source, so this tested generator is inadequate. Neither finding establishes **why** rain selects chorus sites.

The earlier v3.4 models are not an exhaustive mechanism set. They miss a separate proximal causal route:

**rain/noise as an auditory stimulus that changes vocal behaviour without changing pond hydrology.**

Muñoz et al. (2020, *Hormones and Behavior* 118:104605; DOI 10.1016/j.yhbeh.2019.104605; PMID 31644890) experimentally exposed *Batrachyla taeniata* males to rain-sound and conspecific-chorus playback. In the field, **both stimuli increased calling rate**, while short-term testosterone differences were not detected. This demonstrates a sound-evoked calling response *in that species*, not generality to the NAAMP taxa, a rainfall-induced movement pattern, or multi-site spatial coordination.

Sarker et al. (2022, *Ecological Indicators*, “The effect of inundation on frog communities and chorusing behaviour”) independently found taxon-specific positive, negative, or null chorus-duration responses after actual river-flow inundation; the species × flow interaction was significant (χ²=18.8, P=0.016). Thus the hydrology pathway, too, need not be uniformly positive across taxa. The relevant distinctions are **sensory cue**, **real water availability**, and **shared physiological/biotic readiness**, not an undifferentiated “rain effect.”

## 2. Updated generator taxonomy (mapping to existing frozen nomenclature)

**Important:** v3.4 H1/H2/H3 are *working ecological explanations*; the frozen `revision/PROSPECTIVE_LOCAL_HYDROLOGY_DISCRIMINATION_SPEC_V0_2.md` uses a **different numbering** (its H1=broad weather, H2=local wetland, H3=broad state × local filter). Neither code set should be silently relabelled.

In this new prospective document, use mechanism names instead of the ambiguous H-number shorthand:

| Mechanism family | Actual generator | What would count as discriminating evidence? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WATER-LOCAL** | Rain alters physical inundation, water level, temperature or hydroperiod; these vary among wetlands | Measured local water change predicts calling and spatial placement, including across nights with comparable regional rain cues; independent water-level treatment has a response in a permissible field system |
| **SHARED-STATE × LOCAL-FILTER** | A species/night-level readiness factor alters calling at several sites, but expression is conditional on local conditions | After hydrology and acoustic context, out-of-block prediction still requires shared taxon × night structure **and** nonuniform placement; purely additive or uniform all-stop switches fail |
| **HISTORY/BIOTIC/DETECTION** | Prior site occupancy, breeding readiness, social calling, observer or auditory detectability anchors recorded activity | History and prior conspecific calls remain useful after measured hydrology; calibration of recorded versus true calls and repeated within-night recording modifies inference appropriately |
| **SOUND-CUE** | Rainfall sound (or conspecific chorusing that rain elicits) promotes calling through immediate auditory stimulation | Under unchanged measured water state, randomized rain-noise playback versus level/spectrum-matched controls alters **animal-produced calls**; separate conspecific playback tests social facilitation |

All four can coexist. A negative sound-cue experiment for one species cannot eliminate a sensory cue in other taxa.

## 3. Discriminating observations: why W/D archives alone may not suffice

Retrospectively recovered wet/dry has scientific value, but it is a **coarse ordinal, contemporaneous source**. It is neither direct rainfall sound nor a continuous hydrology record. Two ways of generating observationally similar rain × historical-site patterns are:

- **WATER-LOCAL:** R → measured water availability W → calling C; older strong site H is a proxy for high water-retention / recurrent pond suitability.
- **SOUND-CUE:** R → rain sound A → immediate calling C, while R also changes W; historically strongly occupied H sites simply have animals present/ready to respond.

Both yield positive rain × H and rain × W correlations without showing that W causally mediates R. Conditioning on W measured after rain can block mediation, introduce post-exposure/collider distortion and change the estimand. A missing W field is not evidence against either mechanism.

**Nonexperimental discriminators (weaker but feasible):**
- simultaneous calibrated physical rain acoustic intensity, local water depth/temperature and species-specific passive acoustic recordings, all timestamped;
- natural rain before any measurable local inundation change versus hydrological water arrival without local rain (e.g. independently timed managed environmental flows);
- lead/lag analyses predeclared with negative controls for call detection during rain noise (not temporal proof of causality);
- explicit strong-chorus source attribution: actual amphibian calls distinguished from speaker/rain/noise contamination.

**Causal discriminator (subject to ethics and feasible ecology):** in naturally occupied, established breeding systems, predeclare a *blocked randomized factorial* separating (a) permissible local-water manipulation from sham, and (b) short calibrated rain-sound playback from calibrated nonrain sound/silent sham, with an optional separately randomized conspecific playback arm. Manipulations must be authorized and avoid creating unsuitable egg/larval conditions; artificial ponds or managed/approved water control are alternatives. Crossed factors, not simply same-night correlation, enable independent sound and water tests.

## 4. Prospective field experiment skeleton (never applied retroactively to NAAMP)

### Sampling frame

- Choose an independently suitable frog population that calls reproducibly and tolerates the planned manipulation; the chosen species/region must be justified before outcome readback.
- Multiple genuinely spatially separated calling habitats per block, verified physical coordinates and sound propagation / cross-talk assessment; avoid treating ten listener locations around **one shared chorus** as independent ponds.
- Passive recorders continuously monitor the same clock windows across sites before, during and after interventions. Record water level/inundation, water temperature, local rainfall, airborne sound pressure spectra, wind, light and time.
- Freeze taxon-site prior use from an **earlier, outcome-separate observation period**, with an explicit rule for missing prior histories. Site-specific historical strong chorusing is an effect modifier **only if it varies within the study's randomization strata**.

### Randomized interventions / operationalization

- **Water**: independently assigned allowed water-state contrast with documented pre/post water level. Never assume water addition is “rain”—it can alter flow, temperature, substrate and chemical cues. Match disturbance/temperature with a sham where possible. If ethical permissions or hydrology independent control are unavailable, this arm is not executable and the study becomes observational.
- **Sound**: randomized playback of rain acoustics versus calibrated non-rain control with similar acoustic energy/spectral masking, plus sham speaker setup. Randomize order, washout, and speaker/recorder positions; guard against animals at untreated sites hearing the treatment.
- **Positive control**: separately predeclared conspecific-chorus playback to probe social responsiveness, with precautions against habituation and carryover.
- **Before interpretation**: blind assessment of raw call timestamps and species identities, account for artificial playback fragments and acoustic detection/masking. Define actual call rate and CI-like strong chorus from animal-produced sounds only.

### Primary estimands (separate, not collapsed)

1. **Sound-triggered vocal response:** randomized rain-sound vs calibrated control difference in animal-produced call rate with local water unchanged. An immediate effect supports a proximate auditory cue *in this study species*.
2. **Water-triggered vocal response:** randomized permissible water intervention vs sham difference, with sound arm held fixed. A delayed response after physical water change is biologically compatible with water-mediated calling but does not establish successful reproduction.
3. **Sound × water interaction:** does the same sound cue differ across wet versus dry physical habitat state? The coefficient tests **context-dependent cue sensitivity**, not necessarily demographic spatial redistribution.
4. **Spatial configuration conditional on total calling:** for a species, compare the physical sites that express strong chorus at fixed observed activity magnitude k under out-of-block predicted joint placements; a model must reproduce both k and nonuniform pair/dependence structure.
5. **History × treatment:** do historically strong sites show larger randomized sensory/hydrology responses after balancing initial occupation? This targets spatial selectivity but **must not be interpreted as individual memory**.

A study finding only a main playback effect without a history × treatment or configuration contrast **does not replicate frogcs's multi-site mechanism**.

### Decision logic

| Observed on independently held-out field blocks | Conservative decision |
| --- | --- |
| Water manipulation changes call rate/placement, sound alone does not | Direct physical-water contribution supported; not proof that historical NAAMP result is hydrologically caused |
| Rain-sound playback changes calls under verified stable water, water manipulation does not | Sensory cue supported for that species/time; do not transfer across taxa |
| Both water and sound contribute, including an interaction | A multi-route rainfall effect may combine **environmental opportunity and behavioural triggering**; a single-cause narrative is rejected in this system |
| Neither intervention changes calls while natural rain association persists | Null/inconclusive unless power, manipulation efficacy, baseline occupation and acoustic detection checks pass; consider other cues/shared readiness |
| Both marginal call totals predicted but spatial conditional configuration badly predicted | Generator remains insufficient for frogcs-like **joint spatial endpoint** even if successful at ordinary calling prediction |

Record negative results and interaction sign reversals; do not change rainfall threshold, species, pond group, stimulus amplitude or response endpoint after inspecting outcomes.

## 5. Priority and stop rules

**Priority 1:** do not reopen JAE RC6; the significant rain-selective historical-site association is already reported with appropriate limits.

**Priority 2:** the Iowa DNR historical record enquiry remains useful for source preservation and observational water variation, but W/D alone **cannot** identify the sound-cue path or causal mediation. Do not spend indefinitely engineering analysis for a historical archive that may lack W/D.

**Priority 3:** if the scientific aim is the **generating mechanism**, a small, authorized and genuinely crossed **water × sensory cue** repeated fixed-site experiment, with independent timing of regional rain and local water state, is more discriminating than another retrospective weather × history interaction.

**Priority 4:** strong-chorus, species-specific conditional placement and cross-site dependence are needed if the goal is to explain the actual frogcs spatial result; simpler playback-induced total call changes answer a narrower question.
