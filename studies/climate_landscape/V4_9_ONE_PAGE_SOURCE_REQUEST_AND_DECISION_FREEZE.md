## Why this study branch must now stop creating more models without source access

The original frogcs manuscript ([main, RC6](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/blob/main/paper/manuscript.md)) asks three linked *natural-history* questions: rainfall-associated transition from silence to strong chorus, within-taxon coherence across spatially separated stops, and preferential recurrence at historically strong species×physical sites. The newer biological opportunity hypothesis — whether that recurrent strong calling predicts later metamorph production above hydroperiod — is *prospective*, not yet a demonstrated result.

The tested NSW candidate [Ocock et al. (2024), DOI 10.1071/MF23181](https://doi.org/10.1071/MF23181) studied **29** wetlands in Gwydir (15) and Macquarie (14), **343** source site-surveys across **95** survey nights (195 Gwydir, 148 Macquarie). Calling model complete cases: **303**, metamorph model complete cases: **325**. Those are different model datasets, not 303 temporally matched breeding events with independently observed later offspring. Published site totals give **no original visit-by-night ledger**, no cross-study 15/16 physical-site proof, and no fully preserved species-specific, strictly prior strong-call-site panel. The biological and source questions remain **unknown**, rather than null.

The previous v4.5–v4.7 **synthetic** tests show why count margins cannot recover the actual night/site matrix. **Do not present the simulations as if they reconstructed Ocock**. They are useful software QC only; more fabricated arrangements will not supply a biological result.

### New relevant source (2024–25) — distinct cohort, not retroactive imputation

The [NSW government 2024–25 Water for the Environment outcomes](https://www.environment.nsw.gov.au/topics/water/water-for-the-environment/planning-and-reporting/water-for-environment-outcomes-2024-25) states **all monitored sites were surveyed in September and November 2024** in the programme, and reports frog calling and young frogs in Macquarie Marshes. This confirms an **ongoing system and potential later-year source frame**, NOT that all wetlands were sampled **on one night**, NOT that the original Ocock 2015–2020 343-visit data are public, NOT an untouched preregistered holdout, and NOT individual call-to-metamorph survival. Later site frames or survey IDs may differ.

A separate genuine **prospective extension** could compare future location-specific acoustic history versus metamorph recruitment **only if** the complete original visit opportunities, matching physical sites, hydrological measurements and future-night effort are source-authenticated first. A 2024 annual report alone does not satisfy that.

## One clear biological decision rather than repeated source-only modelling

- **If source-defined night×catchment opportunities are sufficiently crossed and dated historical species-level categories survive:** freeze an independent source-authenticated **species×wetland×night spatial-configuration** comparison against strict prior site history. Evaluate shallow as well as deep spatial states; report conditional-k spatial allocation as **predictive**, not a controlled causal effect of rain when k itself depends on rain.
- **If same-night spatial opportunities are scarce but original stage and hydroperiod link reliably:** explicitly **downgrade scope** to within-site **recurrent acoustic activity versus later metamorph occurrence/count** predicted out of year/site, with observation and stage-specific detection effort. Do not call this external verification of frogcs's k≥4 spatial concentration.
- **If original visit/zero/physical-site/hydroperiod-stage linkage cannot be authenticated:** **STOP the NSW route for causal or spatial reproduction claims**. The existing published Ocock result stands; a separate prospective multi-pond Stage 0 study is the honest scientific path. Keep JAE RC6 unchanged.
