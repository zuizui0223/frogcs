## Message for author review

**To:** cewomonitoring@dcceew.gov.au  
**Subject:** Murrumbidgee frog audio and local water records: metadata availability enquiry

Dear CEWH / Flow-MER Monitoring Data Team,

I am assessing the feasibility of an independent secondary study of frog calling on rising versus receding water, and whether earlier calling adds predictive information beyond local water history. Before requesting observations, I would appreciate existing documentation or a brief availability answer for three items:

1. **Recording coverage and physical units.** Is there a dated mapping between recorders, depth loggers and physical wetlands, including station moves and multiple instruments per wetland, together with scheduled, valid, missing or poor-quality recording intervals? Existing aggregate coverage by year and concurrently monitored wetlands would help; no names or coordinates are needed.
2. **Local water measurements and quality.** Do time-aligned wetland depth/inundation histories and their clock, datum, precision, calibration, maintenance and gap documentation exist? Any existing information on agreement or shared errors between colocated loggers would also be useful; no water values are requested.
3. **Acoustic validation and access.** Are species annotation/classifier versions and independent validation records retained for different recording and environmental conditions? Please identify the relevant catalogue/version, data custodian and permitted route for discussing use of derived records.

At this first stage I am requesting metadata only, not sound recordings, frog observations, protected locations or unpublished water measurements. A document link, short yes/no response or referral is sufficient; no new analysis needs to be prepared for this enquiry.

Thank you for maintaining these monitoring records.
