## Message for author review

**To:** cewomonitoring@dcceew.gov.au  
**Subject:** Murrumbidgee frog recorder/depth logger wetland crosswalk — source metadata only

Dear CEWH / Flow-MER Monitoring Data Team,

I am assessing whether long-term Murrumbidgee frog recordings can support an independent ecological comparison of calling on rising versus receding local wetland water, and whether prior calling contributes prediction beyond measured hydrological history.

The published 2024–25 Murrumbidgee Annual Report describes an authoritative project field database, separate hydrological records, and a Lead Data Manager and Spatial Data Manager responsible for governance and original spatial identifiers. **Could this enquiry be directed to those managers or the custodian of the historical passive-acoustic recorder archive?** I am requesting existing metadata only, not observations or protected locations.

1. **Original join and valid observation opportunities:** Is there a date-effective source mapping from historical autonomous frog **recorder/deployment IDs** to independently defined physical **wetland IDs** and local **depth-logger IDs**, including relocations and shared instruments? Does a scheduled/valid/missing/poor-quality interval manifest exist? If already available, an aggregate year-by-year count of wetlands with *overlapping valid audio and logger records* is enough; nominal device counts alone are not needed.
2. **Depth history, time resolution and QA:** The 2024–25 annual report mentions depth loggers across 35 wetland sites and derived **daily** depth series. For the subset potentially matched to passive frog recorders, are the **original logger timestamps**, timezones, measurement datums, dry thresholds, accuracy/calibration, gaps, sensor replacements and any error/agreement documentation retained? Is there a source field dictionary for `qualityCode`/`evaluationCode` where applicable? No depth values or locations are requested.
3. **Passive audio source and permissions:** Are species-level frog annotation or classifier outputs linked to the original recording-opportunity ledger, with model/version and independent wet/dry/noise validation? Please distinguish the archived **automatic recordings** from 2024–25's reported **6 × 2-minute human listening surveys**. Can you identify the responsible source custodian, catalogue version and approved route for discussing non-sensitive derived reuse? If the NSW Saving our Species programme is relevant to the same original passive-audio deployments, a referral or clarification would help.

A short yes/no response, existing schema/document links, non-identifying coverage figures or a referral is sufficient. I do not need new tabulations or any raw audio, frog detections, private coordinates, unpublished water-depth measurements or database access credentials for this first enquiry.

Thank you for your help.

Kind regards,
