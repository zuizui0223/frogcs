**Subject:** Historical Iowa NAAMP (2010–2015): stop-level wet/dry data and station records

Dear Volunteer Wildlife Monitoring Program Coordinator,

I am conducting a source-availability audit of historical frog and toad listening-stop environmental records from Iowa's NAAMP-era surveys (2010–2015). The currently available Iowa DNR datasheet has a *Site Wet or Dry (W/D)* field for each stop, while the USGS public NAAMP release's `Stops.csv` does not contain a wet/dry or local-water-level field.

Could you please advise whether the Iowa DNR has any **state-native 2010–2015 ten-stop NAAMP route** records—such as original paper datasheets, scanned forms, database exports, or dictionaries—containing wet/dry, standing-water status, water temperature, or other local wetland conditions **for each stop on each survey visit**?

I would initially need only (1) confirmation of record availability, (2) the survey years and NAAMP route types covered, (3) the field definitions and missing-value codes, and (4) whether a RouteNumber/stop/visit-date or USGS SiteID/RunID crosswalk exists. I am particularly trying to verify historical stop positions and any relocation logs for **routes 360104 (stop 3)** and **360412 (stop 7)**. If those records were not kept, a brief confirmation would also be extremely helpful.

For this first audit, I am **not requesting species call records or identifiable volunteer information**. If records exist, please let me know the appropriate access and citation process.

Thank you very much for your time and for maintaining the Iowa monitoring program.
