# Iowa original NAAMP 2010–2015: official records-access decision v3.3

**2026-10-08. Source discovery / records access only.** No archived state-native stop-event wet/dry values obtained; no frog outcomes accessed; no outbound contact or public-records request submitted. Separate independent climate–landscape study; JAE RC6 remains locked.

## A positive result from source-route discovery

The Iowa Department of Natural Resources documents **two real official access routes**, not merely an apparently downloadable public database:

1. **Program coordinator / custodian enquiry** via [Iowa VWMP programme About](https://programs.iowadnr.gov/vwmp/Home/About), which explicitly supplies `vwmp@dnr.iowa.gov` for questions about programme records and the online volunteer database. The official [frog-and-toad survey guidance](https://www.iowadnr.gov/programs-services/volunteer-opportunities/wildlife-monitoring/frogs-and-toads/survey) says today's observers both submit data through a portal **and send paper forms**. The latter is evidence of a present-day custody workflow, **not** proof that 2010–2015 paper copies survive.
2. **Formal Iowa public-records portal**, directly linked from the official [DNR Open Records Request page](https://www.iowadnr.gov/about/contact/open-records-request). The page links **Search Released Information and Records** and **Request a Public Record**, states requests can be made online or by other channels, and warns that records-copy fees may apply. It does **not** promise every wildlife-monitoring record is public, surviving, free, or promptly obtainable. Use this only if ordinary data-custodian enquiry is inconclusive or formal documentation is needed.

The same DNR page also lists a separate **OpenText Document Search Web Application**, but explicitly enumerates participating programmes (principally air, land and water compliance programmes). **Frog and Toad Call Survey / VWMP is not listed among those indexed programmes.** Therefore a negative search in that document index would **not** establish absence of the historical Iowa amphibian records.

## Public volunteer database is not proven to expose the target source

[The Iowa VWMP database landing page](https://programs.iowadnr.gov/vwmp/) displays a county picker and a **Download Report** action, and a volunteer login for entering records. This proves the visible UI exists, **not** that this action exports complete 2010–2015 NAAMP stop-event `wet/dry`, date, `SiteID` and historical station relocation information. A county report is not inherently an original unsummarized source table, and no report file or private volunteer session was obtained or inspected in this task. Do not claim publicly retrievable W/D without reading an actual authorized public export and its schema.

## Recommended request sequence

**First (low friction):** send the existing reviewed unsent custodian enquiry to `vwmp@dnr.iowa.gov`, requesting **metadata only**: does a native 2010–2015 **ten-stop NAAMP** dataset or original paper archive still hold per-stop `Site Wet or Dry`, water temperature, skip status, dated route/stop identity or relocation history? Ask about year/route completeness, field dictionaries and access/citation terms. The original national USGS `Stops.csv` lacks local water-state variables, verified at [pinned USGS source-only run 37774857243](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/37774857243). The 2023 state report shows only **partial progress** harmonizing historical NAAMP/traditional data and trends mainly since 2013, so avoid asserting an already completed 2010–2015 combined table.

**Second (only if needed):** if the custodian cannot locate or release relevant original records, consider an author-approved formal **Request a Public Record** through the Iowa public-records portal. Limit scope to original ten-stop NAAMP route forms / field dictionary / non-personal route-and-stop relocation records from 2010–2015. Include routes 360104 stop 3 and 360412 stop 7 as *illustrative exact keys*, but ask first whether a route-wide historical source is available to avoid selection on satellite appearance. Request preservation of original missing and skipped stop codes and file version metadata. Ask for an estimate of fees first; do not automatically incur charges. Do not ask for volunteer identities, email addresses or species response outcomes during this first access stage.

**Third:** do not declare the local hydrology hypothesis tested until the released environmental panel has actually passed response-blind source checks for physical-site identity, complete nonmissing ten-stop surveys, reproducible W/D variation, and [within-stop and survey-event non-additivity](scripts/audit_iowa_state_native_wetdry_feasibility_v32.py).
