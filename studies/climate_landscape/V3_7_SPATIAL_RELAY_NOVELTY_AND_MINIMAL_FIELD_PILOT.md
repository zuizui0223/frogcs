# v3.7 — Ecological novelty and minimum decisive field pilot: a rain-to-chorus spatial relay

**Date:** 2026-10-08. **Status:** source-backed literature comparison plus *prospective* feasibility and decision plan, not an experiment or a new frogcs result. **RC6 science/figures/claims are frozen and unchanged.** No new NAAMP outcomes, Iowa-native W/D, animal manipulations or contact/permit applications.

## 1. What is already known — do NOT claim as frogcs novelty

The mechanism literature has already demonstrated **three distinct phenomena**, at different biological units:

| Original work | Demonstrated result and unit | Why it is NOT proof of the frogcs spatial mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Muñoz et al. (2020), *Hormones and Behavior* 118:104605, DOI [10.1016/j.yhbeh.2019.104605](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yhbeh.2019.104605) | In *Batrachyla taeniata*, field playback of **rain sounds and conspecific chorus** elicited extra male calls over short ~31.5-minute exposures. Short-term blood testosterone did not differ between exposure groups, and laboratory responses were weaker | **Acute vocal activation** in one species; no demonstration of coordinated *between-wetland* activation, exact-k placement, or recurrence at previously strong sites |
| James et al. (2015), *Biological Conservation* 192:287–293, DOI [10.1016/j.biocon.2015.10.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2015.10.001) | Conspecific call playback altered adult distribution and chorus locations **within ponds** in 4 of 5 *Litoria aurea* experimental ponds during longer treatments | Demonstrates **social acoustic attraction and within-pond redistribution**, which cannot be called a new concept. It is not an experiment on repeated historical *independent wetland* selection following rainfall cues |
| Ethier et al. (2026), *Scientific Reports* 16:13073, DOI [10.1038/s41598-026-43613-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-43613-6) | In **captive** *Pseudacris maculata* trials, playback of conspecific chorus increased egg viability (mean 0.797 vs 0.663, p=0.033), and a separate 6-hour high-chorus exposure altered testicular gene expression in reproduction-related pathways. The reported calling-duration increase was **not significant** (p=0.072), despite an almost twofold mean | Shows a potential **downstream reproductive/physiological consequence** of social acoustic signals in one species. It does **not** show a rain-sound effect, cross-wetland distribution, or frogcs's spatial-history interaction |

**Interpretive correction:** “Frogs calling more after rain sound”, “frogs moving toward conspecific playback”, and “conspecific cues having some reproductive consequences” are **not** new mechanisms in general. Their independent findings cannot be assembled as if a causal chain had been experimentally proven. Timescales (minutes / hours / days) are *study-specific precedents*, not yet established lag constants for another system.

## 2. The useful unresolved ecological gap

The narrow *prospective* question that connects these literatures to the observational frogcs result is:

> **Does a transient environmental or sensory rain cue cause species-level calling expression to be redistributed preferentially among historically recurrent, physically separate breeding wetlands, beyond a change in total call output or generic local availability?**

An even stronger potential question, **not demonstrated yet**, is whether a *sequential relay* occurs:

```
rainfall sound and/or physically changing wetland state
                ↓
rapid species-specific vocal activation (minutes)
                ↓
conspecific acoustic/social feedback and altered readiness (hours)
                ↓
non-uniform placement / attraction within and among wetlands (hours–days)
                ↓
potential reproductive outcomes (measured separately if feasible)
```

Do **not** assume all arrows happen, that frog movement between ponds is practical in the study species, or that the last arrow follows from calling intensity. The novelty would lie in **identifying which of these arrows and spatial scales actually operate together**, not asserting the entire cartoon.

### Hard geography requirement

The original NAAMP pattern spans route stops, not ten microphones aimed at one pond. A *within-pond* movement response like James et al. 2015 cannot replicate a **between-pond/route-scale** configuration result. The field design must identify truly independent breeding wetland units, quantify acoustic propagation and possible individual movement, and avoid interpreting “no calls at a point” as animal absence.

## 3. Minimum decisive study: three stages, with explicit stopping points

### Stage 0 — no intervention: determine whether the spatial endpoint is even estimable

Before selecting experimental treatments, monitor the proposed target system using genuinely simultaneous passive recorders at independent wetlands. Record:
- taxon detection/animal-produced calls during fixed observation windows, detector false negatives and raw noise conditions;
- immutable physical pond IDs and coordinates, site/route membership and cross-site sound audibility;
- local water level/inundation and water temperature, rain/noise measurements and clock time;
- strictly preintervention historical strong-site use (and **not simply pond presence**), established from independent baseline monitoring;
- independent evidence of whether animals of the target taxon can plausibly be present at candidate historical and nonhistorical sites.

**Non-negotiable:** if target-taxon occupancy/availability is restricted to historical sites and comparison sites have no animals capable of responding, the trial cannot distinguish preferential *activation of present animals* from apparent history-based site targeting. Separate verified availability/occupancy from calling state as far as practical, and treat unknown availability explicitly.

**GO only if:** multiple acoustically separable wetlands and both historical/reduced-history categories are observed, with adequate comparable visits and authentic animals' calls detectable at the proposed stimulus background. If not, report the endpoint **not feasible in that study frame**, rather than adding microphones or cherry-picking ponds after outcomes.

### Stage 1 — sensory-cue pilot: controlled short playback only

Within genuine independent pond/acoustic clusters, preassign balanced **rain-sound**, **nonrain acoustic control** (appropriately calibrated), and **sham/silence** exposures across site×night blocks, with pre/stimulus/post monitoring and washouts. The control design should distinguish generic noise masking/startle and biological rain cue **as operationalized**, recognizing no control can match every acoustic characteristic without potentially recreating the stimulus.

Measure:
1. immediate pre/post **actual animal-produced call rate** with calibrated detection and unknown/nonassessable audio preserved separately;
2. interaction of randomized sound exposure with *pre-existing* historical-site class, interpreted as **effect heterogeneity by an observational moderator**, not a causal effect of historical use itself;
3. time course and persistence after sound stops, with all lag windows fixed before focal response readback;
4. jointly observed call-state vector over separate ponds, with missing/skipped checks and no post-treatment k selection for causal ITT comparisons;
5. conditional exact-k site placement as a **secondary model-predictive diagnostic**, not a causal fixed-k allocation effect.

Measure whether sound reaches nominal controls and group outcomes at the true treatment/acoustic cluster. A sound effect on merely one nearby group does not support taxon-wide landscape coordination. If the sound stimulus has no detectable effect after calibration and a pre-specified informativeness/power check, the sensory pathway is unsupported *in this population*; this does not rule out hydrological or social pathways elsewhere.

### Stage 2 — water contrast only if real experimental control is permitted and feasible

Consider a water-only and sound×water comparison **only with animal welfare/site permissions and demonstrably independent physical water treatment**. Water addition also changes disturbance, chemistry, temperature and flow; control these as feasible. A naturally wetter pond is **not randomized W**. If the water intervention changes some calling rates but fails to reproduce the historical-site conditional spatial pattern in out-of-block prediction, report **marginal behaviour explained, configuration unexplained**.

This stage should not be promised as automatic after Stage 1; water treatment may be infeasible in many natural breeding habitats.

## 4. Observational vs causal estimands — preserve the v3.6 correction

- **Causal:** assignment-based, intention-to-treat change in the entire vector of verified animal-produced calls across independent acoustic/pond blocks, plus marginal call rates. The experimental unit is the randomization unit, not a recorder or repeated timestamp.
- **Conditional prediction:** fit site-level calling propensities using separate training blocks; compare held-out observed spatial allocation among sites **conditional on realized k**. Here k is post-treatment if stimulus affects activity, so a difference at equal observed k **is not** a randomized direct causal choice effect.
- **Moderator:** `history × randomized sound` can identify heterogeneous randomized sound response across sites with different baseline histories if design rank, baseline availability, interference and all other assumptions pass. **History itself is not randomized**; do not claim memory or a causal historical template.
- **Biological downstream:** egg production, egg viability and larval success need **independent measurements** and appropriate permissions; avoid claiming these from CI2/3 alone.

## 5. Specific falsifiers and mandatory inconclusive calls

| Prospective observation | What it would actually say |
| --- | --- |
| Rain-sound induces short-term genuine calls but **no** cross-site/history interaction | Acute sensory response replicated in a new population; frogcs's spatial mechanism **not** explained |
| Rain-sound causes stronger response at recurrent sites, with the same observable animal availability and measured local water state | History-associated **effect modification** of sensory response; still not individual memory or complete hydrological mediation |
| Calls surge at first stimulated pond, then conspecific social signals predict responses at untreated independent ponds | Possible **inter-pond relay/spillover**; only test causally if acoustic exposure and cluster assignment explicitly identify network interference |
| Conspecific playback shifts distribution within a pond but not between independent ponds | Existing small-scale attraction extended/confirmed; no route-scale spatial explanation |
| Water treatment changes calling independent of sound, including recurrent-site selectivity | Local physical-water contribution in studied system; no proof of historical NAAMP cause |
| Neither stimulus changes calls, sound masks detection, ponds share the same audible cue, or historical-site categories reflect absent animals | **Null only if adequately powered and treatment effective; otherwise inconclusive** |
| Calls increase but direct egg/offspring metrics do not | Behavioural change without demonstrated fitness benefit; do not infer reproductive success |

## 6. Pre-registration and sample-size boundary

The v3.6 *synthetic matrix rank* checks only algebraic identifiability. They are **not** a field power analysis and do not justify 8 ponds, 10 nights, a fixed playback length, species choice or a particular sound level. All of those need:
- independent site-availability/detection pre-pilot data;
- measured spatial spillover and feasible treatment blocks;
- a declared smallest biologically meaningful call-rate or joint-configuration effect;
- predeclared clustering/carryover structure, missingness expectations, and independent randomization;
- simulation-based power for the **actual clustered randomization** using those independently justified inputs;
- timestamped contracts before experimentally measured outcome inspection.

**STOP rule:** If a genuine multi-pond study is not feasible, prioritize a well-calibrated single-pond auditory response experiment **as a narrower study**, without selling it as frogcs's route-scale mechanistic resolution.
