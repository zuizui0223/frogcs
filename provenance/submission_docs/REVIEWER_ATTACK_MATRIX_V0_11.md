## Strongest current claim

> **Rainfall-associated expansion of the behaviourally realized frog community is more boundary-biased than uniform activation predicts; the structural departure survives strong dry-state persistence and restriction to surveys made by the same recorded observer.**

## Deliberate revision reserve

The untested species-specific activation-shift null is the first analysis to run only if an editor or reviewer asks whether species-level response heterogeneity alone can reproduce the observed four-component allocation.
