## Suggested subject

**Request for station-level Wisconsin Frog and Toad Survey historical data**

## Suggested message

Dear Mr. Badje,

I am preparing a research analysis using the traditional Wisconsin Frog and Toad Survey and am writing to ask whether an existing electronic export of the historical station-level records is available for research use.

I am interested in the traditional 10-stop routes, ideally from the earliest electronically available years through the latest completed season. The essential fields are:

- route ID and route type;
- survey year, survey period and date;
- station/site ID or station number;
- species/taxon;
- station-level call index, with documentation of how blanks/non-detections are coded.

If they are maintained separately, route/station lookup or history tables would also be very helpful, especially former route IDs, first permanent year, and station replacement/relocation history. A codebook or data dictionary would also be appreciated.

An existing CSV, XLSX, database export or other native electronic format is completely fine; there is no need to reshape the data or calculate any summaries for me.

I am specifically looking for the underlying records and documentation, rather than any custom analysis or interpretation of rainfall or species responses.

Thank you very much for your time and for maintaining this long-term monitoring programme.

## Why the first message is intentionally short

Do not include:
- the NAAMP effect size;
- expected direction;
- the concentration statistic;
- deep-versus-shallow results;
- requests for staff to test the hypothesis;
- a long explanation of the frozen analysis.

Those details are unnecessary for data access and can bias or burden the exchange.
