# Conceptual-neighbor audit v0.1

## Purpose

Define the closest existing ecological concepts to the current NAAMP frog result and state exactly what the manuscript does and does not add.

The goal is to prevent novelty inflation and to give a compact answer to the reviewer question:

> Is this result simply spatial synchrony, response diversity, a JSDM residual association, site fidelity, or standard pulse ecology under another name?

## Current empirical object

Primary endpoint:

> Among taxa acoustically recruited on the wetter survey, is calling more concentrated within the same taxa across repeated sites than expected after species-specific rainfall response, strictly prior species × physical-site propensity, dry-state persistence and total wet activation are represented?

Primary result:
- prior-history pairs: 2,916;
- observed concentration: 1.650;
- principal comparator: 1.353;
- conditional residual: 0.297;
- null 95% range: −0.132 to 0.119;
- P = 0.000999.

Biological refinement:
- excess is concentrated in the deep multi-site tail;
- CI2/3 dominates deep expression;
- strong activity preferentially recurs at taxon-specific historical strong-chorus sites;
- a uniform route-wide taxon state is too coherent.

## Closest conceptual neighbors

| Concept | What it already explains | Why the current result is not just that concept |
|---|---|---|
| Resource/pulse dynamics — Yang et al. 2008; Holt 2008; Jentsch & White 2019 | Pulse magnitude, duration, timing and spatial distribution can determine ecological response | The NAAMP test conditions on the realised amount of activation and asks whether the **biological response pattern across repeated sites** remains structured |
| Spatial synchrony / Moran-type forcing — Liebhold et al. 2004 | Common environmental forcing can correlate dynamics among locations | The manuscript does not claim synchrony; route stops were sequentially sampled. The endpoint asks whether within-taxon multi-site concentration remains after measured first-order response and site history are represented |
| JSDM residual association — Pollock et al. 2014; Tikhonov et al. 2017 | Residual co-occurrence/association can remain after environmental covariates, and associations can vary with environment | Residual association is not claimed as a new statistical principle. The ecological finding is a pulse-linked, within-taxon repeated-site pattern with a specific deep-tail and recurrent-site structure |
| Response diversity — Elmqvist et al. 2003; Ross et al. 2023; Kunze et al. 2026 | Species differ in response magnitude/direction to environmental change or pulse disturbance | The primary comparator already allows taxa to differ in rainfall response. The remaining question is how a given amount of realised activity is arranged across **sites within taxa**, not how responses differ among taxa |
| Site fidelity / recurrence — Picardi et al. 2023 | Return to locations can reflect memory, habitat structure or chance and requires a null expectation | The manuscript does not infer individual fidelity. Historical SiteID use enters the comparator; the residual result is that strong chorus still preferentially reappears at recurrent taxon-specific sites |
| Constrained incidence/co-occurrence null models — Gotelli 2000; Gotelli & McCabe 2002; Dormann et al. 2009 | Matrix structure can be tested against preserved margins and other first-order properties | The method is not the novelty. The biological result is the specific failure of a comparator preserving taxon response, site history, dry persistence and response magnitude to reproduce the observed pulse-linked multi-site pattern |

## What is genuinely new here

The strongest defensible novelty is not a new component process.

It is the empirical conjunction:

1. a short rainfall-associated behavioural state change;
2. unusually deep expression of the same taxa across separated repeated sites;
3. excess within-taxon concentration after response magnitude and first-order taxon/site propensities are represented;
4. selective involvement of recurrent taxon-specific strong-chorus sites.

The manuscript can therefore claim:

> **Response magnitude did not fully describe the realised spatial pattern of frog chorus activation.**

It should not claim:

> joint responses contain information beyond marginals.

That statement is already embedded in several established ecological frameworks.

## General hypothesis

Safest:

> **Response magnitude alone may be insufficient to describe a short behavioural pulse because the realised spatial pattern can retain additional ecological structure after first-order taxon and site propensities are represented.**

This is a hypothesis generated from the NAAMP frog system, not a universal law.

## Sharper prospective prediction

Post-hoc NAAMP refinement suggests:

> **Broad activation need not erase spatial selectivity: deep multi-site activity may remain more strongly associated with recurrent high-use sites than shallow activity.**

This is more discriminating than simply asking whether spatial dependence exists.

It is explicitly prospective because:
- exact-k deep historical-site alignment was developed after opening NAAMP;
- the deep-minus-shallow contrast is post-hoc;
- external replication is needed to test transferability.

## Reviewer-objection map

### “This is just Moran synchrony.”

Response:
- no simultaneity is observed;
- the manuscript does not use literal synchrony as the endpoint;
- the principal comparator includes measured taxon response and site history;
- residual within-taxon concentration remains.

### “This is just response diversity.”

Response:
- response diversity concerns variation among species' responses;
- species-specific rain response is already represented;
- the residual endpoint concerns repeated-site expression within taxa.

### “This is just a JSDM result.”

Response:
- JSDMs establish that residual joint structure can exist;
- the paper does not claim otherwise;
- the ecological contribution is the particular pulse-linked spatial form of that structure and its historical-site association.

### “Recurrent sites imply philopatry.”

Response:
- no individual identities are observed;
- Picardi et al. show that return patterns can emerge without memory;
- the manuscript uses recurrent chorus location / persistent suitable site language only.

### “Same response total, different configuration is mathematically trivial.”

Response:
- mathematically, many configurations can share a total;
- biologically, the non-trivial result is that the observed configuration systematically departs from a comparator that already encodes species response, prior site propensity, dry persistence and total activation;
- the departure has interpretable structure: deep within-taxon spread and recurrent-site targeting.

## Publication positioning

The manuscript should be sold as:
- a frog natural-history discovery at continental monitoring scale;
- a spatial behavioural-community result;
- a new empirical decomposition of a familiar rainfall response.

It should not be sold as:
- a new general theory of joint distributions;
- the first evidence for spatial synchrony;
- the first evidence for site recurrence;
- a causal rainfall mechanism;
- a universal anuran law.
