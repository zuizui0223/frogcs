# Current endpoint paper spine v0.2

## Status

Reader-facing authority for the NAAMP manuscript on this branch. Supersedes v0.1.

The paper has **one primary endpoint** and one broader ecological hypothesis.

## Primary endpoint

Conditional within-taxon multi-site concentration among taxa acoustically recruited on the wetter survey.

Primary analysis:
- 2,916 wetter–drier pairs with strictly prior physical-site history;
- route-cross-fitted taxon-specific rainfall response;
- strictly prior species × SiteID propensity;
- dry-state persistence;
- matched expected wet incidence.

Frozen result:
- observed concentration **1.650**;
- principal comparator **1.353**;
- residual **0.297**;
- simulated residual 95% range **−0.132 to 0.119**;
- plus-one Monte Carlo **P = 0.000999**;
- held-out rain × history prediction **1.332**.

This is the inferential centre.

## What is not the novelty

Do not claim novelty for:
- rainfall-sensitive frog calling;
- cross-site chorus covariance or synchrony;
- recurrent calling locations;
- the mathematical fact that joint distributions contain information beyond marginal probabilities;
- residual association after environmental covariates.

Spatial synchrony theory and joint species-distribution models already cover those general ideas.

## Biological novelty

The novel ecological question is the **realised spatial pattern of a short behavioural pulse after response magnitude is represented**:

> After first-order taxon response, prior site propensity, dry-state persistence and total activation are represented, how are the realized activations distributed among taxa and sites?

In NAAMP the answer is:
1. activation is over-concentrated within taxa;
2. the excess appears in a deep multi-site tail dominated by CI2/3;
3. deep expression preferentially involves recurrent taxon-specific strong-chorus sites;
4. the pattern is neither independent wetland response nor a uniform whole-route switch.

## General hypothesis

The manuscript does **not** present marginal-versus-joint structure, spatial pulse structure, or response diversity as new concepts.

The broader ecological hypothesis is:

> **Response magnitude alone may be insufficient to describe a short behavioural pulse because the realised spatial pattern can retain additional ecological structure after first-order taxon and site propensities are represented.**

The NAAMP frog result is one empirical example: the realised pattern is over-concentrated within taxa and its deepest expression is associated with recurrent strong-chorus locations.

## Interpretation analyses

### State switching
Silence→CI2/3 and silence→CI3 results show that the primary endpoint concerns substantial reproductive acoustic state change, not merely weak audibility.

### Historical placement
Prior strong SiteID targeting shows where the excess spatial concentration is expressed.

These analyses interpret the endpoint; they are not co-equal headline endpoints.

## Generality boundary

The pattern is broad within NAAMP but geographically heterogeneous. FrogID supports only the taxonomic-deepening direction, not the multi-site spatial pattern.

External replication can test whether the multi-site spatial result transfers to another fixed-site network. It is not required for the present NAAMP submission.
