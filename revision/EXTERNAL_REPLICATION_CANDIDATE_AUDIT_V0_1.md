# External replication candidate audit v0.1

**Audit date:** 2026-10-01  
**Outcome-data status:** not inspected.

## Candidate 1 — Wisconsin Frog and Toad Survey (selected first)

### Structural evidence from public programme documentation

- statewide annual monitoring since 1984;
- approximately 100 permanent roadside routes;
- 10 listening stations per traditional route;
- stations intended to remain permanent;
- three survey periods each year;
- 5-minute listening at each station;
- call index 1/2/3 with semantics closely aligned to NAAMP:
  - 1: separated individual calls;
  - 2: overlapping calls, individuals distinguishable;
  - 3: continuous/full chorus;
- route/station descriptions exist to enable repeated surveying from the same locations;
- route stations are separated sufficiently that individuals should not normally be audible from more than one station.

### Eligibility status

**Design-eligible pending raw-data-access audit.**

Remaining questions before opening outcomes:
1. Are site-level historical electronic records available with route, station, date/run, taxon and call index?
2. Is permanent station identity reliable enough to detect relocations/replacements?
3. Are survey dates sufficiently complete to link external rainfall data?
4. Are enough route-years available with repeated prior history for the frozen comparator?
5. Can observer identity be represented anonymously for sensitivity analysis?

If these fail, WFTS is classified structurally ineligible before outcome analysis.

## Candidate 2 — Iowa Frog and Toad Call Survey (fallback only)

Public documentation establishes:
- monitoring since 1991;
- 5–10 wet areas per route;
- three annual survey windows;
- repeated use of assigned routes;
- site-level species calling data;
- >2,300 sites and >45,000 visits reported programme-wide by 2025.

Reasons not selected first:
- route size varies from 5 to 10 sites;
- the programme absorbed former NAAMP routes;
- traditional routes and later NAAMP-style routes coexist.

Iowa is evaluated only if WFTS fails structural eligibility before the WFTS concentration endpoint is opened.

## Prospective rule

Selection of WFTS was based on survey design metadata only, not observed direction or magnitude of the target response.
