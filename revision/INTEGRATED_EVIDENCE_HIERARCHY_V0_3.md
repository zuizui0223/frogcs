# Integrated evidence hierarchy v0.3 — spatial organization of frog chorus activity

## Central biological claim

**Recent-rain conditions are associated with frog reproductive acoustic activity organized across multiple separated route locations: silent cells often enter strong chorus states, recruited taxa deepen across several sites more strongly than species-specific rain response plus prior site history predicts, and the deepest responses preferentially involve recurrent taxon-specific chorus locations.**

## General ecological claim

**Response magnitude alone may be insufficient to describe a short behavioural pulse because the realised spatial pattern can retain additional ecological structure after first-order taxon and site propensities are represented.**

In NAAMP, that residual structure is expressed as within-taxon multi-site concentration with preferential involvement of recurrent strong-chorus locations. The pattern lies between independent wetland responses and a uniform route-wide switch. This is a testable generalization from the frog system, not a universal law and not a claim that residual association, spatial synchrony or pulse spatial structure are themselves new concepts.

## Important distinction between principal and secondary nulls

The exact N,K-conditioned calculation fixes recruitment count and total incidence but treats taxa as exchangeable. It therefore does not preserve intrinsic taxon spatial breadth, habitat affinity or prior site use.

For the integrated manuscript, the principal comparator is instead:

**cross-fit species-specific rainfall response + strictly-prior species × physical-SiteID history + dry persistence + matched wet-incidence magnitude.**

The held-out rain × history gate is the strongest sensitivity.

## Claims intentionally not made

- rain causes the response;
- acoustic zero means physical absence;
- route stops activate simultaneously;
- individuals move among stops;
- the mechanism is memory or philopatry;
- social facilitation or hydrological connectivity is identified;
- strong chorus equals successful spawning;
- state effects are homogeneous;
- NAAMP cross-fitting is independent confirmation;
- the full mechanism is universal across anurans.

## Exploration boundary

The concentration endpoint and the subsequent spatial-scale and recurrent-site refinements emerged after extensive analysis of NAAMP. Cross-fitting prevents focal-route leakage for fitted response terms and structural transfer diagnostics but does not create an untouched confirmation set.
