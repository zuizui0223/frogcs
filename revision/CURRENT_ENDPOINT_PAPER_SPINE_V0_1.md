# Current endpoint paper spine v0.1

## Status

Reader-facing authority for the NAAMP manuscript on this branch.

The manuscript is organized around **one primary endpoint**, not three co-equal endpoints.

## Primary ecological question

> After species-specific rainfall response, strictly prior species × physical-site propensity, dry-state persistence and total wet-state activation are represented, is rain-associated calling still more concentrated within the same taxa than expected?

## Primary endpoint

Conditional within-taxon multi-site concentration among taxa acoustically recruited on the wetter survey.

Primary analysis set:
- 2,916 wetter–drier pairs with strictly prior physical-site history.

Principal comparator:
- route-cross-fitted species-specific rainfall response;
- strictly prior species × SiteID propensity;
- dry-state persistence;
- pair-level matching of expected wet incidence.

Frozen result:
- observed concentration coefficient: **1.650**;
- principal comparator: **1.353**;
- conditional residual: **0.297**;
- simulated residual 95% range: **−0.132 to 0.119**;
- plus-one Monte Carlo **P = 0.000999**;
- stronger held-out rain × history gate: **1.332**.

This is the inferential centre of the paper.

## Biological interpretation analysis 1 — what kind of activity creates the endpoint?

Rain-associated change is not only weak amplification:
- 65.3% of the CallingIndex slope comes from dry-state acoustic zeros becoming positive;
- 87.1% of that activation enters CI2/3;
- direct 0→CI3 activation is positive;
- the deep multi-site tail is dominated by CI2/3.

Role in the paper:
**state switching gives biological meaning to the primary endpoint.**

It is not a separate headline endpoint.

## Biological interpretation analysis 2 — where is the excess expressed?

Prior species-specific strong-chorus SiteIDs predict strong wet-state chorusing:
- prior CI2/3 SiteID → wet CI2/3: **β = 0.151**;
- targeting strengthens toward the rain-favoured survey: **β = 0.0245**.

Post-hoc exact-k placement further links deep activation to recurrent strong-chorus sites.

Role in the paper:
**site recurrence identifies the spatial substrate on which the primary endpoint is expressed.**

It is not a separate headline endpoint.

## Secondary defence / refinement

Keep secondary to the primary result:
- flexible nonlinear weather and ERA5 72-h rainfall;
- bounded residual dependence and lag profile;
- uniform species-night scalar overcoherence;
- same-observer/site robustness;
- exact N,K diagnostic;
- taxonomic and geographic breadth;
- monitoring covariance diagnostics;
- FrogID taxonomic deepening.

These analyses defend, bound or generalize the endpoint. They do not replace it.

## Reader-facing synthesis

> Rain-associated frog chorus activation is more concentrated within taxa across separated route locations than expected from species-specific response, prior site use, dry-state persistence and total activation. The excess is carried by strong chorus and is selectively expressed at recurrent taxon-specific locations.

Conceptual interpretation:

> Frog reproductive acoustic activity can be organized at an intermediate spatial scale: broader than independent wetland responses, but more selective than a uniform route-wide switch.

## Inferential boundary

The endpoint was developed after opening NAAMP and is exploratory at manuscript level. Cross-fitting prevents focal-route leakage in fitted comparator components but is not independent confirmation.

External replication would upgrade the evidence, but is not required to assemble or submit the present NAAMP paper.
