# Multi-site chorus coherence synthesis v0.1

## Central result

Across repeated NAAMP routes, recent-rain conditions are associated with:

1. direct silence→strong/full chorus transitions;
2. excess participation of recruited taxa at third-and-later sites;
3. within-taxon multi-site concentration exceeding a comparator that includes cross-fit species rainfall response, strictly-prior site history and dry persistence;
4. preferential recurrence of strong chorus at species-specific historical sites.

## Principal spatial test

Population: 2,916 pairs, 439 routes, 20 states with strictly prior physical-site history.

Observed concentration β = **1.6503**.

Principal comparator:
- cross-fit species-specific rainfall response;
- strictly-prior species × SiteID propensity;
- dry-state persistence;
- pair-level magnitude matching.

Predicted concentration β = **1.3535**.
Conditional residual = **0.2969**.
Null residual 95% interval = **−0.1319 to 0.1187**.
P = **0.000999**.

Strongest sensitivity:
- add held-out rain × local-history gate;
- predicted β = **1.3323**;
- residual = **0.3180**;
- null 95% = **−0.1172 to 0.1255**;
- P = **0.000999**.

## Secondary exact diagnostic

Exact N,K-conditioned raw excess:
β = **0.2439**, 95% CI **0.1474–0.3404**.

This is secondary because its combinatorial expectation treats taxa as exchangeable and does not preserve taxon-specific spatial breadth or prior site use.
