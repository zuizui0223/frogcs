# Dynamic-hydrology pre-result interpretation map — 2026-10-07

**Frozen before the M0–M3 hydrology-augmented concentration result.**

## Fixed ecological question

The existing phenomenon is within-taxon multi-site concentration of frog reproductive acoustic activity beyond the principal first-order comparator.

The remote-sensing extension asks what generates that concentration, not whether another spatial pattern can be discovered.

## Outcome A — M1 is sufficient

If adding focal-month local surface-water state moves the concentration residual inside the M1 null interval:

> A broad favourable-night response is spatially filtered by event-specific local wetland water state.

Interpretation:
- regional weather helps determine when reproductive activity is expressed;
- local water availability helps determine where it is expressed;
- the earlier unexplained concentration is largely resolved into dynamic environmental filtering.

Do not claim unique causal mediation or reproductive success.

## Outcome B — M1 is insufficient, but M2 materially reduces the residual

If sustained three-month wetness adds explanatory power beyond focal-month state:

> Chorus placement reflects recent hydrological persistence, not only the instantaneous survey-month water state.

This is a temporal-habitat-memory interpretation, not biological memory by frogs.

## Outcome C — M1/M2 are insufficient, but M3 reduces the residual

If hydro_sd_12m adds information after current and sustained wetness:

> Environmental variability in breeding habitat contributes to the spatial organization of reproductive acoustic activity.

This would support the idea that frogs respond not only to whether wetlands are currently wet but to the recent variability / hydroperiod regime within which breeding opportunities occur.

Do not describe variability as beneficial or harmful to fitness without demographic data.

## Outcome D — M3 is sufficient only after all hydrology terms

If the full M3 model moves the residual inside its null interval while M1/M2 do not:

> The concentration is best explained by a combination of current water state, persistence and recent hydroperiod variability rather than a single wetness snapshot.

## Outcome E — hydrology is partial but residual remains

If M3 reduces the residual but it remains above the null interval:

> Dynamic local hydrology is one generator of the chorus configuration, but additional taxon-night, demographic, social or finer-scale habitat processes remain.

## Outcome F — hydrology adds little or worsens prediction

If M1–M3 remove little or none of the residual:

> JRC-scale monthly surface-water dynamics do not explain the concentration pattern.

This weakens the leading unmeasured environmental explanation and raises the relative priority of:
- water depth / water temperature not captured by surface-water extent;
- sub-monthly hydrological transitions;
- vegetation / calling substrate;
- demographic availability;
- social facilitation or breeding readiness.

Do not search alternative remote-sensing windows or buffer sizes to rescue the primary result.

## Manuscript consequence

The current RC6 submission remains separate until the result is frozen.

A sufficient or strong partial hydrology mechanism would justify reconsidering a fuller mechanistic paper because it changes the study from a residual-pattern report to an environmental-filtering mechanism.

A null hydrology result supports keeping the current paper concise and treating the remote-sensing extension as a falsification/future-work result rather than expanding the submission.
