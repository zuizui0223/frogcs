# Knowledge-shift synthesis v0.1 — what this paper changes

## Purpose

State the paper's contribution as a change in ecological understanding rather than as a list of significant results.

## What was already known

Before this study, the literature already established that:

- frog calling responds to rainfall, temperature, humidity, hydroperiod and season;
- taxa differ in weather sensitivity;
- frog calling can covary across sites;
- wetting or inundation can alter chorus richness and species-specific activity at multiple locations;
- species × wetland calling matrices can show persistent structure;
- recurrent site use and site fidelity are known in frogs;
- ecological theory already distinguishes marginal environmental responses from joint or residual association.

Therefore this study does **not** change knowledge by showing that frogs call after rain, that sites covary, or that calling locations recur.

## What this study changes

### 1. Environmental response has at least two separable empirical properties

The conventional description of a short behavioural response emphasizes **response magnitude**: how many taxa or sites respond, or how much activity increases.

The NAAMP result shows that magnitude does not uniquely determine the realised response.

After representing:
- species-specific rainfall response;
- strictly-prior taxon × physical-site use;
- dry-state persistence; and
- realised wet-state activation magnitude,

the calling pattern remained more concentrated within taxa than expected.

Authorized conceptual update:

> **A short behavioural pulse can be described by both response magnitude and realised configuration. The same amount of activation can be arranged through different taxon-by-place patterns.**

This is not a new theorem about joint distributions. It is an empirical ecological result showing that the distinction matters in a large repeated behavioural monitoring system.

### 2. The relevant spatial unit is intermediate

The observed pattern is inconsistent with two simple spatial pictures:

- independent responses at individual wetlands; and
- a uniform route-wide taxon switch.

Instead, activity is route-spanning but spatially selective.

Authorized ecological update:

> **The spatial unit of reproductive acoustic response can be broader than an individual wetland while remaining more selective than a landscape-wide switch.**

A useful working description is **broad activation plus local filtering**.

### 3. Short-term activation can reveal slower taxon × place structure

Strong wet-state chorusing preferentially reappeared at physical sites where the same taxon had previously reached strong chorus.

Thus favourable conditions need not create a spatial pattern from scratch.

Authorized ecological update:

> **A fast behavioural state can be expressed through a slower recurrent taxon × place template.**

This does not imply individual memory, philopatry or movement. Persistent habitat quality, hydrology, local demographic availability or other site-level processes remain possible generators.

### 4. The pattern has conditional spatial and temporal transferability within NAAMP

The concentration excess persisted when:
- species rainfall responses were trained with each focal State excluded; and
- species response plus physical-site history were frozen through 2008 and tested only in 2009–2015.

Authorized update:

> **The realised configuration is not only a pooled same-sample artifact; its conditional excess persists across blocked geographic calibration and a later time period within the same monitoring programme.**

These are post-hoc within-programme transferability tests, not independent external confirmation.

### 5. One mechanistic interpretation became weaker, not stronger

A non-overlapping future-rainfall amount model produced a prediction very similar to the antecedent 72-h rainfall-amount model while still failing to explain the concentration excess.

Therefore this study should not argue that the incremental fit of 72-h rainfall amount demonstrates a specifically antecedent rainfall mechanism.

Authorized update:

> **The robust result is the spatial organization of the response, not a uniquely identified rainfall-amount mechanism.**

## What remains unchanged

The study does not establish:

- rainfall causality;
- literal synchrony among sequentially sampled stops;
- movement or colonization among stops;
- demographic occupancy change;
- individual memory or philopatry;
- reproductive success;
- a unique hydrological, social or demographic mechanism;
- external transferability beyond NAAMP;
- a universal law for environmental pulses.

## Strongest statement of knowledge change

> **Previous frog studies established when and where calling changes with weather. This study shows that, even after the amount of response and major taxon/site propensities are represented, the realised taxon-by-place configuration retains ecological structure.**

## General ecological implication

The broader hypothesis is not that spatial dependence is new.

It is:

> **Environmental-response studies may lose ecological information when they reduce a multivariate response to magnitude alone. For short behavioural pulses, realised configuration can be a second empirical axis of response.**

This formulation is the preferred bridge from frog natural history to general ecology.
