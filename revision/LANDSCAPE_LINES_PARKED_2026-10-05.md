# Landscape lines parked — 2026-10-05

## Decision

The two post-RC6 landscape branches are **parked** and are not part of the RC6 submission path.

Branches:
- `landscape/naamp-behaviour-v1`
- `landscape/naamp-behavioural-landscape-v1`

## Why

The first prespecified landscape-direction prediction expected deep multi-site activation to be more dispersed/fragmented than the exact-k, fixed-q comparator.

That prediction failed.

After readback, the observed pattern was recognized as modestly more compact than expected. Subsequent compactness/recurrent-core analyses are therefore post-hoc with respect to the failed directional prediction.

The compactness signal is retained honestly for provenance, but it is not promoted into a new RC6 discovery line.

## Authorized summary

- Prespecified dispersion/fragmentation prediction: **FAIL**.
- Post-hoc route-order compactness: modest, exploratory.
- Recurrent-site involvement: already part of the RC6 refinement layer.
- Recurrent sites as geometric cores of compact domains: **not supported by the prespecified core test**.
- Exact geographic geometry: blocked by known coordinate errors.
- No new NAAMP landscape metric, threshold, mechanism or external-covariate outcome test is authorized.

## Future use

Frozen landscape predictions may be tested only in an independent externally reopened study, including WFTS if that route is explicitly reopened and structurally eligible.

Such a test:
- is not required for RC6 submission;
- does not retrospectively change RC6;
- must not be used to retune the NAAMP landscape analyses.

## Submission consequence

**Do not delay RC6 for the landscape line.**

The current paper remains the endpoint-centred RC6 manuscript. Further scientific upgrade should come from prospective external evidence, not another NAAMP exploratory branch.
