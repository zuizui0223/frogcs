## Story sequence

**silence → strong chorus → spatial depth → higher-order within-taxon coherence → historical site recurrence → breadth and heterogeneity**

### Figure 1 — Chorus-state switching

Purpose: show that rain-associated CallingIndex change is not merely amplification of already-active cells.

Required content:
- total CallingIndex β = 2.841
- 0→positive β = 1.855; 65.3% of total
- 0→CI2/3 β = 1.615; 87.1% of activation
- 0→CI3 β = 0.441 (0.114–0.768)
- same-observer + same-SiteID 0→CI3 β = 0.514 (0.101–0.927)

Message:
**Much of the wet-state change is a switch from acoustic silence into overlapping/full chorus states.**

### Figure 2 — Spatial depth and strong chorus

Purpose: localize the unusual response after route entry.

Required content:
- second-stop β = 0.132, not exceptional under the primary nulls
- third+ β = 0.473, P=0.000999 under both primary nulls
- fourth+ β = 0.363
- 97.3% of third+ coefficient carried by CI2/3
- same-observer + same-SiteID strong share = 95.4%

Message:
**The unusual spatial component begins beyond the second occupied site and is carried by substantial chorus states.**

### Figure 3 — Higher-order conditional concentration

Purpose: make the core novelty explicit.

Required content:
- observed full-sample higher-order β = 1.524
- uniform prediction = 1.138
- persistence prediction = 0.784
- cross-fit species prediction = 1.198
- strictly-prior subset observed = 1.650
- joint species + prior SiteID + dry persistence prediction = 1.353
- final held-out rain×history gate prediction = 1.332
- all conditional P = 0.000999
- exact within-pair N,K-conditioned raw excess β = 0.2439 (0.1474–0.3404)
- same-observer + same-SiteID exact raw excess β = 0.2543 (0.1375–0.3712)

Message:
**At the same amount of first-order recruitment and spread, activity remains too deeply concentrated within the same taxa for increasingly structured first-order species/site models.**

### Figure 4 — Historical site targeting

Purpose: show that coherence is historically placed rather than arbitrary.

Required content:
- prior strong SiteID → wet CI2/3 β = 0.1511 (0.1291–0.1731)
- prior strong SiteID → wet CI3 β = 0.0778 (0.0619–0.0937)
- same-observer targeting β = 0.1589 (0.1344–0.1833)
- rain-selective CI3 targeting β = 0.02449 (0.00700–0.04198)
- same-observer rain-selective β = 0.03067 (0.01331–0.04804)

Message:
**Strong wet-state chorusing preferentially reappears at species-specific physical sites with a prior strong chorus record.**

### Figure 5 — Breadth and heterogeneity

Purpose: separate pooled robustness from homogeneous response strength.

Required content:
- 53 taxa; 25 positive
- 19 taxa contribute ≥1% of positive mass
- top1 = 18.8%; top5 = 54.2%; HHI = 0.0855
- all leave-one-taxon-out totals positive; minimum β = 1.209
- all 21 leave-one-state-out estimates and CIs positive
- LOSO β range = 0.967–1.775
- minimum LOSO CI lower bound = 0.397
- state-specific: 14/17 positive point estimates, 2/17 wholly positive CIs

Message:
**The pooled higher-order signal is broad within NAAMP but geographically heterogeneous.**

## Figure-story test

A reader seeing only Figures 1–5 should recover:

> chorus-state switching → spatial depth → higher-order within-taxon concentration → recurrent site placement → broad but heterogeneous programme-scale support.

The main figures must not imply literal synchrony, movement among stops, causal rainfall forcing, individual memory/philopatry, or a universal anuran mechanism.
