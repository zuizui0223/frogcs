# WFTS not pursued — 2026-10-05

## Decision

The prospective Wisconsin Frog and Toad Survey (WFTS) replication was **specified but is not being pursued for the current project**.

As of this decision:
- no WFTS data-request email has been sent;
- no WFTS response export has been requested or received;
- no WFTS frog-response data have been inspected or analysed;
- WFTS is not part of the RC6 evidence base;
- WFTS is not a submission gate;
- no WFTS acquisition or execution step is currently authorized.

## Why the WFTS files remain

The repository contains response-blind schema definitions, request templates, mapping rules, fail-closed preflight logic, synthetic QA and prospective analysis code.

These files are retained for **provenance only**. They document that an external replication route was specified before any WFTS response outcome was accessed. Retaining them does not mean that data acquisition is pending.

Historical WFTS documents that contain language such as “next external dependency” or “ready for acquisition” are superseded by this decision record.

## Current interpretation

The RC6 manuscript stands on the NAAMP discovery, robustness, falsification and breadth analyses already frozen in the release package.

Transferability beyond NAAMP remains untested and is stated as such in the manuscript.

A future independent study could reuse or revise the archived WFTS design only under a new explicit project decision. It would not be a continuation automatically implied by RC6.
