# Evidence-to-claim ledger v0.1 — novelty-maximized integrated track

## Purpose

This ledger maps every major frogcs result to the strongest claim it can support, and separates **main-text discovery**, **supporting defence**, **negative/falsification evidence**, and **prospective confirmation**.

The manuscript-level objective is to avoid treating multiple correlated decompositions as separate discoveries. The main paper should make one biological claim:

> **A short rainfall-associated behavioural gate reveals non-independent, within-taxon multi-site chorus organization on recurrent species × place templates.**

Everything else either establishes one step in that claim, limits it, or defends it against simpler explanations.

### Main biological claim

**Recent-rain conditions are associated with a switch into strong reproductive acoustic states whose extra spatial expression is unusually concentrated within the same recruited taxa and preferentially reappears at historically strong species-specific sites.**

### Main conceptual claim

**Response magnitude alone may be insufficient to describe a short behavioural pulse because the realised spatial pattern can retain additional ecological structure after first-order taxon and site propensities are represented.**

In NAAMP, that structure appears as excess within-taxon multi-site concentration with preferential expression at recurrent strong-chorus locations. The data support this as a frog-system hypothesis, not as a universal law or a claim that spatial synchrony, response diversity or residual association are themselves new concepts.

## B. Why the result is surprising

The intuitive model of rain-responsive chorusing is first-order:

1. rain increases calling probability;
2. more taxa become detectable;
3. taxa already associated with suitable sites respond according to their own rainfall sensitivity and site affinities.

frogcs shows an extra layer after those first-order properties are represented:

- second- and third-stop marginal participation are ordinary under the activation nulls;
- the excess accumulates because taxa persist unusually far into the fourth-through-tenth-stop tail;
- almost all cumulative deep spread is overlapping/full chorus;
- species-specific rainfall response and strictly-prior species×SiteID history still underpredict the concentration;
- strong choruses recur at the same species-specific sites;
- rain selectively strengthens that historical placement.

The unexpected result is therefore **not the amount of activation alone, but the realised multi-site pattern after that amount and the main first-order propensities are represented**.

A concise surprise statement for the paper:

> **Rain-associated activation is not fully described by how much calling appears: the same taxa are expressed across more sites than expected, especially at recurrent strong-chorus locations.**

## C. General ecological hypothesis hierarchy

### G1 — safest generalization

**Response magnitude alone may be insufficient to describe a short behavioural pulse.**

Supported by:
- matched total wet incidence in the principal comparator;
- excess within-taxon concentration after first-order taxon and site propensities are represented;
- deep multi-site expression dominated by CI2/3.

### G2 — stronger and more distinctive

**The realised spatial pattern of a pulse response can retain ecological structure after response magnitude, transferable taxon response and persistent site propensity are represented.**

Supported by:
- principal joint comparator;
- held-out rain×history gate;
- recurrent strong-site targeting;
- exact-k deep-placement result as post-hoc refinement.

This is an ecological hypothesis about the realised pattern of response, not a new statistical theorem about joint distributions.

### G3 — metacommunity formulation

**Short pulses can change the expression of community structure without requiring the structure itself to be created on the pulse timescale.**

Supported by:
- rapid chorus-state switching;
- recurrence at prior species-specific sites;
- matrix growth with relatively stable broad composition/connectance context.

Use as Discussion synthesis, not as a causal or demographic claim.

## F. Defence map

### Reviewer objection: “This is just frogs calling after rain.”
Response:
- calling-after-rain is treated as background;
- the discovery is the allocation/dependence structure of strong chorus across repeated sites.

### “This is just increased detectability.”
Response:
- direct 0→CI3 and CI2/3 dominance;
- same-observer + same-SiteID robustness;
- recorded hearing/noise/wind adjustment.
Boundary: acoustic zeros are still not confirmed biological absence.

### “Widespread rain-responsive species automatically generate multi-site concentration.”
Response:
- principal cross-fit species-response + strictly-prior SiteID-history comparator;
- held-out rain×history sensitivity.
This is the most important reviewer defence.

### “The concentration score is mathematically forced by more species or incidences.”
Response:
- conditioning on route-new-taxon and extra-stop coefficients;
- exact N,K diagnostic.
Keep the exact combinatorial argument in SI.

### “A few taxa or one region drive the result.”
Response:
- taxonomic concentration metrics + leave-one-taxon-out;
- leave-one-state-out robustness;
- explicitly report state-specific heterogeneity.

### “You are calling this synchrony without simultaneous observations.”
Response:
- do not use synchrony as headline terminology;
- NAAMP stops were sampled sequentially and were designed to be about 0.5 miles (0.80 km) apart or more;
- use distributed multi-site organization / concentration across separated stops, not acoustic coordination.

### “You are inferring memory or philopatry.”
Response:
- use historical recurrence / recurrent species×site template;
- individual memory, movement and philopatry remain untested.

### “Why not claim a specific mechanism?”
Response:
- nested null sequence originally stopped after the final gate;
- after explicit reopening, flexible measured weather and actual 72-h rainfall explained only small fractions of the concentration residual;
- residual dependence remains broad within species × route-night, especially among dry-route-silent taxa;
- unmeasured hydrology, breeding readiness, demographic availability and social facilitation remain distinguishable alternatives;
- all post-reopening NAAMP analyses remain post-hoc/exploratory and do not alter the confirmation status of the integrated result.

## G. Novelty positioning

### Do not sell
- frogs call after rain;
- rain increases richness;
- sites covary;
- frogs revisit breeding sites;
- 92% of gains cross a matrix boundary.

All are either known, mechanically expected, or too close to existing frog ecology.

### Sell

**Frog-specific novelty:**
> **Rain-associated activation is expressed as strong, multi-site chorus-state switching that reuses species-specific physical sites.**

**Analytical novelty:**
> **The multi-site concentration remains excessive after modelling transferable species rainfall responses, strictly-prior species×site history, dry-state persistence and the observed magnitude of activation.**

**General ecological novelty:**
> **After response magnitude and first-order taxon/site propensities are represented, a short behavioural pulse can retain a structured multi-site pattern that is not captured by counts of responding units alone.**

The novelty is empirical and ecological: the unexplained object is the **realised taxon-by-place pattern of response**, not another marginal rainfall effect and not the generic existence of residual association.

### Main-text principle

Every paragraph in Results or Discussion should answer one of only three questions:

1. **What switches?** — silent cells → strong chorus.
2. **How is the switch spatially organized?** — excess within-taxon multi-site concentration.
3. **Where does that organization recur?** — historically strong species-specific sites.

Everything else is support.

## I. Confirmation boundary

The integrated NAAMP result remains exploratory because the concentration endpoint emerged after extensive same-dataset exploration. Route cross-fitting protects component fitting from route leakage but is not external confirmation.

If external transferability is pursued, WFTS or another eligible fixed-site network can test the already frozen endpoint/comparator prospectively. The external test must remain separate from the present evidence hierarchy until eligibility, overlap exclusion and informativeness/power rules are frozen before outcome readback.
