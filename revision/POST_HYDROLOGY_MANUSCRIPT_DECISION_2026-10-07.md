# Post-hydrology manuscript decision memo — 2026-10-07

## What changed scientifically

The leading environmental alternative was tested rather than merely named.

Two distinct public satellite measurements failed to reconstruct the within-taxon concentration:

1. JRC Landsat MonthlyHistory visible open-water extent;
2. MODIS DSWEmod partial/potential wetland state, including classes 1–3, plus recent persistence and 12-month variability.

These failures occurred under cross-fitted species-specific effects, same-SiteID wet-minus-dry contrasts, route-relative centering, and unchanged pair-level total-incidence conditioning.

The negative result also held for:
- 500-m and 250-m named spatial supports;
- DSWEmod classes 1–4 sensitivity;
- direct spatial alignment with wet-survey CI>=2 strong choruses.

## What it does NOT show

Do not claim that hydrology is irrelevant.

Untested or poorly represented states include water depth, water temperature, soil/substrate moisture, dense-canopy water, very small ephemeral pools and within-days hydrological transitions.

## Recommended paper use

Keep the main paper concise.

Do not create a new hydrology Results section in the main text.

Recommended main-text addition: one short sentence in the late Results/Discussion or Limitations stating that post hoc remote-sensing tests of local surface-water extent and partial-wetland state did not explain the concentration excess, while finer hydrological states remain unresolved.

Move to SI:
- JRC 250/500/100-m tests;
- DSWEmod 500-m M0–M3 sequence;
- classes 1–4 sensitivity;
- strong-chorus secondary;
- coordinate gate and tile-axis provenance.

## Stronger conceptual framing now justified

Avoid centering the conclusion on 'intermediate spatial scale'.

The stronger supported statement is:

> Reproductive activation has a configuration that is not recoverable from its magnitude, broad environmental forcing, persistent site use, or the tested forms of local surface-water state.

An even more biological version:

> Favourable conditions increase calling, but they do not recreate the breeding landscape from environmental opportunity alone; the realised taxon-by-place configuration retains additional structure.

This remains an observational claim. The mechanism generating the additional structure is unresolved.

## Remaining mechanism hierarchy

1. NWI wetland-type × rainfall filtering — frozen next landscape test.
2. Fine-scale water depth / temperature / substrate moisture — not available in current public data at suitable historical resolution.
3. Demographic readiness / local abundance.
4. Social or behavioural state not reducible to short-distance acoustic contagion.

Do not reopen already negative JRC/DSWEmod radius, class or temporal-window searches.
