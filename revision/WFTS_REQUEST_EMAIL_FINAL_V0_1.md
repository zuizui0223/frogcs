**To:** Andrew.Badje@wisconsin.gov

## Subject

Request for station-level Wisconsin Frog and Toad Survey historical data export

## Body

Dear Mr. Badje and Wisconsin Frog and Toad Survey team,

I am preparing a prospective external replication using the traditional Wisconsin Frog and Toad Survey. The analysis endpoint, structural eligibility rules and result-interpretation rules have already been fixed before inspection of any WFTS species × station × year outcomes.

I am writing to ask whether the existing electronic station-level WFTS records can be provided for research use, ideally for all available years of the traditional 10-stop calling routes.

The minimum fields needed are:

- route identifier and route type;
- survey year, survey period/run and survey date;
- station/site identifier and site number;
- species/taxon;
- station-level call index;
- any existing valid/incomplete survey flags.

If available, I would also greatly appreciate the existing route/station lookup or history tables, especially:

- former/legacy RouteID and first permanent year;
- route/station start or end dates;
- station replacement, relocation or renumbering history;
- documentation distinguishing traditional routes from NAAMP/protocol, phenology, mink-frog or other survey types;
- a data dictionary and missing/non-detection coding.

A CSV, XLSX, database export or the programme's existing native electronic format would all be suitable. A long-format table is convenient but not required. Current station coordinates are helpful but not essential because many current WFTS route locations are publicly documented.

To preserve the prospective nature of the replication, please **do not provide any summary of whether the focal pattern is present, any relationship with rainfall, or any species-level effect direction**. I would prefer the raw records and documentation only.

If these records are not distributed directly by the programme, I would be grateful for guidance on the appropriate Wisconsin DNR records-access process.

Thank you for maintaining this exceptionally valuable long-term monitoring programme and for considering the request.
