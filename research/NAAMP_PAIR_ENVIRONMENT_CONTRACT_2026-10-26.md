## Questions and rivals

H_environment: a crude pair association can reflect persistent site suitability and shared route-night conditions. H_coarse_interaction: association remains after adjustment, possibly negative for coarse suppression. H_lag: the partner's preceding-visit activity is associated with focal activity after own previous activity adjustment. Negative adjusted associations are only consistent with coarse interference; masking, misidentification and changing local habitat remain rivals. Positive/zero associations do not falsify subsecond call alternation or individual aggression.
