# G3: Pond-breeding priority and vegetation — source-feasibility preregistration

**5 November 2026; frozen before inspecting new GBIF stage-coverage results.** New staged Draft, based on PR162. Existing R1–R4, G1, G2 and G2B positive and negative results are unchanged.

## General hypothesis and limits

The main novelty is a **difference between producing an interaction-like geographical signature and benefiting the focal population**, supported by newly sampled but model-native structural and demographic comparisons. The possible regional reproduction-priority hypothesis is a next independent observational test, not a biological mechanism already identified in NAAMP.

The exact original source developmental contexts differ as follows: context 0 has S arrival day40, S aquatic duration40 and D duration40; context 1 has S arrival day40, S duration40 and D duration60; context 2 has S arrival day0, S duration60 and D duration60; F always arrives day20. **Context1 vs context2 changes both salamander arrival and duration.** Therefore the previously positive stage2 vegetation response is not an isolated priority effect. It motivates the preregistered *field* prediction beta(vegetation × relative salamander breeding lead) > 0 conditional on climate, original guild predictors, year, site/region and sampling effort, but that specific positive field coefficient is not derived from a directly calibrated observation model.

## What counts as a breeding observation

Only exact dated, wild, georeferenced, explicitly reproducing frogs or pond salamanders can support relative breeding chronology: independently verified eggs/egg masses, oviposition or mating/amplexus, with source-specific label and original event ID. A spring adult sighting, even an iNaturalist Research Grade ID, records occurrence rather than reproduction. LifeStage=Egg is a possible egg-stage reporting signal, not necessarily the exact egg-laying day. Larvae observed later indicate aquatic occupancy, not onset. LifeStage and reproductiveCondition are different Darwin Core terms (GBIF and TDWG). Preserve verbatim values, dataset source, method, coordinate precision, site identity and date uncertainty.

The original NAAMP source contains 15 Anura and 7 Caudata model species. Only five are in the previously frozen pond-Caudata group: Ambystoma maculatum, A. mavortium, A. tigrinum, A. laterale and Notophthalmus viridescens. Plethodon cinereus and Eurycea longicauda are separate non-pond controls, never pooled into the primary pond-breeder measure. All GBIF keys must come from original main commit 5e2f0a87c639df373fa2700b29403d2ab417a1cc, research/results/interaction_species_sdm_20261026/ssdm/species_thresholds.csv. No post-result taxon substitutions.

GBIF primary search bounds: United States, calendar years 2010–2025 inclusive (exclude incomplete 2026), occurrenceStatus=PRESENT, hasCoordinate=true, hasGeospatialIssue=false. A full record-level extraction would also exclude known coordinate uncertainty >10km and unqualified date intervals and match source dataset/date/year/region. Missing uncertainty must be reported separately. First occurrence by month or year and first spring adult appearance must never be relabeled reproductive onset.

## G0: mandatory metadata-only, outcome-blind initial feasibility

Query all 15 original model Anura, five pond-Caudata and two non-pond control taxa, obtaining GBIF total US 2010–2025 occurrence counts and **all raw LifeStage facet labels** at exact source keys. Preserve unclassified labels; classify explicit egg only when the raw label is unambiguously egg/eggs/ovum/ova (not generic larva or embryo unless source validated). Attempt reproductiveCondition faceting only as a separate optional completeness check; **unsupported API facets or failed requests are UNKNOWN, not biological zero**. No downloading thousands of records, no looking at outcome correlations or fitting a phenology × vegetation model at G0. Status and source URLs/dataset keys are archived. Early spring adult sightings do not qualify.

**Pre-fixed G0 pass threshold:** >=3 of 5 pond-cauda­te taxa and >=5 of 15 frog taxa EACH have >=50 explicit egg-stage US records across 2010–2025, AND >=300 total egg-stage records for EACH guild. These are pragmatic lower bounds for feasibility, not biological truth or significance thresholds. If either guild fails, G0=NOT_QUALIFIED; skip field-priority slope entirely. If an original source response is inaccessible, report UNKNOWN and do not declare either success or absence.

If G0 passes, a **separate G1 response-blind date/effort gate** must still verify actual per-day egg records, >=10 independent 1-degree region×year units with both frog and pond salamander explicit breeding, >=3 recurring years, >=3 distinct original provider datasets, adequate early-season sampling opportunities and enough record volume for a fixed early 20% quantile per species×region×year (>=10 dated egg reports), native-range species/locality validity and at least two months of breeding-stage observer effort. No G1 pass, no acoustic-effect fit. Matching only a single GBIF grid centroid to a NAAMP site cannot establish a same-pond mechanism.

## If and only if sufficient data exist: proposed independent field test

Compute salamander lead L = 20th-percentile frog egg-reported day-of-year minus 20th-percentile pond salamander egg-reported day, within matched source sampling region/year, after quantifying and adjusting for original source taxon/dataset/seasonal effort. L>0 means the salamander egg stage was *reported* earlier, not proof of exact oviposition onset. Avoid using the earliest uncontrolled occurrence and avoid pooling summer and spring frog taxa without species adjustment.

Join qualified region/year L to the **original NAAMP site×year** acoustics (only genuinely date-overlapping sampling years), not site-level lifetime fraction as if every year were independent. Retain original 3km flooded vegetation V and both original SDM O/S backgrounds, lower-order O/S/V and L effects, spring temperature, snowmelt/hydroperiod if available, effort, spatial/elevation/effective geographical controls. Predeclared directional hypothesis: coefficient V×L > 0, **while treating region/year as the independent unit** for clustered uncertainty and holding entire 2-degree geographical regions out. The prior chosen positive direction is model-motivated but not a field-estimated prior; there is no calibrated model F_B→adult calling transformation. Required negative controls: source/detection effort, nonbreeding adult first-report lag, matched annual climate/region, and label permutations within source region/year that preserve dates and effort. Both SDM backgrounds and regional holdouts must show a consistent result to support a cross-background association, and any positive result remains observational, not vegetation-mediated predator replacement.

## Already existing relevant source qualification

The original repository has a stricter global life-stage/event response-blind gate and a source-backed Flanders INBO same-site chorus/larva study: 96 exact shared location IDs, 247 matched site-year survey sequences, but neither measures North American NAAMP **pond salamander egg onset and site vegetation together**. Historical ESGR/Mohonk sources and several Dryad original files have reported access/measurement limitations. Reuse these results rather than making new field claims from unqualified sources.

## Stop rule and publication language

G0 insufficient -> stop before fitting V×L. Do not invent regional breeding dates or claim validated priority ecology. Independent numerical new-seed food-web replication is not independent real-world ecological reproduction. The main article remains framed as **interaction signature generation versus demographic outcome divergence**; vegetation hazard shares and stage-dependent signs are model-based mechanisms, and the field phenotype is adult acoustic recording, not larval mortality. The new G0 coverage audit cannot change those original conclusions. Keep main unchanged, PR160/161/162 Draft and untouched.
