# G4: field–mechanism observation alignment and identifiability preflight (2026-11-06)

**Frozen before new field/model cross-tab outcomes.** New stacked Draft on PR164. The goal is a stronger *falsifiable comparison* between R1 real adult frog calling and original PR161 matched 256-structure model surfaces; no wild predator mechanism is identified from correlations.

## Central identification challenge

The original R1 high-minus-low caudate-suitability difference in the Spearman correlation between odonate suitability and adult **strong acoustic calling** is **not the same statistic** as the R2 sign criterion OS−/OV−/SV+/OSV+ computed from a balanced 2×2×2 factorial on log modelled **aquatic F_B**. The site-level O/S predictors are environmental **suitability**, not observed predator supply or densities; R1 flooded V is a **3km landscape proxy**, not measured pond refuge. G1 added O×S on top of flexible shared environmental terms without a stable held-out benefit in both SDM backgrounds. PR145's externally scored source-mechanism feature also failed to improve held-out strong-call prediction beyond full empirical O/S/V interactions.

In particular, any 8-corner simulation surface **multilinearly interpolated** in continuous O,S and binary V is exactly spanned by the unrestricted eight-term basis `[1, O, S, V, OS, OV, SV, OSV]`. If added on top of a model already containing those terms, it supplies **no mathematically independent column**. A ridge-penalized apparent improvement may be reparameterization/regularization, not evidence identifying that predator mechanism. This is an analytic identity to be checked numerically on exact R1 source sites, NOT an empirical finding that the model is biologically invalid.

## Initial G4 response-blind and source-audited test

1. **Freeze real cohort and denominators**: original 4,248 NAAMP listening sites, 63,180 completed visits, 432 route components, 56 geographically distinct two-degree blocks, 506 sites with source 3km flooded vegetation binary positive. Both original uniform and target-group SDM suitability backgrounds; same original source data at `main` SHA `5e2f0a87c639df373fa2700b29403d2ab417a1cc`. Do not invent pond S/D densities or larval-to-call transition.
2. **Eight-corner field support preflight**: using the original predictor-only low/high caudate and odonate suitability tertiles, form eight source site cells `V∈{0,1}×S∈{low,high}×O∈{low,high}` with mid-tertile sites excluded *only from this coverage diagnostic*. For each SDM background, publish site, completed-visit, strong-call event, route, geographical block counts, and whether the cell meets the pre-specified minimum `>=20 sites, >=5 independent routes, >=3 independent blocks and >=200 completed visits`. If any cell fails, a full eight-corner **observed** four-sign estimator is under-supported; do not claim direct sign agreement with model simulations. These thresholds are pragmatic identifiability gates, not biological effect significance.
3. **Original synthetic source geometry**: download the archived independently audited PR161 256 original source backgrounds × three developmental contexts × four legal vegetation-function arms × eight V/S/D vertices from Actions run `41408471121`. Calculate predesignated **source-prior-average log(F_B) eight-corner surfaces** for arms 00 and 11, averaging equally over all 256 original backgrounds and three developmental contexts, with **no selection on field R1 outcomes** and no retention only of models passing four signs. Reconstruct a trilinear surface at the 4,248 sites using only the original relative suitability ranks (O/S mapped affinely to [0,1]) and the original binary V. Test numerically that each constructed feature lies in the span of `1,O,S,V,OS,OV,SV,OSV` with maximum absolute reconstruction residual <=1e-9. This demonstrates exactly why naive "add model F_B as an extra covariate to the saturated empirical interaction model" **cannot independently identify the mechanism**.
4. **Report both eligible and failing components**. Preserve all 16 field cell denominators, site classification, 2 synthetic surfaces and source feature rank-audit, their original file SHA-256. No acoustic outcome model fitting and no choosing the preferred structural variant based on field data at G4. A field signature match is conditional on (a) the field corner support gate and (b) a separately calibrated O/S/V source→field observation map. Neither is guaranteed to pass.

## What would actually strengthen field/model connection, beyond this preflight

**G5 predictive structural-constraint test**, only with G4 source support declared: replace—not append—four unrestricted O/S/V statistical interaction terms with a **single pre-committed, source-average mechanistic surface feature**. Compare against (a) additive environmental baseline, (b) four empirically free O/S/V interactions and (c) a source-structure placebo, in the **same five 56-block original spatial holdouts**. Fit only an observation coefficient on training sites, preserve model source values and independent scores, publish NLL/Brier plus block uncertainty for both original SDM backgrounds. A win in both backgrounds can support **predictive compatibility / parsimony** but still not uniquely attribute wild predation to a surface of suitability predictors.

**Actual mechanistic identification requires information not contained in these R1 predictors**: independent pond-level odonate and salamander larvae measurements, tadpole stage counts/transition rates, vegetation architecture, predator-specific per-larva hazards, hydroperiod and later actual adult calling/detection. A controlled timing×duration experiment (PR164) isolates **simulation** arrival/duration effects, not their presence in NAAMP field sites; G3's GBIF breeding egg-stage minimum failed, so adult first sightings cannot be substituted for reproductive onset.

## Publication gate

If geographically held-out, source-locked mechanism-constrained predictions beat the environment-only and unconstrained model, with uncertainty supported under **both original SDM backgrounds and sensible negative controls**, a *hypothesis-driven* model-to-field **predictive link** becomes defensible and strengthens an Ecology Letters submission. Merely achieving the four model signs or maximizing a posterior acceptance rate within the same synthetic simulations does not. Original R1 and positive/negative R2–R4 results, failed G1/G2B field discrimination and all other source PRs remain intact. PR stays Draft; no merge or journal submission.
