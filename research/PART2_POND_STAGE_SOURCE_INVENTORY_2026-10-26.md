# Part II real-data qualification inventory — 2026-10-26 (frogcs-only)

**Research objective:** support or refute a **new** within-pond life-stage × predator × hydroperiod prediction independently of the frozen NAAMP acoustic pattern. This is **not** a meta-analysis of published effect estimates. Scope checked: ESGR, Mohonk/EDI, GBIF sampling-event pairs, additional independent hydroperiod control, historical Carroll. Check actual package bytes and columns before making any positive mechanism claim.

## Newly verified experimental G2B — real stage×abiotic×perceived-predator treatment (2026-10-26)

**Dataset now actually acquired and analysed:** Delgadillo-Méndez et al. 2025, *Leptodactylus fuscus*, CSIC original [handle 10261/411885](https://digital.csic.es/handle/10261/411885), original Excel SHA256 `eb76b0ec8162278c14860fddcfe30a487ff9e8fc4b8fe40311f88dabe0fd9b25`, **CC BY 4.0**. Treatment factorial 30°C / 35°C × experimentally high/declining water × predator chemical cues present/absent, with three clutches and **8 large thermal baths**. Original response-blind audit: **79 small exposure units in the source (80 initially planned), 226 individual records (240 planned)**, **14 individuals missing from original workbook**. [QA](results/part2_g2/fuscus_assignment_g0.json), [source-frozen contract](FUSCUS_EXPERIMENT_G2B_FROZEN_ANALYSIS_2026-10-26.md), [all numerical results](results/part2_g2/fuscus_factorial_observed_results.json), [ecological interpretation](results/part2_g2/FUSCUS_G2B_REAL_EXPERIMENT_REPORT_2026-10-26.md), [successful actual-source CI](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/40258386081).

**Observed result:** 213 animal records marked survived to Gosner stage 46, 13 marked dead; 14 planned but unlisted not silently counted as deaths. Among 79 container survivor-means, water-drawdown treatments had lower stage-46 mass in both temperature strata, but the predator cue model **did not beat** environmental/clutch baseline under leave-entire-large-bath-out validation. Mass log-MSE M0→M1→M2 **0.020890 → 0.021249 → 0.021316**, time-to-stage-46 log-MSE **0.015731 → 0.016492 → 0.016485**. Study published experiment; our additional source QC / bath-heldout model is explicitly **post-publication exploratory**.

**Interpretation ceiling:** experimental animal phenotypes and risk cues, **not actual feeding, long-lived predator larvae, monitored natural waterbody drying, egg cohort survival or acoustic site returns**. This is a **controlled G2B developmental-risk countertest**, not G2 field predation proof.

**Separate field predator origin dataset quality gate:** McPherson et al. 2026, [Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.18956129](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18956129), source-MD5-verified [two original CSV assays](results/part2_g2/allobates_predator_pool_g0.json), 221 individual novel-environment records with 849 repeated rows and 220 individual shelter assays with 848 repeated rows, **but only 11 source ponds**: predator-exposed pool sample sizes **1,1,2,3,9,13,19** vs predator-free sizes **36,40,47,50**. Major pond-level imbalance and varying conspecific density mean **do not equate 220 tested individuals with 220 independent aquatic predator exposures**. Behavioral outcomes not fitted because the priority G2 water×stage measurement was achieved more directly via the controlled CSIC factorial. No direct fish consumption or egg survival measured.

### Critical negative interpretive controls

- **RC6's null has already controlled strictly prior focal taxon site history**; a significant hypothetical predator coefficient elsewhere cannot retrospectively identify the RC6 residual as predation.
- **ESGR** source project has amphibian AND macroinvertebrate surveys, but the **public 2015 Dryad workbook is specifically annotated amphibian abundance/occupancy**. Verify actual predator-stage raw availability, never assume one workbook contains both.
- **Mohonk** has frogs and salamanders recorded by life stage and environmental conditions, but no validated predator kills or survival denominators. *Egg mass counts `t` and later larvae `t+δ` are not an individual-cohort survival rate*, particularly with unequal effort and detectability.
- **GBIF stage report** across km² or calendar months is not physical encounter: stage labels, explicit event units, true zero/detection effort and native wetland IDs are mandatory. Two separate INBO projects can be connected **only** by a fully verified common `locationID` issued by their publisher, not by blurred coordinates.
- **Hydrological reset** requires documented complete summer/autumn dry-out and subsequent re-wetting, not merely a temporary-pond class or monthly meteorological drought index. If fish are absent one year and present later, also require source records for actual surveyed fish status and timing.
- **Species natural history** is pre-registered for *directly measured* species-stage predator–prey contrasts; same-season adult acoustic effects remain a separate tested and unsupported NPS H1A.

## One quantitative Part-II test, to be activated only on qualified data

**Primary endpoint candidate:** next-census prey tadpole presence/count or egg stage incidence at an exactly matched pond, not falsely labelled survival. In qualified same-pond pairs compare a block-trained **hydroperiod/canopy/fish, species/site/year/effort baseline** against a **measured predator-size/stage/overlap** extension; report *pond-held-out* log score (and year holdout), not an in-sample `p`. If no measured predator-stage exposures, **downgrade to stage-transition phenomenology**, do not imply trophic mechanism.

**Alternative strong ecological endpoint:** documented fish extinction after drought at ESGR or St Marks, with site-time hydroperiod observed: fish × pre/post dried-vs-not-dried interaction predicting prey recolonization, controlling water depth/habitat and reporting the changing eligible pool. Fish-related community filter is not the same as long-lived green-frog tadpole competition.

**Stage-network M0–M2:** (M0) documented hydrology/size-independent habitat and observation effort; (M1) measured life-stage overlap/density if available; (M2) interaction of stage overlap with measured pond dry-out and vegetation. Fit and compare only models with all required variables observed on *the same unit/time*. Otherwise report **non-identifiable**, not simulated results as ecology.
