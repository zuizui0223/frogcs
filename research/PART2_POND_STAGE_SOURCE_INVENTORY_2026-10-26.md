### Critical negative interpretive controls

- **RC6's null has already controlled strictly prior focal taxon site history**; a significant hypothetical predator coefficient elsewhere cannot retrospectively identify the RC6 residual as predation.
- **ESGR** source project has amphibian AND macroinvertebrate surveys, but the **public 2015 Dryad workbook is specifically annotated amphibian abundance/occupancy**. Verify actual predator-stage raw availability, never assume one workbook contains both.
- **Mohonk** has frogs and salamanders recorded by life stage and environmental conditions, but no validated predator kills or survival denominators. *Egg mass counts `t` and later larvae `t+δ` are not an individual-cohort survival rate*, particularly with unequal effort and detectability.
- **GBIF stage report** across km² or calendar months is not physical encounter: stage labels, explicit event units, true zero/detection effort and native wetland IDs are mandatory. Two separate INBO projects can be connected **only** by a fully verified common `locationID` issued by their publisher, not by blurred coordinates.
- **Hydrological reset** requires documented complete summer/autumn dry-out and subsequent re-wetting, not merely a temporary-pond class or monthly meteorological drought index. If fish are absent one year and present later, also require source records for actual surveyed fish status and timing.
- **Species natural history** is pre-registered for *directly measured* species-stage predator–prey contrasts; same-season adult acoustic effects remain a separate tested and unsupported NPS H1A.

## One quantitative Part-II test, to be activated only on qualified data

**Primary endpoint candidate:** next-census prey tadpole presence/count or egg stage incidence at an exactly matched pond, not falsely labelled survival. In qualified same-pond pairs compare a block-trained **hydroperiod/canopy/fish, species/site/year/effort baseline** against a **measured predator-size/stage/overlap** extension; report *pond-held-out* log score (and year holdout), not an in-sample `p`. If no measured predator-stage exposures, **downgrade to stage-transition phenomenology**, do not imply trophic mechanism.

**Alternative strong ecological endpoint:** documented fish extinction after drought at ESGR or St Marks, with site-time hydroperiod observed: fish × pre/post dried-vs-not-dried interaction predicting prey recolonization, controlling water depth/habitat and reporting the changing eligible pool. Fish-related community filter is not the same as long-lived green-frog tadpole competition.

**Stage-network M0–M2:** (M0) documented hydrology/size-independent habitat and observation effort; (M1) measured life-stage overlap/density if available; (M2) interaction of stage overlap with measured pond dry-out and vegetation. Fit and compare only models with all required variables observed on *the same unit/time*. Otherwise report **non-identifiable**, not simulated results as ecology.
