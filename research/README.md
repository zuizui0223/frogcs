## Corrected PEM meaning and mechanistic triad hypothesis (2026-10-25)

**PEM = USFWS NWI Palustrine Emergent Wetland (freshwater emergent marsh), NOT phylogenetic eigenvector maps.** PEM1A=temporarily flooded, PEM1C=seasonally flooded, PEM1F=semipermanently flooded; PEM1 stems persist. Verified from official NWI GIS code schema ([4-row official receipt](results/NWI_PEM_OFFICIAL_CODE_READINESS_2026-10-25.json)). None of the original NAAMP/NPS listening sites currently has a **source-verified site×NWI PEM wetland polygon** assignment — no PEM-specific acoustic effect is claimed.

- **[PEM wetland frog breeding synchrony and salamander/odonate network evidence synthesis](results/PEM_THREE_GUILD_NETWORK_SYNTHESIS_2026-10-25.md)**: strict separation of experimentally supported wood-frog explosive breeding reducing older-tadpole cannibalism, predator/prey phenological size mismatch, wrong inducible predator-specific defensive morph, 2009 Hokkaido D⊣S⊣F indirect protection and 2023 Australian frog→emerging-dragonfly reverse feeding.
- **[Species-stage and NWI source-anchored new study protocol](PEM_STAGE_CONSTRAINT_NETWORK_SPEC_V0_1.md)**: test whether persistent PEM hydrology/plant structure predicts historical species-specific frog chorus sites and modifies larval predator constraints; preserves frozen RC6 and known NAAMP coordinate geometry blockers.
- **[Synthetic-only PEM1A/C/F seasonal triad model](scripts/simulate_pem_three_guild_stage_balance.py)** / [synthetic report JSON](results/pem_three_guild_stage_balance/synthetic_results.json) / [successful CI](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/40203066228). 128 arbitrary parameter draws, 432 wetland×stage scenarios, 216 paired frog synchrony contrasts. Without imposed concentrated-brood crowding, 108/108 favor a narrow synchronized pulse; with a deliberately strong alternative density/resource cost, 96/108 favor dispersed breeding, 12 tie. **These numbers do NOT estimate empirical PEM wetland responses or actual coexistence; they show mechanism-dependent sign reversal in a transparent toy model.**
- **Next actual data**: response-blind corrected physical NAAMP coordinates, NWI Wetlands MapServer polygons (code `ATTRIBUTE`), local hydroperiod time series, stem-structure and egg/larva predator-stage/individual abundance panels. Do not merge GloBI/GBIF 1° overlap as verified local predator encounter.

## PEM wetland persistence × frog stage-network tests (new 2026-10-25)

**[Source-qualified PEM/frog chorus and stage-network synthesis](results/PEM_CHORUS_HISTORY_STAGE_NETWORK_SYNTHESIS_2026-10-25.md)** combines two very different evidence tiers, **not a single causal result**:

- **New REAL NPS result**: [source-verified Hydroperiod × same-species strong chorus recurrence](results/nps_hydroperiod_history/observed_results.json), [code](scripts/nps_hydroperiod_strict_history.py), [CI PASS](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/40203501528). Adjusted prior-call association: Ephemeral **+0.352** (95% interval .252–.442), Semi-permanent **+0.502** (.396–.603), Permanent **+0.495** (.411–.568), all on the original 1,815 site×taxon×year NPS sample. The additional hydro × history interaction is **post-hoc observational**; one Ephemeral-only within-park×species×year subgroup design is not estimable. **NPS Hydroperiod≠USFWS NWI PEM1A/C/F**.
- **New fully SYNTHETIC three-guild model**: [rare-invasion season and cohort counterfactual results](results/pem_mutual_invasion/synthetic_results.json), [code](scripts/pem_three_guild_pair_resident_invasion.py), [CI PASS](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/40204209121). **Within-frog synchronized egg pulses** and **between-species synchronized predator/prey stages are different questions**: under the arbitrary tested toy parameters, every nonzero predator-stage shift improved 3-guild pair-resident invasibility relative to exact phase match (96/96), whereas narrow frog breeding pulses were favored in 108/108 when concentration had no added cost and reversed when cost was imposed. These are **thought experiments**, not findings about natural PEM wetland population frequencies or frog breeding adaptation.
- **NWI spatial assignment safety**: [official PEM1A/1C/1F code parser and source-verified wetland-point overlay](scripts/nwi_pem_verified_site_overlay.py), [schema and synthetic CI PASS](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/40204461792). Assigns no habitat to unverified NAAMP/NPS listening stops, whose available locations are not sufficient to identify exact breeding ponds.
- Biology: odonate larvae can directly eat tadpoles but indirectly protect them by suppressing salamander-larval behavior (Kishida et al. 2009); separate Australian frog–dragonfly pair has direct qualitative reversed predation during emergence (Beranek et al. 2023). These motifs are not observed simultaneously in this repository's acoustic stations. The next decisive study must measure the **same pond**: water/hydroperiod, above-water odonate emergence stems, underwater tadpole cover, frog reproduction/cohorts, predator larval stages and field predation intensity.

- **[Global within-species immature-versus-adult habitat result](results/global_ontogenetic_wetland/observed_results.json)**: **21** species (19 dragonflies, 2 frogs), **179** same-species 10° latitude-band×calendar-month strata. ## Research boundaries

- **NAAMP SiteID ≠ verified individual pond;** see [coordinate geometry QC](../revision/NAAMP_COORDINATE_GEOMETRY_QC_V0_1.md).
- **Past calling sites ≠ individual philopatry**, nor egg/tadpole/juvenile success.
- The unexplained residual concerns broad already-measured atmospheric/observation effects, **not all local wetland environment**. Pond-level plants, hydroperiod and predator stages remain absent.
- **Vegetation × fish can be positive or negative** depending on frog taxon, plant structure, predators and target outcome; do not assume all emergent cover is refuge.
- **Predicting marginal call probability is not predicting the constrained multi-site configuration**; C requires a separately held-out null generator preserving the appropriate first-order incidence constraints.
- A stage-resolved GloBI edge and an inferred wetland habitat class cannot be joined into demonstrated concurrent local predation.
