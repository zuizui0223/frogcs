# Low versus high odonate supply and the origin of the caudate interaction

Selection: all 1,925 of the original 20,000 G12 draws with log_OS and log_OV below -1e-6. Equal weight per selected draw and vegetation level. Each draw retains its original assumptions, rates and stage calendar. Low and high predator supplies are 0.25 and 2.0 for both predators; neither is predator absence. These are density inputs in the annual immigration term, not observed site densities.

Run `python research/scripts/joint_density_history.py`, `python research/scripts/joint_density_recruitment.py` and `python research/scripts/figure_joint_density_history.py`. The first script checks all four archived interaction coefficients for every draw at 1e-10 absolute tolerance. Each natural trajectory uses the original 80 years, half-day steps and years 61–80 reporting window. Counterfactual seasonal completions are readouts only and never fed back into those histories.

- season_means.csv.gz: years 61–80 means at every draw × vegetation × supply corner for the natural reconstruction and three paired replays. Common frog sets the entry frog cohort to the geometric mean across the four predator supply cells within draw/year/vegetation. Matched predators takes the geometric mean of starting caudates across odonate supply at each caudate supply, and vice versa for odonates. This removes cross-predator history while retaining their respective low/high gradients. Both matched applies both interventions. Seasonal predation and intraguild interactions remain active in every replay.
- high_minus_low_odonate.csv: high-minus-low odonate contrasts disaggregated by caudate supply. interaction_summary.csv and interaction_by_vegetation.csv difference those contrasts between high and low caudate supply. interaction_by_draw.csv.gz preserves heterogeneity. log_F_start and log_F_end use the log of the 20-year arithmetic mean; log_retention = log(mean completion / mean start). H_D and H_S are mean seasonal cause-specific cumulative per-frog hazards. Mean log survival equals minus their sum; log_retention need not equal mean log survival.
- annual_account_by_draw.csv.gz: exact accounting on the original log-mean entry-density scale. The next cohort receives surviving adults, delayed recruits and identical frog immigration across predator supplies. For each of the final 20 entries the previous adult stock and the cohort completing its aquatic season two years earlier are recovered. The delayed recruitment difference is partitioned between that cohort's entry density and its season survival by averaging both orders of replacing low- with high-odonate values in the nonlinear recruitment formula. The log-mean slope converts the additive differences to the archived scale. The three components sum to the archived OS. This is an attribution of the most recent annual update, not a unique lifetime causal allocation; previous frog densities themselves reflect earlier survival.
- high_low_odonate.png: natural high-minus-low contrasts averaged over vegetation and selected draws.

Natural high-minus-low odonate log entry contrasts are -0.19349 at low caudate supply and -0.22077 at high supply. Mean seasonal retention contrasts are -0.54455 and -0.54456. Interactions in odonate hazard (-0.15574) and caudate hazard (+0.15576) nearly cancel, with opposite net seasonal retention interactions by vegetation (-0.03444 absent, +0.03441 present). The cohort completion interaction is -0.02729, changing to +0.00213 with common frog entry. Matching both frog and predator entry backgrounds yields +0.30620. These are contrasts on completion in one season, distinct from the original sign-selection criterion on entry. In the exact annual-update accounting, the entry interaction -0.027275 comprises -0.010914 via surviving adult stock, -0.006649 via lagged entry in recruitment and -0.009712 via lagged survival in recruitment. The first two terms account for 64.4% of the mean signed interaction. Neither the selection-conditioned estimates nor the allocation apply directly to field calling.
