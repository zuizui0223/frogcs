# Caudate amplification among the original 20,000 simulations

Run `python research/scripts/two_sign_pathways.py`. Inputs are the eight archived G12 shards. There are 1,925 draws with log_OS and log_OV both below the original -1e-6 threshold, 3,274 OS-only, 7,592 OV-only and 7,209 neither. OS is the change in the high-minus-low odonate supply effect at high versus low caudate supply, averaged across vegetation levels, on log long-term aquatic frog cohort size. A negative interaction alone does not prove both simple slopes are negative or that individual predation hazard increased.

- case_composition.csv compares the distribution of assumptions among the 1,925 positive and 18,075 remaining draws.
- scenario_counts.csv gives unadjusted absolute counts, the conditional fraction among OV-negative draws, and sensitivity to a 0.01 threshold, raw-scale signs and the saved survival floor. Joint_and_floor uses all draws in that row as denominator. Timing, trophic edges and the response switch are also fully cross-tabulated.
- matched_contrasts.csv compares observed settings within common strata only. Each stratum has equal weight; repeated identical parameter rows are averaged first. Exact strata fix all other assigned inputs (structural flags, calendar and nuisance rates), excluding the model ID which encodes the flags. Broader strata match attack, recruitment, maturation and the remaining focal switches, permitting differences in other features. Results from OV-negative draws alone describe a selected subset. Differences are second minus first. Continuous os_v0 and os_v1 are reconstructed from the mean OS and the stored three-way difference. Exact pairs are sparse and are local to supported settings. No new simulated interventions or field measurements enter this comparison.

Late caudate arrival was associated with more joint signs. Holding baseline odonate duration at 60 days, moving caudates from late/short to early/long reduced the joint rate by 7.5 percentage points across 428 broader matched strata and by 7.9 points in 38 exact strata. Arrival and duration change together. Reciprocal predation was enriched among cases, but 81 cases had neither edge. Turning on odonate predation on caudates did not switch joint status in any of 29 exact strata. With both edges and 60-day odonate duration, the density response was associated with higher joint frequency in the late/short caudate context (10.7% to 18.9%) and lower frequency in the early/long context (7.9% to 3.1%). In all 31 exact response-switch strata, four gained and one lost the joint sign. The unadjusted advantage of the density switch did not persist for both contrasts below -0.01 (3.6% off, 2.8% on). These comparisons identify calendar-dependent candidate pathways, not a unique cause of the field pattern. The shards lack within-season prey densities and predator-specific kills.
