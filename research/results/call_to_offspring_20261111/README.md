# Earlier calling and later aquatic stages in two independent field datasets

Exploratory reanalysis, November 11, 2026. No NAAMP pond was matched to these independent studies. One observation per exact location, species, breeding year and outcome stage. The endpoint is the first eligible census strictly after a calling survey in the same calendar year. Counts are recorded survey counts, without an effort or survival denominator.

## INBO
Hyla arborea, Flanders. Prior chorus size is the mean of the daily counts of calling males. Repeated same-day adult records were collapsed by median. The alternative exposure is the fraction of prior survey days with audible males, restricted to at least two surveyed days. Responses are source-labelled counts including explicit zeros. Multiple count records on the chosen date use their median, without assuming captures are additive; the single-record sensitivity avoids this ambiguity. Explicitly incompatible survey methods are excluded for each stage. Missing effort descriptions remain in the broad comparison; terrestrial metamorph counts explicitly documented are analysed separately. Other frog taxa had at most two eligible site-years per stage. Repeated visits do not identify parentage. No adjustment for population size or capture efficiency.

Rank correlation with earlier male counts was 0.378 [0.248,0.494] for tadpoles at 93 ponds, 241 pond-years, and 0.207 [-0.060,0.462] for metamorphs at 35 ponds, 48 pond-years. Intervals resample whole ponds 1,500 times. Larval results persisted when restricted to calling-positive ponds/years and single count records. Chorus frequency and larval counts correlated at 0.415 [0.310,0.509]. For metamorphs the frequency result was positive in the simple comparison of 42 pond-years but uncertain after adjusting for season, year and effort. With site fixed effects and repeated sites only, the association between year-to-year calling and larval counts within ponds was uncertain. The primary analysis of earlier counts includes 241 larval pond-years rather than the 245 in the earlier detection-only analysis; the two analyses use different eligibility and aggregation rules.

Larvae of at least one named or genus-identified newt were confirmed during 84 first follow-up pond-days, representing 50 ponds. Among visits with a single frog count and explicit Triturus cristatus larval status, 29 were positive and nine explicitly negative. The estimated change in adjusted rank slope was +0.190 [-0.655,1.036]. An unlisted predator is unknown. Odonate larval records and identifiable aquatic vegetation structure are missing in these joined archives. Unlabelled measurement values were not assigned plant meanings.

## USGS
Atchafalaya, Louisiana, 64 sites, 230 site-years and 13 exactly matched frog species. Original USGS presence/absence data; one prior inside-site acoustic visit per analysed site-year. Strong is chorus index 2 or 3; comparison is 0 or 1. First later census had same-species tadpoles in 7/57 versus 40/2933 site-year-species records. All seven strong-call positives belonged to P. crucifer (5) or P. fouquettei (2). Species- and year-adjusted linear probability differences with day of year, its square and interval since calls; uncertainty clustered by site. Regularly flooded vegetation is the static mean of EarthEnv class 8 at valid cell centres within 3 km of the survey coordinates, not aquatic plant structure. Seven first follow-up site-years had Ambystoma opacum larvae; only two corresponding frog species records were positive. No larval odonate survey in these tables.

## Replay
Requires Python 3, numpy, pandas, scipy, statsmodels and rasterio. Save the two original INBO DwC archives next to the scripts as adult.zip and larva.zip, then run inbo_followup.py, inbo_adjusted.py and inbo_newt.py. The first script verifies both publisher file digests. Local replay inputs remain in work/call_followup. For the USGS analysis download the original CSV files from the repository source artifact into a usgs/ subfolder, retaining filenames. Place EarthEnv class 8 in ../earthenv_cache/earthenv_8.tif relative to the script. Run usgs_followup.py. Three original USGS hashes are asserted. Aggregate-only outputs omit site identifiers.
