# Survey-year Annual NLCD wetland composition and strong chorus associations

All 4,248 frozen NAAMP listening sites, 432 routes, 26,489 surveyed site-years and 63,180 completed visits (36,554 strong). The original site visit counts and strong-call fractions were reproduced. Every study-year pixel selected inside the discs had a valid land-cover code.

For each year from 2001 to 2015 we used the corresponding USGS Annual NLCD Collection 1.1 national land-cover raster, 30 m native grid. The extraction counts centres in WGS84 geodesic discs of radius 3 km (360-vertex polygon). Class 95 is emergent herbaceous wetlands and 90 woody wetlands. For every site we averaged its annual valid-area fractions weighted by completed visits to match the period represented by the site-level strong-chorus frequency. This is a cross-site analysis with year-matched landscape exposure; it does not estimate the effect of temporal land-cover changes.

The uniform background is primary. We used six odonate and five pond-breeding caudate SDMs refitted without the flooded-vegetation predictor, retaining original climate and open water inputs. In each joint linear model the standardized rank of strong-chorus frequency was regressed on standardized suitability ranks O and S, both centred wetland predictors H and W, O×S, O×H, O×W, S×H, S×W, O×S×H and O×S×W. O×H minus O×W compares moderation at mean ranked S. Raw cover compares an equal area increment for both classes; standardized rank compares relative positions within each wetland distribution on the scale of the original analysis. Paired percentile intervals resample 432 routes with replacement 1,000 times and recompute ranks on each draw. Within-route fits subtract route means from all design columns and the response. Target-group results are sensitivity analyses.

Under the primary uniform background, at a common increase of ten percentage points of the buffer area, whole-region interaction coefficients were −0.384 (herbaceous) and −0.056 (woody). The paired difference was −0.328 [−0.544, −0.025]. The same difference within routes was −0.151 [−0.332, +0.043]. Only 108 sites had at least 10% herbaceous wetland, compared with 1,477 for woody wetland. Median covers were 0.113% and 5.351%, respectively. On the standardized rank scale the regional coefficients were −0.080 and −0.087, difference +0.007 [−0.098,+0.125]. Thus equal-area moderation was steeper for emergent herbaceous wetlands regionally under the primary background, but relative landscape gradients showed negative moderation for both classes and no clear difference. The equal-area contrast did not replicate under target-group backgrounds. Wetland cover measures neither vegetation structure within a target pond nor larval predator density.

## Reproduction

- Annual source URLs, downloaded archive hashes and extraction counts: raster_sources.json. The published source is [USGS Annual NLCD](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/annual-nlcd-data-access).
- For a full rebuild, run research/scripts/annual_nlcd_wetland_extract.py YEAR TEMP for each of the 15 years using the same TEMP directory, then research/scripts/annual_nlcd_wetland_compare.py TEMP OUTPUT. The archived survey_year_wetlands.csv.gz may instead be split by year into TEMP/YEAR.csv to replay the comparisons without raster downloads.
- survey_year_wetlands.csv.gz contains annual pixel counts and visit denominators. site_wetlands.csv.gz contains visit-weighted site cover. coefficients.csv includes all specifications, both direct contrasts and conditional odonate slopes at the quartiles of each wetland type with the other at its median and caudate suitability at its mean rank.
- Original 15-year extraction [workflow run](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs/actions/runs/42955544070).
