# Fixed calibration design (before fitting)

Source: Kaczmarski et al. 2025, DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-11706-3, official supplement Dataset1. Units: 44 pond-date observations, two ponds, 2023 season. Primary predictor log1p automatically detected calls in second hour after sunset; sensitivity whole-night calls. Outcome log1p visually counted adult males. Neither is larval density or NAAMP calling index.

No model search. Report within-pond Spearman correlations, pooled correlation as descriptive, and fixed pond plus linear day-of-season OLS with versus without the call metric. Use 999 date-cluster bootstrap draws for the added call slope; insufficient rank draws reported. Leave each calendar date out jointly across ponds for prediction, baseline pond + date and added call predictor. Also leave each pond out: train a shared date trend with or without calls, predict other pond without a fabricated pond intercept. Compare squared error on log1p count scale. Two ponds do not support a population-level external-transfer claim. Do not interpret snapshot visual counts as detection-corrected abundance or seasonal survival. Full sound recordings are on request; published aggregate metrics suffice for this audit. Keep all outcomes, regardless of sign.
