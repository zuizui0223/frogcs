# Matched within-season test of frog-density-mediated odonate hazard

Reproduce with `python research/scripts/matched_seasonal_hazard.py` and `python research/scripts/figure_matched_seasonal_hazard.py`. Inputs are all eight archived original 20k shards. We kept each draw's structural flags, attack scale and size/behavior settings. We replaced its calendar and held entry densities fixed across interventions, eliminating inherited starting-density differences. Recruitment and conversion do not operate in this one-season assay. The archived two-sign classification is a grouping variable, not a new outcome of the intervention.

Each draw received all 48 combinations of frog entry density (1, 2 model units), caudate arrival (day 0, 40), caudate aquatic duration (40, 60 days), vegetation (0, 1) and three caudate arms (absent, present with attacks on frogs disabled, present with attacks enabled). Odonates enter on day 20 at density 2 for 60 days plus the original vegetation-dependent emergence delay where enabled. Caudates enter at density 2 if present. Frogs enter on day 20 and complete their aquatic stage on day 65. Water persists throughout. The frozen kernel advances at 0.5 days; a passive observer reads start-of-step states without changing dynamics. Mass balance, integrated hazards, equal aquatic predator trajectories in the two caudate-present arms, and zero feeding-mediated hazard shifts in the density-independent runs are checked.

- daily.csv.gz gives daily start-of-step arithmetic means for each stratum, calendar, density, vegetation and arm, over days 20 through 64. Cumulative kills are in model density units, hazard is an instantaneous per-frog rate per model day. No pre-arrival or post-metamorphosis zero hazard is imputed. Day 65 seasonal endpoints include the final half-day.
- season.csv reports means of paired differences. Feeding = feeding on minus feeding off (identical odonate trajectories); net = feeding on minus absent (includes predator interactions). Integrated hazard is the time integral of the cause-specific per-frog rate, not a probability. Actual cumulative kills include competition between causes of death. Fractions count individual draws using a 1e-10 sign tolerance.
- calendar_contrasts.csv contrasts the same feeding/net effects after varying only arrival or duration. Long minus short is identically zero for late caudates because frogs finish before either removal time.
- feeding_timeseries.png shows feeding-on minus feeding-off for the 747 originally joint-sign draws with a density-dependent response, initial frogs = 1; all groups are in the tables. Late short/long traces coincide.

With density-dependent odonate feeding, the model uses a per-frog hazard multiplier 2/(1+F) in bare habitat and 2F/(1+F²) in vegetation. At starting F=1, reducing F therefore increases the former and decreases the latter. At F>1 the vegetated response can transiently increase as F falls towards one. This response shape is assumed. Temporal magnitude, changes in actual kills, and the net comparison with caudates absent are simulated outcomes. The integrated hazard shifts have the same average directions among the 5,997 density-response draws without the original joint signs. The starting-density-controlled within-season pathway does not identify the cause of the long-term interaction or of calling patterns in the field.
