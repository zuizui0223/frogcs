# Species life history and within-route calling associations

breeding_traits_44.csv retains all 44 individually named NAAMP taxa. The hydroperiod column uses USGS AmphiTraits: Flexible includes use across water regimes, not a requirement for short-lived water. A separate regional column records the nine overlapping species in the comparative Texas study. Oviposition position and duration are literature descriptions with separate URLs. Days are approximate (30 d/month and 365 d/year); single values are reported examples, not exact species constants. Values reflect the selected sources, not bounds over the full range. The table flags egg-to-metamorphosis, onset-only and ambiguous starting points. Development varies with locality and environment.

species_timing_trait_contrasts.py takes the pinned USGS Counts.csv and Stops.csv directory as an argument. The 22-species panel retains frogs with strong calls on at least 25 routes. Each correlation uses all surveyed sites in routes with any focal call. Within each route, centered ranks describe the species' strong-call frequency versus each uniform-background vegetation-omitted guild SDM. The chorus sensitivity additionally residualizes both variables against the site fraction of visits with any other strong label; unresolved complex labels can include the focal species. This all-season index measures the surrounding acoustic community, not competition, tadpole counts, or co-detection restricted to the focal species' breeding window.

Calendar matching uses differences in representative calling days across each species' own sampled geography. The second clock centers calls on the median visit day at the same site and year. Contrasts average paired differences in species correlations for thresholds of 7, 14, and 21 days; each pair is used once per contrast. Species are reused across pairs. The outputs report descriptive differences and deletion of each species, not independent-pair p-values. They do not propagate site-level estimation or trait uncertainty and do not control for phylogeny or enforce shared sites. Surface_spawn means floating eggs or a surface film were explicitly reported, including mixed placements; the alternative broad coding also includes mentions of distance from the surface. Duration contrasts compare geometric midpoints or nonoverlapping reported ranges, excluding different developmental endpoint definitions. The chorus adjustment is a sensitivity check: predators and habitat can affect other frog species, so it can remove part of an indirect pathway. Neither model estimates competition or causal risk.

At a seven-day gap the permanent-water contrasts have no within-genus pairs. The surface-spawn contrast for caudates remains positive within the same hydroperiod class (12 pairs) and within genus (5 pairs), but those small sets do not separate oviposition, geographic distribution and shared ancestry. The simulation represents one frog cohort and saturating recruitment; this descriptive analysis does not fit a multi-frog competition model.
## Shared-site and local-calendar comparison

Run species_timing_trait_contrasts.py and then shared_site_surface_spawn.py with the pinned NAAMP Counts.csv and Stops.csv directory. Shared-site files keep all hydroperiod-matched contrasts between opposite surface-spawn codes in the previous 22-species analysis panel. Local calendars were recomputed rather than requiring the previous across-range seven-day screen. Within each site-year where both species were heard (any call index), the absolute difference between their median observed calling days had to be at most seven days. Each species' strong-call site frequency uses the same completed visits across all eligible years, including visits when neither reached index 2. The common chorus covariate uses visits where any of the other 42 named taxa reached index 2, leaving both focal species and all ambiguous complex labels out. Each correlation uses within-route residualized ranks on the same sites; the partial correlation additionally residualizes both ranked variables against the ranked chorus frequency after route centering. Positive differences favor the species documented to lay floating eggs or a surface film.

Primary support is 25 routes with at least two eligible sites each. The interval is a percentile interval over 1,000 synchronized route resamples, recomputing ranks, correlations and equal-pair means, conditional on the observed species, eligible calendars and pairs. It does not treat species pairs as independent. Alternate windows, use of all years at the matched sites and requiring two distinct call dates for each species are tabulated. Sparse timing observations and selection on both species being heard limit inference. A listening stop can include multiple water bodies. Matching the species-level hydroperiod category and exact site-year does not establish hydroperiod of each breeding pond.
