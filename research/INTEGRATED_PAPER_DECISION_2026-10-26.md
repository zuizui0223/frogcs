## One paper — one discriminating scientific question

**Does a wetland's environmentally generated breeding opportunity become a taxon-selective and stage-structured reproductive response because the community retains past aquatic cohorts and predator/prey developmental timing?**

The motivating pattern of RC6 is *rain-associated non-uniform spatial configuration of recorded adult calling*, not measured population-wide same-instant synchrony, spawning, egg survival or aquatic interactions. NAAMP did **not** measure event-level pond water depth, larval stages or local fish/odonate predators; the RC6 excess remains after the weather and historical-use factors actually represented, not after a measured local-pond hydrology adjustment. Its 0.2969 concentration residual **cannot be allocated to larval predation by arithmetic subtraction or analogy**.

## Proposed final scientific structure (subject to evidence gates)

**I — Phenomenon (locked RC6).** 4,236 matched NAAMP wetter/drier pairs; strictly prior-history 2,916 pairs; observed C=1.6503 versus main matched null C=1.3535 (conditional residual +0.2969, MC P=.000999); recurrent species-specific strong-chorus places beta +0.1511. Interpret as taxon-selective, within-route multi-stop spatial structure in sequential acoustic surveys, not instantaneous synchrony. Keep historical/post-hoc nature and all negative controls.

**II — Independent stage-structure discrimination.** On genuinely measured repeated ponds compare (1) physical hydroperiod/vegetation and sampling effort; (2) larval predator/competitor stage density, size, overlap and adult breeding pulses; (3) stage-by-hydroperiod interaction, with prey egg/hatch/larval outcome in the same aquatic unit. Distinguish (a) frog acoustic/egg timing, (b) larval counts conditional on detection, (c) larval survival/individual recruitment, and (d) direct kills. Do not conflate stages; per-pond coverage and temporal order are the eligibility gate. Main uncertainty is existing public data may not contain exposure and endpoint together.

**III — Independent geographic generality and mechanistic model.** Event-based GBIF/EDI observational datasets screen same-site phenology and recorded stages; presence overlap does not document attacks or actual phenophase synchrony. Fit seasonal three-guild stage-structured model only if sufficient natural-stage exposure and vital-rate data exist to constrain parameters; compare habitat-only and interaction models out of blocked ponds/years. Current synthetic models illustrate possible outcomes and parameter sensitivity, **not verified natural coexistence**. Do not use identical published prey edges as independent confirmation of the same model.

## What would turn a proposed connection into a result?

- **G0**: source bytes + licence/version checks, stable `pond_id`, dates, taxon-stage and sampled-zero/effort definitions verified without outcomes.
- **G1**: direct measured adult calling/egg or animal-stage linkage at same pond, with temporal precedence and out-of-pond/year prediction compared to a strong hydrology/site model. This checks transition coupling, not predation per se.
- **G2**: measured large/overwintered predator-stage density and independently estimated prey egg/larval outcome at same pond plus physical hydroperiod. Demonstrate a non-additive stage/overlap/hydroperiod association with uncertainty and stable comparator, or a causal predation effect via intervention.
- **G3**: genuinely independent external evidence or validated parameter constraints for future/time-staggered community model; assess whether results answer ONE question and whether RC6 and stage module can coexist without implying direct statistical mediation unsupported by their data.

**H1A (NPS adult-acoustic proxy)** is a locked **failed directional suppression prediction**, beta +0.0143 (95% station-cluster approximate interval −0.0691,+0.0977); the reverse is positive +0.1181, not a demonstrated direction-specific trophic reversal. No species/lag/window retuning to rescue the H1A sign.

**H1B (overwintered larva → eggs/survival), H3 (measured dry-down resets larval community) remain untested** unless qualified sources actually have those measurements. A cross-sectional site-presence association or annual maximum recording is not enough.
