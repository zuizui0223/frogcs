## 1. Scientific object and where the idea came from

**Primary paper subject:** spatial variation in observed **frog acoustic site use** in relation to the environmentally modeled suitability of **Anura, Odonata, and Caudata**, and how **landscape vegetation, preceding-year satellite water, and strictly earlier acoustic use** contextualize these associations. Focus on **observed species occurrences, correlations and conditional heterogeneity**, not the success of predictive classifiers or a claim of a single causal mechanism.

A working question:

> **Does the association between frog calling and odonate niche suitability depend on pond-breeding salamander/newt suitability and wetland landscape context, and how does antecedent surface-water occurrence relate to repeatedly used calling locations?**

**RC6's correct place:** A **short historical motivation in the Introduction**. The source NAAMP survey's response magnitude alone did not summarize the distribution of chorusing across listening stops; this prompted a separate, broader community-level question about which species guilds and landscapes are associated with frequently used calling sites. The historical matched wetter/drier acoustic excess (+0.2969) can be quoted *once as a previous within-programme exploratory observation*, with method unit clearly attributed. **It is NOT the abstract's leading effect, the first main result, a flagship figure, or an independent replication of the present NAAMP-derived macroecological analysis.**

## 2. Main paper's evidence and claim ceiling

**Source-backed center** ([October 26 macro report](FROGCS_MACRO_ANALYSIS_FINAL_REPORT_2026-10-26.md), [full audit](RESEARCH_COMPLETION_AUDIT_2026-10-26.md)):

1. **Guild-niche pattern, actual results.** 4,248 repeatedly surveyed NAAMP listening locations, 432 route components. Adult strong-calling frequency has weak, consistently negative **whole-region** Spearman correlation with mean modelled **Odonata suitability** (uniform background rho −0.095, target-group background rho −0.086); within-route effects are much weaker. Modelled odonate **environmental suitability includes adult occurrence records** and is not an observed nymph density at any listening stop.
2. **Third-guild conditioning, main macro result.** In the **high** pond-breeding Caudata-suitability stratum, uniform-background Odonata–calling rho −0.284 versus +0.101 in the low stratum; high minus low −0.385 (95% route-bootstrap interval −0.509 to −0.240). Target-group background high −0.183 versus low −0.004; difference −0.179 (−0.328 to −0.029). These are exploratory **associations conditional on fitted SDMs**. Correlation strength depends on geographic context, suitability background and strata. Low-group positive point estimates do not alone establish a sign reversal.
3. **Vegetation as context, not a demonstrated refuge mechanism.** EarthEnv's ~1 km static source raster averaged in 3-km neighborhoods raises vegetation-positive area coverage from 46 point-locations to 506/4,248 nearby locations. Whole-region vegetation interaction coefficients are Odonata×vegetation negative in both background approaches, Caudata×vegetation positive in sign; the **three-guild interaction** is +0.037 (0.008 to 0.068) only in uniform-background whole-region pond-breeding Caudata models, and is uncertain or attenuated in other backgrounds/within-route and 1-km comparisons. Plants' **emergence timing, stem density and aquatic shelter function** are not measured. The 3-km vegetation mean versus SD rank correlation 0.99984 is near identity and not independent verification.
4. **Preceding surface water rather than true hydroperiod.** For 3,907 fixed pairs with preceding-year 3-km water-detected pixels, the strong-chorus recording side has satellite conditional **water-detection frequency** −1.481 percentage points (95% interval −2.601 to −0.294) relative to silent recording side. The difference in satellite **ever-water area fraction** is not comparably clear. Annual GLAD water frequency is NOT field pond inundation days; zero-water-pixel pairs must stay ineligible for the conditional statistic, not be imputed zeros.
5. **Recurring site association.** In all 4,040 within-run strong-vs-silent fixed pairs, the strong recorded calling site has strictly prior strong-chorus site-use **+27.68 percentage points** (95% route-component interval +25.07 to +30.17). The 388 connected original-route components (shared physical site links) are the cluster unit, not 4,040 independent ponds. This association also appears across low/medium/high preceding-water-frequency strata but cannot distinguish true individual philopatry, acoustic detection differences or persistent habitat suitability.

**Source coverage:** 36,627 obtained GBIF records across 30 ecology-evidence-selected species candidates (17 Anura, 7 Caudata, 6 Odonata); 28 species modeled (15 Anura, 7 Caudata, 6 Odonata); 2 with inadequate ≥30 independent 10-min source-cell coverage left NOT MODELED, never assigned predicted absence. From a separate 464,941 original survey-event×taxon source table, use *method-scoped detection* and covariate overlap only as an independently described supporting observational axis; NAAMP positive-only calls, NPS actual 0 scores, Hungary measured plant covers and INBO field statuses have **different detection denominators**.

**Paper storyline, in this order:** (I) data/source eligibility & geographic model; (II) guild correlations and Caudata moderation **main result**; (III) vegetation interactions & scale/background sensitivities; (IV) preceding-year water and matched within-run/historical contrast; (V) community-assembly explanations **as alternative hypotheses**, limitations, and realistic future pond-scale verification. NO standalone Results section that retells the entire RC6 null/simulation history.
