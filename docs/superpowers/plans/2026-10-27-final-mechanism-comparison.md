# Final mechanism comparison — fixed design, 2026-10-27

Objective: rank explanatory candidates for the three macro associations without treating synthetic model agreement as field causal identification.

Scope: finite existing mechanism catalog, not every ecological possibility. Keep documented S→F and D→F edges; cross reciprocal S→D/D→S edges (4), four refuge targets (16), emergence survival/timing (4), independent D feeding support on F/S (4), postmetamorphic F→emerging-D reversal (2), no behavior / D suppresses S→F / D-induced F refuge (3), simple vs litter functional response (2): 12,288 logical combinations. Litter response omits stacked D→F exponential refuge. Mechanistically identical cases will be marked/deduplicated. Unsupported suppression of S feeding on D is not eligible as a preferred mechanism; prior evidence-constrained comparisons provide its negative-reference history. Egg wrapping, explicit age/size distributions, toxicity, habitat choice, abiotic confounding and measurement processes are not implemented and remain alternatives outside this catalog. No claim to exhaust nature.

Screen: matched 18 cells (V 0/1; S,D supply .25/1/2) at three prespecified stage contexts: F20/duration45; (S40,D20,durations40,40), (S40,D20,40,60), (S0,D20,60,60). Attack 3, rho .5. Coarse dt .5, 40 seasons, final 10 summarized. Save all outcomes. Coarse rejection is not proof of impossibility. Mandatory anchors retained regardless of screen outcome.

Readouts: retain prior conditional DV<0,SV>0,SD<0 on F index, log F index, log F survival and -predation hazard. Also fit the same seven rank-standardized main/interaction terms as macro to the 18 synthetic cells, testing O:S<0,O:V<0,S:V>0. Their magnitudes are descriptive pattern distances only: synthetic cell distribution and observables are not the field sampling process; no likelihood, AIC, posterior or empirical confidence. Track minimum F and S survival, survivor-pool compression, and convergence. A block is floor-avoiding when all 18 F and S seasonal survival fractions >=.05; this threshold is a diagnostic, not a biological law.

Candidate expansion: deterministically retain top candidates by screen consistency, with structural diversity and mandatory anchor models (F-only refuge, S-only refuge, both, all refuge, emergence support/timing alternatives, cue-only and behavioral-refuge alternatives, litter alternative). At most 20 candidates. All face the same fresh, seeded 24 nuisance blocks varying arrivals, durations, attack .5/1.5/3, rho .25/.5/.75, conversion 2/4/8, refuge order and strength. This is parameter robustness, not independent empirical validation. Full dt .25,80 seasons,last20. Joint success must cover conditional hazard directions and macro-form rank directions. Report screen-only success separately.

Selection: prioritize joint matches that occur without low F/S survival; then shared-block stability and numerical stability. Evidence tiers and active-assumption counts are separate columns, not hidden weights. If no candidate dominates, name a tied/Pareto set. If none meets the stricter requirements, say so and reject a claim of a uniquely supported mechanism. Examine relative effect size and hazard-path decomposition in finalists; do not invent field calibration. Repeat selected successful/failure boundary blocks at dt .125 and160 seasons, and remove directed edges to verify claimed mechanisms. Preserve old macro data/text.

Implementation: a streaming wrapper reuses the unchanged seasonal simulator and annual recurrence; tests compare summaries against simulate_years. Scripts own design generation, screen, validation, selection and reproducible outputs. Source notes reuse the primary evidence audit and distinguish stage/predator extrapolations.
