## Ecological result

The strongest defensible interpretation is **structured reactivation of a latent local chorus network**.

Rainfall-associated change is not mainly an increase in intensity among species × stop cells that were already active. In the full 4,236-pair NAAMP sample, 65.3% of the CallingIndex rain slope is assigned to 0→positive activation, and 87.1% of that activation slope is assigned to direct 0→CallingIndex 2–3 transitions. Even 0→CallingIndex 3 alone is positive (β = 0.441, 95% CI 0.114–0.768). CallingIndex 3 is the NAAMP full-chorus state, in which calls are continuously overlapping and individuals cannot be distinguished.

These results persist when observer identity and physical listening-site identity are simultaneously held constant (3,115 pairs): 0→CI3 β = 0.514 (0.101–0.927). They also persist after adjustment for recorded hearing impairment, major-noise timeout and wind, and after excluding the design explanation that the result is only a consequence of surveys being scheduled within three days of rain.

## The spatial effect is depth, not simply more recruited species

The number of route-new species does not show the unusual null departure. The excess appears after a recruited species has already entered the route:

- second-stop incidence β = 0.132, not exceptional under either primary null;
- third-and-later-stop incidence β = 0.473, above both null 95% ranges (both P = 0.000999);
- fourth-and-later-stop incidence β = 0.363, also above both null ranges.

The third-and-later-stop coefficient is almost entirely carried by species that reach substantial chorus intensity: CI2/3 components explain 97.3% of the coefficient in the full sample and 95.4% in the same-observer + same-physical-stop subset. CI1-only spatial deepening is not supported.

Thus rainfall-associated recruitment is better described as **spatial deepening of substantial chorus participation** than as simple addition of isolated new detections.

## Local acoustic memory

Most of the apparent “new” wet-route incidence is historically recurrent.

Among physically stable pairs:

- 98.1% of the rain coefficient for route-new incidence is assigned to species already recorded elsewhere in the same route-season history;
- in the strictly prior-only sensitivity, the recurrent share is 98.9%;
- the prior-only one-off/no-prior component is approximately zero (β = 0.009, 95% CI −0.141–0.160).

Full-chorus reactivation is similarly recurrent. In leave-pair-out history, 98.1% of the new-CI3 rain coefficient is recurrent and 90.4% is recurrent at the same physical SiteID. With strictly prior history, 82.6% is same-site recurrent.

Conditional on the same focal pair and the same route-new species, a SiteID where that species had previously reached CI2/3 is much more likely to host a later wet-survey CI2/3 state (within-pair × species β = 0.151, 95% CI 0.129–0.173). The same result holds for CI3 alone (β = 0.0778, 0.0619–0.0937) and in same-observer pairs (β = 0.159, 0.134–0.183).

Opportunity-normalized prior-only analyses also show that increasing rain contrast selectively favors historically recurrent full-chorus candidate cells (β = 0.0245, 0.0070–0.0420; same-observer β = 0.0307, 0.0133–0.0480).

This supports a **temporal gate × stable local spatial template** interpretation: rain is associated with the reappearance of chorus activity at historically favored species × SiteID combinations.

## Memory persistence

The formal ≥4-year memory test did not meet its prefixed route-count gate (136 informative routes vs required 200), so no formal durable-memory classification is made. The prespecified diagnostics nevertheless point in the expected direction:

- old-memory β = 0.101 (0.068–0.133);
- recent-memory β = 0.173 (0.115–0.231);
- within-memory lag effect, β for log1p(years since last strong chorus) = −0.135 (−0.189 to −0.081).

The safe interpretation is therefore **multi-year local chorus recurrence with decay**, not proof of continuous occupancy or individual fidelity.

## What did not explain the mechanism

The following simple explanations were tested and were not sufficient:

- uniform activation;
- strong dry-state persistence;
- route-cross-fit species-specific rainfall shifts;
- strictly prior species × SiteID local-memory probabilities;
- combined cross-fit species shifts + prior local memory + dry persistence;
- a final held-out rain × local-memory gate;
- a simple social-facilitation proxy based on other species already calling at the dry stop;
- a sharply immediate 0–1-day response window;
- NWI short-vs-persistent hydroperiod for the strong-chorus endpoint;
- NWI Palustrine vs Riverine/Lacustrine wetland system for the strong-chorus endpoint;
- temperature- or seasonal-window multicue interactions;
- observer turnover, stop relocation, and measured hearing/noise/wind differences.

The final held-out null was still rejected at P = 0.000999. In the 2,916-pair strictly-prior subset, observed boundary crossing was 0.921 versus a final-null mean of 0.814; observed taxonomic-deepening share was 0.399 versus 0.316; within-core share was 0.079 versus 0.186.

## Generality

Within NAAMP, the process is not driven by one species or one state:

- 28 of 57 species have positive strong-activation contributions;
- top species contributes 13.9% of positive mass; top five 49.2%; HHI = 0.074;
- removing any single species leaves the total coefficient positive;
- leaving out each state in turn leaves the strong-activation coefficient positive with a positive 95% CI.

But state-specific effects are heterogeneous: only 13/19 estimable states have positive point estimates and 6/19 have entirely positive CIs; the mixed-model population slope is 1.65 with state-slope SD 3.46.

Direct cross-continental replication of the full species × site allocation is unavailable. Existing external datasets either lack complete effort-zero reconstruction or lack the multi-site matrix structure required. The available AnuraSet tests do not reproduce the prefixed threshold-dominance rule. **Universality is therefore not established.**

## Stopping conclusion

The prefixed mechanism decision tree required stopping after rejection of the final held-out rain × local-memory gating null. No additional trait fishing, same-data site-specific rainfall coefficients, or relaxed gates are authorized.

The final exploratory conclusion is:

> **Rainfall-associated acoustic expansion predominantly reactivates a historically recurrent, site-structured chorus network. Previously silent species × site cells often return directly at overlapping or full-chorus intensity, and recruited species deepen their spatial participation beyond the second occupied stop. Yet species sensitivity, prior local memory, dry persistence and a held-out rain × memory gate still cannot generate the full observed taxonomic-versus-spatial allocation. The public observational data therefore identify structured latent-network reactivation, but not a sufficient unique lower-level mechanism.**
