# Known causal biotic hierarchy versus what frogcs has actually tested — 2026-10-22

## Three experiment systems, not one verified mediation chain

1. **Macrophyte manipulation and adult amphibian breeding-site selection**: Šigutová et al. 2021 (doi:10.1038/s41598-021-98632-2; data doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.16627561.v2). Published fully randomized 2 fish × 3 aquatic vegetation structures, 24 tanks in four blocks. Our original data reanalysis finds fishless submerged/floating macrophytes elevate `Bufotes viridis` male habitat selection by **45.86 percentage points** relative to no plants. Exact within-block randomized plant-label P **0.00617284**, BH q among four pre-fixed toad comparisons **0.0246914**, **4/4 original blocks positive**; source experimental effect on habitat choice, not larval tadpole fate. Fish×plant interaction trend **−44.67 pp**, four-block CI −86.56 to −2.79, but **BH q 0.1706**, so not a confirmed moderator.
2. **Fish → dragonfly predator density → bullfrog tadpole survival**: Adams, Pearl & Bury 2003, *Ecology Letters* doi:10.1046/j.1461-0248.2003.00435.x, [USGS public record](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/indirect-facilitation-anuran-invasion-non-native-fishes). In an independent replicated field experiment, native dragonfly larvae killed bullfrog tadpoles unless introduced non-native sunfish were present to reduce dragonfly predators. This is **published experimental evidence of a fish-mediated indirect biotic effect in a different pond system**, not data accessed/reanalysed in frogcs and **not an aquatic plant manipulation**. It does not validate a causal effect on *calling adult* bullfrogs or greenfrogs observed in NAAMP.
3. **Competition×predation×prey species experimental response heterogeneity**: Arribas et al. 2018, *Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution* doi:10.3389/fevo.2018.00028, [open article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/ecology-and-evolution/articles/10.3389/fevo.2018.00028/full). **80 mesocosms in 8 spatial blocks**, randomized presence/absence of tadpoles of `Agalychnis callidryas` crossed with **no predator, caged dragonfly, free dragonfly, caged water bug or free water bug**; four other core amphibian larvae species were observed for growth/survival and trophic isotope signatures. Published significant **competition × predation × prey species** heterogeneity for tadpole final body size (**χ²=30.27, P=0.00019**) and δ15N (**χ²=22.65, P=0.031**). These are actual factorial treatment-by-species responses, although the focal species identity is not an independently randomized third biotic species intervention and this study has **no manipulated macrophyte structural factor**. We did not access individual raw observations, so these numbers are publisher results, **not a new frogcs model run**.

## Combined ecological inference boundary

These three studies jointly show that the ingredients are **biologically realistic**:
- plants can change breeding habitat selection;
- fish can change the risk imposed by predatory macroinvertebrates and affect bullfrog larvae;
- frog competition and aquatic predator regimes can produce different effects among prey species.

**They do not show that all links operate in the same wetlands, same frog species/life stages, or that aquatic vegetation produces the NAAMP adult frog calling network via fish or odonates.** Concatenating individual experimental effects across incompatible experiments is **NOT** identifiable causal mediation. NAAMP itself remains an exploratory observational network investigation.

A genuinely discriminating experiment must assign plant structure, fish, predatory odonate and a competitor frog presence within the **same independently randomized replicated system** while tracking focal frog response, odonate density and frog survival/behaviour over time (see protocol `exploration/FROG_PLANT_PREDATOR_TRUE_HIGHER_ORDER_EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL_V0_1.md`). Causal treatment interaction and mediator-specific indirect effect are **different estimands**; fish×insect statistical interaction alone is not natural mediated proportion.

## Evidence-status definitions

| Claim | Evidence level |
|---|---|
| Experimental fishless submerged/floating macrophyte effect on B. viridis male habitat selection | **Supported in original 2021 experiment; reproduced from source in frogcs** |
| A general aquatic vegetation×fish interaction on adult toad choice | **Not established after four-test BH correction in our reanalysis** |
| Fish suppressing dragonfly larvae can facilitate bullfrog tadpole survival | **Supported in separate published 2003 experimental system, not frogcs source analysis** |
| Competition×predation effect differs by amphibian prey taxon | **Supported in separate published 2018 randomized experiment; published statistics only** |
| Simultaneous plant→fish→odonate→greenfrog adult-calling mediation in NAAMP | **Not measured or established** |
