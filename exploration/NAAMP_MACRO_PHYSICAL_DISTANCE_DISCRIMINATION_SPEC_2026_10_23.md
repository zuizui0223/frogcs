# Frozen exploratory extension: true geographic distance versus sequential stop-order interval in NAAMP frog community turnover

**2026-10-23, follow-up after reading the positive stop-index-gap effect and null mapped PEM boundary from the macro community turnover run. Therefore this is explicitly POST-HOC, not registered before the original outcome.** No literature search; real USGS NAAMP + original validated USFWS NWI coordinate sources only.

### Scientific test
The previous real-data full-taxon route-night model found increasing Sørensen acoustic assemblage dissimilarity with difference in stop numbers but could not distinguish physical separation from sequential survey timing. This design adds **haversine physical distance between the two independently verified coordinates of the physical stop SiteIDs**, **while retaining the absolute difference in ordinal stop number** as a nuisance. Ask whether the spatial distance term has its own nonzero association in the exact same population.

### Frozen estimator
- Same source, same NWI 250m mapped PEM, same 2001–2015 NAAMP standardized runs, same 10 stop survey membership. Load only original verified NWI `latitude,longitude` for the exact `State,RouteNumber,SiteID` keys; map a run/stop to a SiteID from source Stop records. Reject missing/conflicting geo, never silently fill from another route.
- Primary any call 1–3; secondary strong 2–3. Include **identical both-acoustically-active station pairs and 2 cross / 2 same PEM comparison gate** as preceding frozen turnover script; verify both **pair and route counts identical** before considering signs.
- Use Sørensen dissimilarity `1-2 shared/(rich_i+rich_j)`. Within-run demean response and six predictors: different mapped PEM class, abs stop ordinal gap /9, abs total NWI wetland cover difference (old ×10 scale), abs taxon richness difference, mean richness, **log(1+physical-km-distance)**. Route-clustered sandwich t(df=number routes−1) confidence interval.
- Frozen primary target: coefficient for `log1p_geographic_distance_km` **conditional on stop-order gap** (positive hypothesis: greater physical separation associated with more compositional differentiation). State uncertainty, estimated effect for 1km vs10km as `beta × (log(11)-log(2))`; distinguish association from causation/dispersal.
- Sensitivities: route A/B geographic folds, same-strata richness matched `|rich_i-rich_j|<=1`, route-equal weights, leave-one-state-out sign fraction. Flag high collinearity between log km and stop order, refuse claim if design nonidentifiable. Absolutely no alternate radius, 3km/5km distance cutoffs, elevation, state selection, taxon exclusions or sequential search for significant effect.
- *New* timing diagnostic: report official source `Stops.csv` header field names containing `time` or `minute` **only**. Unless explicit stop timestamp is actually present and validated, stop-order gap is merely a proxy for elapsed time and genuine time-vs-distance separation remains unproven.
- Opposite geographical folds must both have positive distance coefficients and overall route-clustered CI must exclude zero and ≥80% leave-one-state-out positive and richness-matched sign positive to label `exploratory_conditional_distance_association`. Otherwise `nonconfirmatory`.
- No station pair is an independent randomized replicate; minimum independent unit is whole route; repeated runs and repeated taxa nested within route; mapping does not create new presence observations. NWI 2001–2015 time mismatch persists.
- A positive result is an acoustic **spatial beta-diversity** signal, not individual movement, ecological competition, plant-caused interaction, or third-species-mediated dynamics. Frozen JAE RC6 remains unchanged.
