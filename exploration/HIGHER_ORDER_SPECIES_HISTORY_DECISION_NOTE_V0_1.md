# Higher-order coherence under species/history nulls — decision note v0.1

**Status:** fixed before endpoint readback.

## PASS rule

PASS only if the previously fixed higher-order conditional residual exceeds the upper 95% null-residual bound under BOTH:

1. route-cross-fit species-specific rainfall response on the full 4,236-pair sample; and
2. route-cross-fit species-specific rainfall response + strictly-prior species × physical-SiteID history + a=0.75 dry-state persistence on the exact 2,916-pair prior-history subset.

## If PASS

Authorized interpretation:

> The higher-order concentration of extra spatial participation within the same recruited taxa is not reproduced by transferable species-specific rainfall sensitivity, nor by its additive combination with strictly-prior local site propensity and dry-state persistence.

This strengthens the claim from "independent-cell activation is insufficient" to:

> **The wet-state route contains residual higher-order spatial dependence beyond the tested first-order species and site-history structure.**

Do not claim:
- an unknown causal factor is proven;
- literal simultaneity or synchrony;
- movement among sites;
- social facilitation;
- hydrological connectivity;
- a universal anuran mechanism.

## If FAIL under either null

The manuscript must not say higher-order dependence persists beyond species response + site history.

Allowed fallback:
- higher-order coherence exists relative to uniform/persistence nulls;
- it is robust to observer and SiteID controls;
- but the stronger species/history process is sufficient for that endpoint.

No alternative statistic, subset, cross-fit rule, null family or tail will be tried after result readback.
