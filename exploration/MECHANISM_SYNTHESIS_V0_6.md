# Mechanism synthesis v0.6

**Status:** exploratory; the frozen RC11 submission remains authoritative and is not modified by this programme.

## Ecological interpretation

The current evidence is most consistent with a **temporal gate × local acoustic template** model.

Recent rain is associated with activation of species × stop cells that were silent in the drier survey. This is not mainly a small increase in calling intensity among cells already active: about 65% of the CallingIndex rainfall slope is contributed by 0→positive activation, and about 87% of that activation slope is contributed by cells entering CallingIndex 2–3. Direct 0→3 full-chorus transitions are themselves rainfall-associated and persist when observer identity and all ten physical SiteIDs are held constant.

The spatial placement of reactivation is not random. Route-new species are disproportionately reactivated at physical SiteIDs where the same species had called in earlier years. Using only monitoring years strictly before the focal pair, essentially all of the rainfall slope in strong new chorus activity is assigned to historically known route cells; about 84% is assigned to the same SiteID and about 70% to SiteIDs where the species had previously reached CallingIndex 2–3. The no-prior-record component has no positive rainfall slope.

An opportunity-normalized comparison reaches the same conclusion: as rainfall contrast increases, CI3 activation rises more strongly among prior same-SiteID candidate cells than among candidate cells known only elsewhere on the same route. This remains true in same-observer pairs. Thus local chorus history contributes information beyond species identity, focal pair identity, route-level commonness, and the number of available candidate cells.

## Spatial depth

The excess spatial footprint is not produced primarily by more route-new species or by more species merely reaching a second stop. It is concentrated at the **third and later occupied stops**. The third-plus-stop incidence coefficient is about 0.473, versus null upper bounds near 0.36 (uniform) and 0.28 (persistence). About 97% of this third-plus coefficient is carried by route-new species that reach CallingIndex 2–3, with the CI3-present component alone contributing about 0.363.

The additional stops are route-topologically coherent: adjacent StopNumber recruitment is excessive even after conditioning on total extra-stop spread and remains so in the same-observer + physical-stop subset. However, Euclidean MPD/MST compactness conditional on occupied-stop number is not supported. The defensible description is therefore **deep, route-coherent reactivation**, not a proven Euclidean wetland cluster.

## What the data do not identify

The analysis does not identify the proximal biological trigger. Coarse NWI hydroperiod and Palustrine-vs-Riverine/Lacustrine context do not explain the robust CI2–3 activation. A simple social-cue proxy, a sharply immediate rain window, and prefixed temperature/season interaction families also fail. State-specific effects are heterogeneous even though leave-one-state-out results are robust.

Accordingly, hydration, local inundation, endocrine readiness, prey/activity changes, and other weather-linked mechanisms remain candidates rather than identified mediators.

## Generality

Within NAAMP, the phenomenon is taxonomically diffuse and geographically robust to removal of any one state, observer turnover, physical-stop relocation, protocol targeting and measured detection conditions. State-specific slopes are nevertheless heterogeneous. Two exploratory Neotropical AnuraSet site tests do not reproduce the prefixed threshold-dominance rule, and no independent effort-complete multisite acoustic matrix suitable for a direct spatial replication has yet been identified.

The supported scope is therefore **broad within the sampled NAAMP system, not universal**.

## Novelty target

Rainfall effects on frog calling and breeding-site/calling-station fidelity are individually established in the literature. The apparent gap addressed here is their community-level conjunction: **weather-driven community expansion is routed through persistent species-by-site chorus history**. Rain supplies the temporal opportunity; prior local chorus use predicts which dormant acoustic cells become expressed.

## Stopping rule

Do not add further mechanism analyses unless they use an independent external dataset or a genuinely new measured proximal variable. Further re-partitioning of the same NAAMP outcomes risks post hoc mechanism fishing.
