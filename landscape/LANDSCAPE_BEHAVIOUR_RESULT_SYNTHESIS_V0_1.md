## Initial prediction and falsification

Initial frozen prediction:

> Multi-site activation may be more dispersed along route topology than expected after exact k and fixed q.

Result:

**Failed.**

Primary mean-pairwise-separation excess:
- observed **−0.2049**;
- null 95% **−0.0723 to 0.0702**;
- frozen upper-tail P = **1.0**.

Do not describe NAAMP as showing excess route-topological dispersion.

## Unexpected landscape result

The sign was consistently opposite.

Given the exact number of active stops k and the strongest fixed-q stop propensities, realised active stops were **more compact** than expected.

Post-readback lower-tail quantification:

| Metric | Excess | Null 95% | lower-tail P |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| mean pairwise stop separation | **−0.2049** | −0.0723 to 0.0702 | **0.00050** |
| topological span | **−0.2644** | −0.0860 to 0.0847 | **0.00050** |
| contiguous component count | **−0.1212** | −0.0290 to 0.0283 | **0.00050** |

Observed versus fixed-q exact-k expectation:

- mean pairwise separation: **3.455 vs 3.660 stop-number units**;
- span: **5.552 vs 5.817 stop-number units**;
- active components: **2.179 vs 2.300**.

## Scale dependence

Compactness occurs in both shallow and deep activity, but weakens as activation becomes spatially deeper.

Mean pairwise-separation excess:
- shallow k=2–3: **−0.2929**;
- deep k>=4: **−0.0987**.

Post-readback deep-minus-shallow contrast:
- observed **+0.1942**;
- null 95% **−0.1333 to 0.1355**;
- two-sided P = **0.0060**.

Interpretation:

> As activation broadens, the realised pattern becomes less locally compact, but even deep activation remains more compact than its exact-k fixed-q expectation.

This is consistent with **expansion without complete spatial homogenization**.

## Multi-scale ecological picture

The combined NAAMP evidence now suggests two spatial layers:

### Broad state
Residual dependence persists across far route positions.

### Local expression
Given k and q, realised active sites cluster more tightly along the route than expected.

Together:

> **A favourable-night taxon state may be route-spanning in readiness but locally clustered in realised expression.**

This reconciles:
- positive far-lag dependence;
- modest near > far dependence;
- exact-k compact active subsets;
- recurrent strong-site targeting.

## Physical-scale interpretation

NAAMP protocol places consecutive listening stops at least 0.5 miles apart; equidistant routes use exactly 0.5 miles.

Therefore stop-number geometry has a conservative route-path interpretation.

For k>=2 clusters:
- observed mean pairwise stop separation 3.455 implies at least **1.728 route miles (~2.78 km)** between active stop pairs on average under the 0.5-mile lower bound;
- observed mean span 5.552 implies at least **2.776 route miles (~4.47 km)** from first to last active stop on average.

These are conservative route-path lower bounds, not Euclidean distances.

## Revised landscape hypothesis

> **Environmental pulses may create spatially broad behavioural readiness while local habitat or site structure clusters where that behaviour is actually expressed.**

This is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated mechanism.

Candidate mechanisms for future independent tests:
- local hydrological covariance;
- clustered breeding habitat;
- local demographic availability;
- spatially autocorrelated habitat quality;
- social aggregation.
