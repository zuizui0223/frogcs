# NAAMP route-topology geometry interpretation v0.1

## Frozen result

Authority:
- `landscape/NAAMP_ROUTE_TOPOLOGY_GEOMETRY_CONTRACT_V0_1.json`
- `landscape/NAAMP_ROUTE_TOPOLOGY_GEOMETRY_RECEIPT_V0_1.json`

Coverage:
- 2,835 focal pairs;
- 428 routes;
- 1,693 dry-route-silent taxon × route-night clusters with wet k>=2;
- 45 taxa.

## Primary directional prediction

The frozen primary prediction was **positive topological dispersion** beyond exact k and fixed q.

This prediction failed:
- observed mean pairwise-separation excess = **−0.2049**;
- frozen null 95% interval = **−0.0723 to 0.0702**;
- pre-specified upper-tail P = **1.0**.

Therefore do **not** claim excess landscape dispersion.

## Unexpected sign reversal

All three frozen geometry summaries point in the opposite direction:

| Metric | Observed mean | Exact-k fixed-q expectation | Excess | Null 95% interval for excess |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| mean pairwise stop separation | 3.4555 | 3.6604 | −0.2049 | −0.0723 to 0.0702 |
| topological span | 5.5523 | 5.8166 | −0.2644 | −0.0860 to 0.0847 |
| contiguous component count | 2.1790 | 2.3001 | −0.1212 | −0.0290 to 0.0283 |

This was **not the pre-specified direction**. It is a post-readback hypothesis-generating observation.

## Ecological interpretation

The new landscape picture is not a dispersed route-wide network of active sites.

Instead:

> **Given the same activation depth k and the same first-order stop probabilities q, active stops tend to form a more compact route-topological configuration than expected.**

This is compatible with a multi-scale description:
- broad taxon-level favourable-night readiness can span a route;
- actual expression is locally filtered into compact groups of route positions;
- recurrent strong-chorus sites remain important within that non-uniform expression.

This is consistent with, but does not prove:
- local habitat clustering;
- shared local hydrology;
- local breeding aggregation;
- spatially autocorrelated suitability.

It does not identify:
- movement;
- acoustic propagation;
- social facilitation;
- hydrological connectivity;
- exact geographic clustering.

## Relationship to earlier NAAMP results

The new result helps reconcile two earlier facts:

1. far stop-number residual dependence remains positive, indicating route-spanning shared structure;
2. near dependence is modestly stronger than far dependence.

The topology result suggests that the realised active subset can be **route-spanning in dependence but locally compact in expression**.

That distinction was not resolved by activation depth k alone.

## Deep versus shallow descriptive pattern

The frozen descriptive strata show compactness in both groups.

Mean pairwise-separation excess:
- shallow k=2–3: **−0.2929**;
- deep k>=4: **−0.0987**.

Topological-span excess:
- shallow: **−0.3562**;
- deep: **−0.1535**.

Component-count excess:
- shallow: **−0.1213**;
- deep: **−0.1210**.

These are descriptive after readback. Do not infer a formal shallow/deep contrast without a separately frozen follow-up.

## Revised landscape hypothesis

> **Favourable-night chorus activation may be spatially broad at the taxon state level but locally clustered in its realised expression.**

This is stronger biologically than a simple “more sites” statement because it distinguishes:
- breadth of activation;
- geometry of realised sites.

This result cannot modify or upgrade RC6.

It belongs only to the separate landscape-behaviour line.
