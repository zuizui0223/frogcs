# Landscape exploration parked — 2026-10-05

## Status

**PARKED. No further NAAMP outcome-driven landscape analysis is authorized.**

This landscape line is separate from RC6 and does not modify the RC6 manuscript, endpoint, figures or claims.

## What happened

The prespecified route-topology prediction expected deep multi-site activation to be more dispersed/fragmented than the frozen exact-k, fixed-q comparator.

That directional prediction **failed**.

After readback, the observed route-order pattern was recognized as modestly more compact than expected. Subsequent compactness and recurrent-core analyses are therefore post-hoc with respect to the failed directional prediction.

Those results are retained for provenance and may generate future external predictions, but they are not upgraded into a new NAAMP discovery line.

## Authorized interpretation

- The original landscape directional prediction failed.
- Post-hoc route-order compactness is descriptive/exploratory.
- Its magnitude is modest and overlaps conceptually with the route-scale spatial structure already reported as defence/refinement around RC6.
- Historical recurrence and compactness must not be collapsed into one mechanism; the recurrent-core test did not pass its prespecified support rule.
- Exact geographic distance remains blocked by known coordinate transcription errors.
- No new StopNumber-derived topology metric, threshold, landscape mechanism or NAAMP outcome association may be added.

## Future use

The frozen external compactness prediction may be retained as an **optional prospective test in an independent dataset**, including WFTS if that external route is explicitly reopened and structurally eligible.

A future external result:
- does not alter RC6 retrospectively;
- must not be used to retune the NAAMP landscape metrics;
- should be treated as a separate study or revision-stage external test.

## Reopening rule

Reopen this line only if one of the following is true:

1. an independent external fixed-station dataset becomes available under a frozen design; or
2. independently validated site geometry is obtained without reference to frog outcomes.

Otherwise this line remains parked.

## Relation to RC6

RC6 remains the current paper.

The landscape line is not a submission gate, not missing evidence for RC6, and not a reason to delay submission.
