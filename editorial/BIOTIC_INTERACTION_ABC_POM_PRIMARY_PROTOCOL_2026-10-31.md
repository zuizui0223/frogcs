# frogcs — PRIMARY biological-interaction ABC–POM protocol (2026-10-31)

**Active scope: macroecology → direct/indirect trophic mechanisms, stage effects and vegetation mediation.** The previous idea to identify animal dispersal modes from NAAMP acoustic spatial statistics is PAUSED. No new field sampling is required for existing-data analysis. This is an inference protocol, not a claim that one trophic explanation has been identified.

## Research question

Why does the geographical Odonata suitability–frog strong-calling association change with pond-breeding Caudata suitability and landscape vegetation? Which combinations of direct predation, interactions among predators, ontogenetic timing and vegetation-specific refuge/handling can reproduce the QUANTITATIVE joint macro patterns?

Notation: F=frog/tadpole; S=caudate/salamander larva; D=odonate/dragonfly nymph; V=landscape periodically flooded vegetation. The observed guild predictors are occurrence-derived potential suitability, NOT larval densities at the sampled ponds.

## Competing biological interaction hypotheses

- **H0 shared environment:** no trophic edges. Climatic, wetland, geographical and survey patterns can generate correlation without actual predation. Mandatory comparator.
- **H1 direct predation:** S→F and/or D→F. Paired removal of each edge, holding source biological-rate vector and site observation mapping constant.
- **H2 indirect three-guild effect:** D→S→F potentially releases F from S predation, while D→F remains directly harmful; their net effect can change with the S pool. S→D supplies an additional direction when stages overlap. Contrast edge-disabled versus original network at SAME input rates.
- **H3 vegetation mediation:** V can protect F from S/D or protect S from D, and can change density dependence of D attacks. Preserve these as separate functional roles, not an undifferentiated plant benefit; do not count a refuge as active after its target predatory edge is removed.
- **H4 developmental stage and body size:** three source developmental contexts, feeding/handling modes, risk state and properly specified source stage windows. Completed F→emerging D reverse predation is exactly inactive in some timing contexts but ACTIVE in PR148's third context; only exact response-equivalent conditions can be canonicalized.

**Not an ABC model-selection axis:** animal dispersal regime, artificial cohort count q or local return count. Existing PR147–155 long-run outputs remain as theoretical stress/sensitivity results (Supplement), not a fitted mechanism for adult calling. PR157 spatial neighbour-lag research is parked.
