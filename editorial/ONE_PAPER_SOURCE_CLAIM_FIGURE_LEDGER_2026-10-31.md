## II. Claims for the new THREE main figures

### Figure 1 — Macroecological third-guild context (real data)

- NAAMP **4,248 acoustic site locations**, 28 SDM-modelled freshwater guild taxa (15 Anura, seven Caudata, six Odonata).
- Pooled Odonata suitability vs frequent frog strong calling Spearman **−0.095 uniform / −0.086 target-group**.
- High-minus-low pond-caudate suitability Spearman contrast **−0.385 [−0.509,−0.240]** uniform and **−0.179 [−0.328,−0.029]** target group. Separate lower/higher stratum ranks (+0.101/−0.284 and −0.004/−0.183) apply to their original field correlation estimator.
- **Do NOT** insert these stratum-specific correlations into the site-mapped ABC POM's *different global-rank* estimator, where observed low/high uniform are **+0.0321/−0.2364**.
- Interpret as a geographically contextual association with potential odonate suitability, NOT observed odonate nymph predation. Within-route, shared-environment and survey detection/effort remain priority field discrimination gates.

### Figure 2 — Vegetation, history and water context (real data)

- Rank model with original 3km EarthEnv: O×Caudata −0.166 [−0.229,−0.108] uniform and −0.086 [−0.151,−0.023] target group; O×V −0.087 [−0.124,−0.048] / −0.064 [−0.115,−0.014].
- S×V +0.058 [+0.015,+0.101] / +0.046 [−0.022,+0.109]; O×S×V **+0.037 [+0.008,+0.068] / +0.027 [−0.024,+0.073]**, the latter not supported at 95% in the second background and attenuated in within-route specifications.
- Prior strong calling +27.68 percentage points [25.07,30.17] in 4,040 fixed comparisons and prior-year water conditional detection −1.481 pp [−2.601,−0.294] in **3,907 different eligible comparisons**.
- Potential niche suitability ≠ actual larval predator density; EarthEnv 3km landscape vegetation ≠ measured pond refuge; satellite water frequency ≠ pond hydroperiod.

### Figure 3 — Direct/indirect biotic interactions and empirical adequacy

**Current governing protocol**: [Biotic-interaction ABC–POM](BIOTIC_INTERACTION_ABC_POM_PRIMARY_PROTOCOL_2026-10-31.md). The prior [joint dispersal-demography ABC plan](JOINT_MACRO_DEMOGRAPHY_ABC_POM_PROTOCOL_2026-10-31.md) is marked SUPERSEDED. Only the FE reverse edge with actual stage overlap is active.
- **Observed target vector**: *six continuous effects* from the same original 4,248 actual acoustic sites—OS,OV,SV,OSV coefficients and O–F correlations in low/high S *global-rank* site masks. Covariance from 56 geographical blocks/999 source resamples. Do not conflate with the separately ranked ±0.101/−0.284 Spearman stratum summary.
- **Source adequacy negative result**: 132 original fixed-site scenarios (104 equivalence response groups), zero meeting all six descriptive marginal 95% interval targets under both original SDM backgrounds. Closest uniform case_005 distance10.8269 and 0/6 observed-statistic intervals, target-group case_036 distance8.2624 and 2/6. Uniform low-S global-rank observed +0.032, model −0.717 for closest candidate. This is NOT a formal "no mechanism in nature" conclusion.
- **Original candidate mechanisms are not field fits**: Main 3,600 structures ->20×24 showed 1/24 pairwise/hazard compatibility. Observed OSV uniform +0.037 versus model −0.0482/−0.0104, a critical sign mismatch. PR146 genuine 12×256×2 original 80y source 6,144 simulations generated four *model-native* signs in 254 simulations, with 108 canonical class structural-prior dependence. **Those 254 are NOT an ABC posterior conditioned on the field site six-pattern fit.**
- **Source rate and edge control**: SAME trophic structure, rate theta, vegetation/stage and site observation map for every H0→H4 edge ablation. No general theory posterior until true six-pattern adequacy. With immigration OFF, rho times conversion is an unidentifiable product. A plant-refuge role cannot be counted as biologically different if its protected edge is OFF.
- **Demography/dispersion**: PR147–155 finite-N results show specific q3 S→F-only growth-dependent dispersion switch but failure of that strict switch at q9/12. **Actual field model residuals and edge-by-edge predictions should become Fig3 only after common-source same-rate mapping is run.** A conceptual edge diagram alone is not proof of biotic interaction. No final figures produced yet.

## III. One manuscript, not a results catalogue

**Authoritative flow:** macro conditioned contrast → vegetation/environment/history patterns → stage mechanism screen including mismatch → scale- and recruitment-limited alternative demographic outcomes.

**What gets demoted:**
- RC6 full old 4,236 wet/dry comparisons and within-taxon concentration: Introduction's idea history and provenance only.
- Early PR146 rate screens, late q grids and every per-shard technical test: SI/provenance, not five or ten extra Results.
- Large numbers of synthetic return events (hundreds of thousands) as if independent tests: SI receipts only, never ecological sample size.
- After-results selected mechanism labels or q thresholds as field validated general ecological processes.
