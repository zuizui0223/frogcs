### Data and code availability — manuscript wording

NAAMP observations originate from the U.S. Geological Survey release *North American Amphibian Monitoring Program (NAAMP) anuran detection data from the eastern and central United States (1994–2015)* (Foreman et al., 2017; [10.5066/F7G44NG0](https://doi.org/10.5066/F7G44NG0)), which USGS identifies as CC0 1.0 Universal. The retained analytical cohort contains 4,248 listening sites and 63,180 completed acoustic visits during 2001–2015. This interval was calculated from the frozen visit records and differs from the full release's temporal coverage.

Regional occurrence records were retrieved through the GBIF occurrence-search API on 26 October 2026. No study-specific GBIF occurrence-download DOI was generated. The frozen [attribution table](../research/results/regional_species_pool_20261026/gbif_dataset_attribution.csv) and [manifest](../research/results/regional_species_pool_20261026/gbif_dataset_attribution_manifest.json) retain the citations, dataset DOIs, licenses and occurrence-file hashes for the 60 contributing datasets. Interaction evidence came from the bounded GloBI API snapshot recorded on 26 October 2026, retaining original references where supplied (Poelen et al., 2014).

Environmental inputs were WorldClim 2.1 bioclimatic layers at 10-arc-minute resolution (Fick & Hijmans, 2017) and EarthEnv Consensus Land Cover version 1, full version with DISCover, at 30-arc-second resolution (Tuanmu & Jetz, 2014). Source URLs and checksums are retained in the [environmental receipts](../research/results/interaction_species_sdm_20261026/environment_receipts.json). Original raster archives are obtained from their providers. WorldClim's published terms permit academic and other noncommercial use but require prior permission for redistribution or commercial use; EarthEnv supplies these layers under CC BY-NC 4.0.

Scripts, workflows, frozen processed records, provenance receipts and the manuscript claim-replay inputs are accessible in the [frogcs repository](https://github.com/zuizui0223/frogcs), in the Git revision containing this record. The [claim-input bundle](../research/results/submission_frozen_sources_20261106/README.md) preserves 28 original numerical files and one required P1 source receipt, so claim replay no longer relies on retention of the original Actions artifacts. No repository-wide code license or project archival DOI has yet been established. Source-specific attribution and reuse conditions continue to apply; Git versioning does not constitute a permanent archival deposit.

## 3. What the primary studies support

The [reference register](../research/results/submission_references_20261106/verified_references.json) stores authors, issue year, title, journal, volume, pages, DOI or primary URL, inspected source URLs, intended citation placement and a claim limit for each reference. A DOI is not invented for the JMLR scikit-learn paper, whose primary record supplies a canonical URL.

| Manuscript claim | Primary references | Boundary retained in the manuscript |
|---|---|---|
| Spatial associations do not identify ecological interactions; patterns can constrain candidate models | Blanchet et al. (2020); Grimm & Railsback (2012) | The observed quantities are adult calling and relative suitability. Four idealized signs are not complete field calibration. |
| Structural habitat can change predation through different pathways | Hossie & Murray (2010); Mocq et al. (2021) | Hossie manipulates leaf litter with tadpole prey. Mocq shows vegetation-assisted dragonfly attack on **invertebrate** prey. Neither calibrates the model's frog attack-support coefficient or the two flags' joint operation. |
| A third predator can modify indirect trophic effects | Kishida et al. (2009); Davenport & Chalcraft (2013) | Kishida's pathway involves larval salamander traits; Davenport's involves tadpole refuge use and **adult** newts. Vegetation amount was not the tested causal contrast in either example. |
| Developmental context can change interaction outcomes | Alford & Wilbur (1985); Rasmussen et al. (2014) | The former manipulates amphibian introduction timing; the latter manipulates initial dragonfly size/instar. Neither is P1's independent salamander-arrival × aquatic-duration intervention. |
| Habitat effects depend on predator identity and intraguild context | Anderson & Semlitsch (2016) | No universal vegetation benefit or direct experimental validation of the present frog model is inferred. |
| Occurrence/background selection and structured validation need explicit methods | Phillips et al. (2009); Roberts et al. (2017); Pedregosa et al. (2011) | Independently fitted penalized logistic SDMs; distinct species-SDM and acoustic folds; no MaxEnt, joint SDM or guaranteed spatial independence is claimed. |

## 4. Reproduction and version boundaries

The [replay instructions](../research/results/submission_frozen_sources_20261106/README.md) run the unchanged claim auditor against the preserved source bundle. The [workflow](../.github/workflows/research_submission_claim_source_QA_20261105.yml) first checks all 29 input hashes, then recomputes **44 manuscript claims** and the existing main/SI structure gates, and finally replays the **eight G6 tables** against their frozen references. The [current QA receipt](../research/results/submission_references_20261106/reference_and_provenance_QA_receipt.json) records final word counts, reference coverage, source-file verification, byte-preserved result sections and unchanged figures. Original historical integration and numerical receipts remain unchanged.

The result sections, controlled P1 methods, SI S8/S9 and original figure bytes are preserved. P1's positive early-arrival-plus-long-duration frog response, positive sign-compatibility gains and their contrasting magnitudes remain explicit. G4/G5's unsuccessful mechanism-discrimination gates and G6's retrospective status also remain explicit. Citation changes do not convert model counterexamples into identification of a wild trophic mechanism.
